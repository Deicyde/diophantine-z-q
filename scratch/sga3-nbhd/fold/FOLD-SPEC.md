Removes the research field `sga3_VIB_3_10_Nbhd : SGA3IdComp.SGA3_VIB_3_10_Nbhd` (OpenResearchLeaves28, TailZ45 l.52).
Strict count -1, no new leaf.

Why it can't be autofolded: autofold discharges a field with a proof *of the field's type*. Nothing here proves
`SGA3_VIB_3_10_Nbhd`. Instead, every chain reader of `CToPrinted.SGA3_VIB_3_10` (into which the field was converted
at TailZ44) is replaced by a variant that does not need it. Every bundle generation from OpenResearchLeaves14
to OpenResearchLeaves28 carries the field (as `sga3_VIB_3_10` up to generation 26), so the generations are
re-declared without it. This is the "weaker-leaf bypass".
