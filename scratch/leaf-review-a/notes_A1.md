## 1. `r1r2 : LeafAdopt.R1R2fpRep` (l.19168)

**Statement.** `∃ Y Φ X Ψ fY π₆` with: `R1fpProps Y Φ` (l.15235: `Y/ℚ` proper, smooth of rel. dim.
1, has a `ℚ̄`-point; `Φ` natural in field extensions with `IsBaseChangeOf`; `R1famProps`: `Φ` is
induced by a family `𝒜/Y`; `R1univProps`: every `QMFamily` over a proper smooth curve `S/ℚ` with
object fibres is classified by some `S → Y`), `R1heckeProps` (every twist `α ∈ Aut 𝒪` and every
`rightMul u`, `u ∈ 𝒪^×`, is induced by an automorphism of `Y`), `X6Props` (`X/ℚ` proper smooth
curve, `Ψ : X(ℚ̄) ≃ {QM pairs/ℚ̄}/≅` Galois-equivariant, twists induced by automorphisms of `X`,
`fY` = forget on `ℚ̄`-points), `R2X6PropsIff` (`π₆(x) = π₆(x')` ⇔ the pairs are `Aut 𝒪`-twist
isomorphic; `HasCM4 ⇔ π₆ = 0`, `HasCM3 ⇔ π₆ = ∞`, `HasCM24 ⇔ π₆ = −16/27`), `R1recProps`
(residue fields of `Y`-points with one of the three CMs unramified above 5), `X6Surj` (`π₆` onto
`P¹(ℚ̄)`, `fY` onto `X(ℚ̄)`), `R5ptProps` (González–Rotger degree and `k ↪ ℚ(x)` for residue
fields of CM points of `X`), `R1pi0Props X` (`X` geometrically connected).

**Definitions checked.** All of the above (l.15155-15365, 19083-19168), plus the common objects.

**Source.** Shimura, Ann. of Math. 85 (1967), Main Thms I-II; Deligne, Sém. Bourbaki 389 (1971)
§§2, 4 (canonical models, PEL moduli, `π₀ = ℚ^×_{>0}\𝔸_f^×/nrd K`); Buzzard, Duke Math. J. 87
(1997) (polarization-free moduli problem; representability); Baba–Granath, Canad. J. Math. 60
(2008) Thm 2.1, Lemma 3.1, §3.1 table; González–Rotger, J. Math. Soc. Japan 58 (2006) Prop. 5.6,
Thm 5.12; Shimura reciprocity (Shimura 1967 Main Thm II; Deligne 1971 §3). All locators
UNVERIFIED.

**Verdict: CONSEQUENCE** (of the published statements above plus Lemma QP).

**Derivation / checks (STANDARD unless marked).**
- *R1fpProps.* Take `Y = Y₃`, the fine moduli scheme of `(A, ι, λ, η)` at the neat level
  `K₃ = (1 + 3𝒪̂)^×` (neat: no root of unity of `𝒪` is `≡ 1 mod 3𝒪`). Restricting the
  representing isomorphism to `Spec K` gives `Φ` and its naturality; `λ` is dropped by Lemma QP;
  the full-level structure is a `K`-rational `𝒪`-linear `(𝒪/3)_K ≅ A[3]`, i.e. this `η`.
  `R1famProps`: the universal family. `R1univProps`: a `QMFamily` over a smooth (hence reduced)
  curve `S` whose field fibres are objects is an object over `S` (proper smooth with a section
  and abelian-variety fibres ⇒ abelian scheme, Mumford GIT §6.1, UNVERIFIED number; `ι(a)` are
  homomorphisms and multiplicative fibrewise, hence globally since `S` is reduced; `η` gives a
  fibrewise-iso map of finite étale group schemes `(𝒪/3)_S → 𝒜[3]`), then Yoneda. Note
  `QMFamily` does not require `a ↦ ι(a)` to be a ring hom, but this is forced by the fibre
  hypothesis on a reduced base, so the clause is not stronger than representability. `Y₃` is not
  required to be geometrically connected (correct: `π₀(Y₃,ℚ̄) = ℤ̂^×/nrd(K₃) = ℤ/2`).
- *R1heckeProps.* On the polarization-free functor, `(A, ι, η) ↦ (A, ι∘α, η∘α)` and
  `η ↦ η(·u)` are natural automorphisms (all `α ∈ Aut 𝒪`, including `Ad n` with `nrd n < 0`, and
  all `u ∈ 𝒪^×`, including `1 + i` of norm `−1`); Yoneda. (With `λ` in the functor the matching
  `λ'` exists by Lemma QP; docstring's repair 3.)
- *X6Props.* `X = X₀⁶(1)` (Shimura's canonical model, coarse moduli of `(A, ι, λ)`): coarse moduli
  in char 0 give `X(ℚ̄) = {(A, ι)}/≅` Galois-equivariantly (λ by Lemma QP); twists descend to the
  coarse space; `Y₃ → X₆` by the coarse universal property.
- *R2X6PropsIff.* `X₆/W ≅ P¹_ℚ` (Baba–Granath Thm 2.1). Fibres: `W = N_B(𝒪)/ℚ^×𝒪^×`
  (`= N_B(𝒪)^+/ℚ^×𝒪¹` since `𝒪^×` has the norm `−1` unit `1 + i`), and twisting by `Ad u`,
  `u ∈ 𝒪^×`, is trivial on classes (`ι(u)` is an iso); Skolem–Noether gives `Aut 𝒪 = N_B(𝒪)/ℚ^×`.
  CM clauses: each of `CM(−4)`, `CM(−3)`, `CM(−24)` has `2^b h = 2` points on `X₆` and is one
  `W`-orbit (for `ℤ[i]`, `ℤ[ζ₃]`: `w_p` with `p | 6` inert swaps the two local orientations; for
  `ℤ[√−6]`: `w₂` acts as the non-principal class of `(2, √−6)`) — Baba–Granath §3.1. The three
  images are Galois-fixed, hence rational and distinct, and `PGL₂(ℚ)` moves them to
  `0, ∞, −16/27`, so the normalization adds nothing.
- *X6Surj.* Quotient map onto `P¹(ℚ̄)`; every `(A, ι)` over `ℚ̄` has a level-3 structure since
  `T₃A` is torsion-free of `ℤ₃`-rank 4 over the local maximal order `𝒪 ⊗ ℤ₃` (a non-commutative
  DVR), hence free of rank 1.
- *R1recProps.* As for `r1rec` below (residue field = field of moduli on the fine space).
- *R5ptProps.* As for `r5` below, with the residue field of the point of `X₆` (which is what
  González–Rotger print).
- *R1pi0Props.* `π₀(X₆,ℚ̄) = ℤ̂^×/nrd(𝒪̂^×) = 1` (Deligne §2).

All witnesses are the published objects; the bundle is jointly satisfied by them. The audit's
caveat ("qualified consequence: the specialization of Deligne's PEL model to this functor needs a

## 2. `r1b : ∀ h : R1R2fpRep, R1bFamily (ownY …) (ownΦ …) (ownπ …)` (bundle l.83176; def l.62736)

**Statement.** For the triple `(Y, Φ, π)` that `Classical.choice` extracts from the bundle
(`ownY/ownΦ/ownπ`, l.62498-62505, `π = π₆ ∘ fY`), there is `τ ↦ y_τ ∈ Y(ℂ)` such that for every
`τ ∈ ℍ` the object at `y_τ` has an analytic uniformization (`IsUniformization`, l.21078:
surjective homomorphism `ℂ² → A(ℂ)`, kernel exactly the lattice, open chart preimages,
holomorphic regular functions) with kernel `Λ_τ = ι(𝒪)(τ,1)ᵀ` (`qmLattice`, l.36604) that is
`ι`-compatible (`IotaCompatible`, l.62565: `Φu(ι(a)z) = ι_Q(a)(Φu z)`, `ι(a)` real matrices), and
`τ ↦ π(y_τ) ∈ P¹(ℂ)` is continuous and holomorphic (`IsHolP1At`, l.62655).

**Source.** Shimura 1967 Main Thm I (holomorphic `φ : ℍ → V(ℂ)` to the canonical model with
`φ(τ)` the moduli point of `ℂ²/ι(𝒪)(τ,1)ᵀ`); Deligne 1971 §4.15 (UNVERIFIED locators).

**Verdict: CONSEQUENCE.**

**Derivation.** (i) For the genuine `Y₃`: `y_τ = φ(τ)` on the component given by the level
structure `η_τ(a) = ι(a)v_τ/3`, every `τ` gives an abelian surface (`ℂ²/Λ_τ` is polarized by
`±Trd(μ⁻¹xȳ)`), GAGA algebraizes it with its `ι`; `π∘φ` is holomorphic since `π` is a morphism.
(ii) Transport to the chosen triple (needed because `r1b` must hold for whatever witness
`choice` picks): both triples satisfy `R1fpProps` (`own_spec`, l.62507). `R1univProps` of the
genuine `Y₃` applied to the own universal family (from own `R1famProps`; `Y_own` is a proper smooth
curve) gives `g : Y_own → Y₃` with `Φ_own = Φ ∘ g` on field points; symmetrically `g'`. Then
`g∘g'`, `g'∘g` are the identity on all field points, hence equal to the identity (reduced source,
separated target: the equalizer is a closed subscheme containing every point with its residue
field). So `g` is an isomorphism, `y'_τ = g⁻¹(y_τ)` works, and `π_own ∘ g⁻¹` is an algebraic
morphism, so `π_own(y'_τ)` is holomorphic. The docstring's appeal to qm-pin's comparison is this
argument (VERIFIED as an argument here; SPECULATIVE only in that I did not read qm-pin §3.3).

No hypothesis missing: `IsUniformization` asks for a genuine analytic uniformization, and the
kernel is required to be exactly `Λ_τ` for the same `τ` that parametrizes `y_τ`.

## 3. `r1rec : LeafQM.AV.R1rec` (l.15571)

**Statement.** For every `P : QML3 ℚ̄` with `HasCM4 ∨ HasCM3 ∨ HasCM24`, `fieldOfModuli3 P` is a
number field unramified at every prime `p ≥ 5`.

**Source.** Shimura reciprocity for CM points on the canonical model: Shimura 1967 Main Thm II;
Deligne 1971 §§3.7-3.13, Thm 4.19 (UNVERIFIED locators); with R1 (fine moduli: field of moduli =
residue field of the point of `Y₃`).

**Verdict: CONSEQUENCE.**

**Derivation.** For a CM point `y = [h, g]` with `End_𝒪 = 𝒪_E` (`E = ℚ(i)`, `ℚ(√−3)`,
`ℚ(√−6)`; maximal orders, optimally embedded), reciprocity: `s ∈ 𝔸_E^×` fixes `y` iff
`s_f ∈ E^× (Ê^× ∩ gK₃g⁻¹)`, and `Ê^× ∩ g(1+3𝒪̂)g⁻¹ = 1 + 3(Ê ∩ g𝒪̂g⁻¹) = 1 + 3Ô_E` (optimality).
So `E(y)` lies in the ray class field of `E` of conductor 3, unramified outside 3 over `E`;
`E/ℚ` is ramified only at 2 and/or 3. Hence `ℚ(y) ⊆ E(y)` is a number field unramified at all
`p ≥ 5`. `fieldOfModuli3 P` = residue field (fine moduli in char 0; generated subgroup has the same
fixed field as the stabilizer). Lemma QP identifies `QML3` with the PEL objects.

## 4. `r5 : LeafQM.AV.R5fp` (l.15712)

**Statement.** For an imaginary quadratic `k`, an order `R ⊆ 𝒪_k` (subring of finite index;
`conductor` = additive index, `0` if infinite, so `Coprime (conductor R) 6` excludes infinite
index) with `(f(R), 6) = 1`, and a QM pair `P/ℚ̄` with `End_𝒪(A) ≅ R` (ring iso):
`[ℚ(x):ℚ] = (1 if b = 0 else 2)·#Pic(R)` and, if `b ≥ 1`, `k ↪ ℚ(x)`; here
`ℚ(x) = fieldOfModuliPair P`, `b = inertCount6 k = #{p ∈ {2,3} : p𝒪_k prime}`,
`#Pic R = Nat.card (CommRing.Pic R)` (= `h(R)`, the class number of the order).

**Source.** González–Rotger, J. Math. Soc. Japan 58 (2006), Thm 5.12 (with Thm 5.8): for
`P ∈ CM(R) ⊂ X₀(D, N)`, `(f, DN) = 1`: `k(P) = H_R`; `ℚ(P) = H_R` if `D(R)N*(R) ≠ 1`, and
`[H_R : ℚ(P)] = 2` if `D(R)N*(R) = 1` (UNVERIFIED numbers; `D(R)` = product of the `p | D` with
`(R/p) = −1`; `N*(R) = 1` at `N = 1`).

**Verdict: CONSEQUENCE** (of Thm 5.12 at `D = 6, N = 1`, with `[H_R : ℚ] = 2h(R)` by class field
theory, `Pic(R) ≅` the class group of proper ideals of `R`, `ℚ(x)` = field of moduli = residue field
for the coarse space in char 0, and Lemma QP).

**Checks.** `D(R) = ∏_{p|6 inert in k} p` because `(f, 6) = 1` makes `(R/p) = (k/p)`; so
`D(R) ≠ 1 ⇔ b ≥ 1` — matches the case split. `b = 2` occurs (`k = ℚ(√−19)`), degree still `2h`.
If 2 or 3 splits in `k` there are no CM points by `R` and the statement is vacuous (correct).
`com P.q ≅ R` as abstract rings is unambiguous (`R̄ = R`, and the ring determines `k`). Mechanism
behind the printed theorem (STANDARD, Jordan 1981): complex conjugation sends `P` to `w_D(σP)`,
`σ ∈ Gal(H_R/k)`, and `w_p` for `p | 6` inert in `k` leaves the `Pic(R)`-orbit, so
`k ⊆ ℚ(P) ⇔ b ≥ 1`.

## 5. `r5count : LeafQM.AV.R5fpCount` (l.15745)

**Statement.** Same `k, R` hypotheses; if some QM pair over `ℚ̄` has `End_𝒪 ≅ R`, then the number
of iso classes of QM pairs over `ℚ̄` with `End_𝒪 ≅ R` is `2^b · #Pic(R)`.

**Source.** González–Rotger 2006 Prop. 5.6 (`#CM(R) = 2^{#{p | D(R)N(R)}} h(R)`); Ogg 1983;
Jordan 1981 §3; Eichler's count of optimal embeddings `h(R)∏_{p|D}(1 − (R/p))` (UNVERIFIED
numbers).

**Verdict: CONSEQUENCE** (published count on `X₀(6,1)`, with `X₆(ℚ̄)` = iso classes of QM pairs
by Lemma QP and coarse moduli). Constant checked: ramified `p | 6` contribute `1 − 0 = 1`, inert
ones `1 − (−1) = 2`, so `2^b h(R)`; examples `ℤ[i], ℤ[ζ₃]` (`b = 1, h = 1`, 2 points),
`ℤ[√−6]` (`b = 0, h = 2`), `ℚ(√−19)` (`b = 2, h = 1`, 4 points) agree with the docstring.
`Nat.card` of an infinite set is 0, but the set is finite, so no junk value enters.

## 6. `cmOptimalEmbedding : FS5NoCM.CMOptimalEmbedding` (l.57321)

**Statement.** For a QM pair `P/ℚ̄`, an imaginary quadratic `k`, `R ⊆ 𝒪_k` of finite index with
`End_𝒪(A) ≅ R`: there is a `ℚ`-algebra embedding `φ : k → B` with `R = φ⁻¹(𝒪)`.

**Definitions.** `AV.com`, `AV.conductor` (VERIFIED). `B`, `O` in this block (l.57134-57344 has no
`open` line): the file compiles (leaf-fold3 §5), and every `B` declared in the file is
`QuaternionAlgebra ℚ 2 0 (-3)` and every `O` the order `ℤ⟨1, i, (1+j)/2, (i+k)/2⟩` (copies at
l.151/14237/21208/36490 and 9344/14264/21238; VERIFIED by reading), so whichever copy is resolved
the statement means the same thing. (Exact resolution not checked without Lean; a `#check
@HDec.FS5NoCM.CMOptimalEmbedding` in a check file would settle it.)

**Source.** Shimura 1967 (`End_𝒪(ℂ²/ι(𝒪)v_τ) = φ_τ(k_τ) ∩ 𝒪`); Jordan, thesis (Harvard 1981) §3;
González–Rotger 2006 §5; Eichler class number one for indefinite `B` (UNVERIFIED locators).

**Verdict: CONSEQUENCE.**

**Derivation.** Over `ℂ` (End is invariant under `ℚ̄ ⊂ ℂ`): `H₁(A,ℤ)` is a torsion-free left
`𝒪`-module of `ℤ`-rank 4, hence locally free of rank 1 (𝒪 hereditary), hence free (Eichler; `B`
indefinite); so `A = ℂ²/ι(𝒪)v_τ`, `τ ∈ ℍ^±`. `𝒪`-linear endomorphisms of the lattice are right
multiplications by `b ∈ 𝒪`; `ℂ`-linearity forces `b` into the centralizer of the complex
structure, i.e. `b ∈ φ_τ(k_τ)` for the CM field `k_τ`. So `End_𝒪(A) ≅ (φ_τ(k_τ) ∩ 𝒪)^op`
(commutative). An iso `R ≅ φ_τ(k_τ) ∩ 𝒪` extends to fraction fields `k ≅ k_τ` (ℚ-linear in
char 0); composing gives `φ` with `φ⁻¹(𝒪) = R`. (The proved companion
`not_dvd_conductor_of_optimal`, l.57210, then gives `(f(R), 6) = 1`, consistent with
optimal embeddings into the local maximal orders at the ramified primes 2, 3.)
