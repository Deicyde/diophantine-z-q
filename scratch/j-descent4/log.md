# Genus comparison: sizing and results

- **VERIFIED (source), manuscript:** L583–587 uses a dimension-one abelian variety over the field of definition and its algebraic j-invariant. The manuscript does not need to state the genus of the generic stalk separately. The genus step is on-the-spot glue (c) for producing short coordinates from that stalk. The supplied reading of Fowler §2·1, p. 613 is unchanged.
- **VERIFIED (source), places:** `JDescent3FunctionField` obtains the actual generic stalk, the Tau Ceti function-field and exact-constants facts, and the rational identity place. AnthropicFLT's `AlgebraicCurve.Place` is a proper valuation subring containing the constants. Its `AlgebraicCurve.exists_closedPoint_range_stalk_eq` already implements the proper valuative criterion for an integral smooth proper curve. The converse comes from `AlgebraicCurve.exists_place_range_stalk_eq`. The Tau Ceti ring must be transported to the vendor place type and back; the shared data are valuation subrings of the same field. `AlgebraicCurve.IsCurveOver` was used for the separating generator, whereas the Hurwitz API takes `TauCeti.IsFunctionField`.
- **VERIFIED (source), invariant differential route:** `CloudB3W16Cartier/GroupDifferentials.lean` proves freeness of the sheaf of relative differentials using the identity and the group law; its vendor and `CloudR2Poincare/L3` have relative differential stalk comparisons. Smooth charts and rank-one Kähler facts are available (e.g. Tau Ceti's `Differential/Kaehler.lean`). Tau Ceti defines Weil differentials as linear functionals on repartitions; `Differential/LocalOrder.lean` describes their coefficient orders and `degree_weilDifferentialDivisor` gives degree `2g-2`. No map with normalization on the local Kähler generators was found. The AnthropicFLT Čech and Riemann–Roch results use their own function-field invariants; the residue theorem exposed there is over an algebraically closed base. The Weierstrass invariant differential cannot start from the given group scheme without first constructing its cubic.
- **VERIFIED (source), étale route:** `PkgI3.AbVar` has a group object, so multiplication by 2 is the pointwise square of the identity. `CloudB3W16Torsion/NativeBridges.lean` already has the finite and étale theorems for it. AnthropicFLT's relative group law degree theorem gives `2^(2g)` over an algebraically closed field; `RelativeBridges.lean` supplies that group law for `PkgI3.AbVar`. Mathlib has formally unramified stalk maps and the equality of maximal ideals under extension along such a local map. Tau Ceti has dominant pullback on generic stalks, normalized restriction of places, the scaling identity for ramification indices, characteristic-zero tameness, and `hurwitz_genus_formula`. The missing steps are the compatibility of centered valuation rings with the pullback (to turn the maximal ideal equality into index one), and a proof of generic degree greater than one; using the vendor degree theorem would require geometric base change and comparison of fibre rank with generic field degree.
- **SPECULATIVE (size):** Choose the étale route. The local comparison should fit in a small pair of modules using the existing place-centering and unramified ideal interfaces. The remaining degree inequality should need only a nontrivial kernel point or the existing geometric degree calculation, without its numerical value. The differential route would need a new order-preserving comparison with the repartition dual.
