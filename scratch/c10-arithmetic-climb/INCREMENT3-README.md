## What is proved

For the **given** nonzero level, Qbar-valued Dirichlet character, unit-valued β/ε, original sign/local-constancy/square/cochain certificates and fixed complex place, the proof derives finiteness of all three full coefficient ranges and both full unit ranges. It constructs their literal least rational adjoin as a number field, lifts the same values to genuine field/unit/character objects, and preserves every equation, character order, full field/unit image and the caller's place. Finite image is not used to infer local constancy: the original GIVEN LC is transferred through injective inclusion. Normalization at one remains conditional on ε(1,1)=1.

The field is not asserted to be the original B2 Galois L or full C10 C/E. B2 literature leaves and arithmetic L/inflation/T, B1 a/c/af, Tate matrices and local embeddings, actual 3/5 representation/Frobenius/modular/conductor/height inputs, H and Hilbert10 remain separate. Named external complex χ/β certificates omitted by coarse `actualB2Transport` must be retained from its existing strong witness flow; this proof does not invent them.
