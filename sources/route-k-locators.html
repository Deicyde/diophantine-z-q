    Locators for the Route K chapter

    Route K is not in the manuscript. It changes the pole polynomial of Section 5 to the congruent number curves \(y^2=x(x-\ell)(x+\ell)\) and replaces the restricted converse (Theorem 5.3) and the simultaneous descent of Section 5.1 by an explicit descent lemma and one narrow hypothesis on congruent number curves. The mathematics is written in project-authored research notes in docs/research/ of this repository; they are specifications written for this project, not published sources. The deep theorems that the hypothesis CR₃ rests on are cited at the end.

    Soundness for the congruent number curves

    docs/research/rv-route-k.md §4.1: Lemma 5.1 re-derived for the pole polynomial with the curves \(x+\ell\). The step \(v_p(x+3\ell)=\min(v_p(x),v_p(\ell))\), which needs \(p\ne3\), becomes \(v_p(x+\ell)=\min(v_p(x),v_p(\ell))\) for every prime \(p\).

    Points and the pole polynomial

    docs/research/rv-route-k.md §4.2: a point of \(C_\ell\) with abscissa \(\beta\) up to squares, the reformulation of \(\Phi\) through two points with abscissas in \(\beta\mathbb Q^{*2}\), and the torsion bookkeeping. The rescalings that turn points of \(C_N\) and \(C_{N\theta}\) into pole witnesses are those of the Lean proof (module docstring of RouteK/Assembly/Points.lean).

    Inputs and the properties of theta

    docs/research/slot-k.md §1.1, recalled from docs/research/descent-alt.md §3.1 and the manuscript's Section 5.1 (lines 1027–1033): admissible inputs, \(\beta=\mathrm{sqf}(mB)\), \(\theta=\mathrm{sqf}(m(m-B))\) and the properties (θ1)–(θ4) with their proof.

    Certificates and the descent lemma

    docs/research/slot-k.md §1.2 (certificates of torsion and of Selmer type) and §1.3 (the statement of DL and the two call sites of pole completeness).

    Configurations and the Selmer system

    docs/research/slot-k.md §2.1: configurations, reciprocity, the product class, Monsky's matrix and the system \(\Sigma(\mathcal C)\) with its rows E1, E2, (R) and (D).

    Proposition 0

    docs/research/descent-alt.md §4.1: for an odd squarefree \(d>0\) the system \(\Sigma(\mathcal C_d)\) is the 2-Selmer group of \(C_d\) in coordinates; the place-2 table was checked against an independent exact descent on 600 curves. docs/research/slot-k.md §2.1 rechecks by hand the structure of the place-2 rows and the rows for \(c=5\).

    Partner and gluing lemmas

    docs/research/slot-k.md §2.2: Lemma 1 (partner) and Lemma 2 (gluing), with the inverse formulas (2.1) and (2.2).

    Base configuration

    docs/research/slot-k.md §2.3: Lemma 3, the configuration \(\{p^*,q_0\}\) with classes \(7\) and \(3\).

    Slots

    docs/research/slot-k.md §2.4: step 0 (the multiplier), the slot primes \(q_0\), \(r_p\), \(r_\ell\), their existence by the Chinese remainder theorem and Dirichlet's theorem, and the reverse symbols by reciprocity.

    Proof of the descent lemma

    docs/research/slot-k.md §2.5, steps 1–6: the first curve through Lemmas 1, 2 and 3, the second curve through Lemma 2 with the primes of \(\theta\) glued onto \(q_0\).

    The Lean plan

    docs/research/slot-k.md §4.1 (the narrowed hypothesis CR₃ and its justification) and §4.2 (the lemmas L1–L6 and the chain \(\mathrm{L1},\mathrm{L2},\mathrm{L3}\Rightarrow\) configurations; \(\mathrm{L4},\mathrm{L5}\Rightarrow\) certificates; \(\mathrm{CR}_3\Rightarrow\) points; \(\mathrm{L6}\Rightarrow\Phi\)).

    The deep input on published theorems

    docs/research/tyz-5b.md §1 (Tian–Yuan–Zhang, the form of their Theorem 3.5(2) that is used, and what Gross–Zagier–Kolyvagin add), §§2–4 (row 5b of Smith's determinants, proved by forest expansion, and its value \(1\) on a curve with 2-Selmer dimension \(3\) and a first coordinate \(\equiv3\pmod4\)), and §5 (the curves of the slot construction). docs/research/rv-route-k.md §4.3 checks the hypotheses of Kriz's theorem for the curves \(C_d\).

    The trust statement for CR3

    docs/research/cr3-trust.md, a project-authored note: the forms of the hypothesis CR₃ that the variants of the main theorem take, and the implications between them (§0); a numbered outline of the argument for the class-number form in positive-rank form, with a supplier and a status for every step (§1): the match of the statement with Tian–Yuan–Zhang's quantities (Part A), their deep results (Part B), Proposition N (Part C), Gross–Zagier with an auxiliary imaginary quadratic field from Bump–Friedberg–Hoffstein, Waldspurger or Friedberg–Hoffstein, with the theorem numbers and the level still to be checked (Part D, which also records that the manuscript's Lemma 5.5 and Kriz's 2-converse are not used), and the steps proved here (Part E); and what remains outside refereed sources (§2). docs/research/rv-cr3-trust.md is a model review of that note.

    Kriz

    D. Kriz, Supersingular main conjectures, Sylvester's conjecture and Goldfeld's conjecture, arXiv:2002.04767v5 (2022), Theorems 10.13 and 10.17(2): the 2-converse theorem in rank one for curves with CM by \(\mathbb Z[i]\). An unrefereed preprint. With the corank lemma it is an alternative route to rank one that the hypotheses do not need (docs/research/nt-tyz.md §5.1).

    Tian-Yuan-Zhang

    Y. Tian, X. Yuan and S.-W. Zhang, Genus periods, genus points and congruent number problem, Asian J. Math. 21 (2017), no. 4, 721–774 (read in arXiv:1411.4728v1), Theorem 3.5 (first assertion) and the argument of the proof of Theorem 3.5(2) in §3.3, as completed in docs/research/nt-tyz.md §4 by Proposition N (no readable version of Tian–Yuan–Zhang states this form directly, nt-tyz Verdict 1): for squarefree \(n\equiv5\pmod8\), an odd genus sum \(T_{5b}(n)\) makes \(L(C_n,s)\) vanish to order exactly one at \(s=1\).

    Gross-Zagier-Kolyvagin

    The manuscript's Lemma 5.5 (lem:forward-rank, line 1086): analytic rank one implies rank one, by Gross–Zagier and Kolyvagin. The trust statement for CR₃ does not use it: Gross–Zagier over an auxiliary imaginary quadratic field gives rank at least one, which is all that is needed (docs/research/cr3-trust.md §1, D4).

    Cassels-Tate

    The manuscript's corank lemma (lem:corank, line 1072): for an elliptic curve with full rational two-torsion, a 2-Selmer group of dimension \(3\) gives \(2^\infty\)-Selmer corank one. The proof uses only that the Cassels–Tate pairing on \(Ш/Ш_{\rm div}\) is nondegenerate and alternating, so \(\dim(Ш/Ш_{\rm div})[2]\) is even; no finiteness of \(Ш\) is assumed. For elliptic curves this is due to J. W. S. Cassels, J. reine angew. Math. 211 (1962), as docs/research/pc-audit.md RK3 cites it; the title (Arithmetic on curves of genus 1. IV. Proof of the Hauptvermutung) and the pages 95–112 were not checked against the journal. The manuscript cites B. Poonen and M. Stoll, The Cassels–Tate pairing on polarized abelian varieties, Ann. of Math. 150 (1999). docs/research/pc-audit.md RK3 and docs/research/rv-cr3-dl.md §2.2 check the argument.
