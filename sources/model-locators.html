    Locators for the elliptic model chapter

    Line ranges refer to diophantine_zq.tex (the 11/5 manuscript, November 5, 2026 revision) in this repository. TeX labels are given where the manuscript has one.

    Model setup

    sec:model, Section 3, lines 254-266: the field \(K_0=\mathbb Q(s)\), \(s^2=2\), the element \(d=75-53s\), the curve \(E:y^2=x^3-d^2x\), the point \(P_0\), the code of the identity and of an affine point, and the notation \(\iota(n)\) for the code of \([n]P_0\).

    Generator certificate statement

    prop:generator, Proposition 3.1, lines 268-270: \(P_0\) has infinite order and \(2E(K_0)=\mathbb ZP_0\). The proof occupies lines 271-348 and cites sources S05-S07.

    Class number and units

    Proof of prop:generator, lines 273-276: class number one of \(\mathbb Z[s]\) by the Minkowski bound \(\sqrt2<2\), four unit squareclasses, sign vectors of \(-1\) and \(1+s\).

    First descent image

    Proof of prop:generator, lines 277-283: the \(x\)-squareclass map on \(E\), its image in the classes generated by \(-1,1+s,d\), the torsion classes \(1,-1,d,-d\), the change of variables to \(C:y^2=x^3-\delta^2x\) with \(\epsilon=1+s\), \(\delta=3-s\), and the point \(R=(1+s,2s)\).

    Second descent image

    Proof of prop:generator, lines 285-288: the curve \(E':y^2=x^3+4d^2x\), total positivity, the valuations at \((s)\) and at \((d)\), and the residue field \(\mathbb F_7\).

    Descent index

    Proof of prop:generator, lines 289-291: the exact two-isogeny sequence and \([E(K_0):2E(K_0)]=8\).

    Torsion bound

    Proof of prop:generator, lines 293-295: good reductions at \((17,s-6)\) and \((23,s-5)\) with \(20\) and \(24\) points, injectivity of prime-to-residue-characteristic torsion, and torsion equal to the full two-torsion.

    Rank and odd index

    Proof of prop:generator, lines 295-297: rank one from the Mordell-Weil theorem and the oddness of the free index of \(R\) from real signs of squareclasses.

    Duplication bounds

    Proof of prop:generator, lines 299-313: the duplication forms \(F_2,G_2\) on \(C\), the archimedean bounds with the constant \(676\), the common ideal at \((s)\) and \((\delta)\), and \(4h_x(T)-\log392\le h_x(2T)\le4h_x(T)+\log676\).

    Height comparison

    Proof of prop:generator, lines 314-322: the canonical height normalization, the bound \(h_x(T)\le2\widehat h(T)+\log392/3\), \(x(2R)=(57-28s)/8\) with height \(\log41\), the bound for \(h_x(S)\), and the integer comparison \(41^3\cdot676\cdot392^{36}<9^{108}\).

    Finite search

    Proof of prop:generator, lines 324-344: the search box for nonrational and rational abscissas, the exact square test, the seven surviving abscissas, the counts 7,004, 14,008 and 111, and the identification of the four nontorsion survivors with \(R+T\).

    Transfer to the generator

    Proof of prop:generator, lines 346-347: \(2C(K_0)=\mathbb Z(2R)\) and the inverse change of variables sending \(2R\) to \(P_0\).

    Membership and addition

    Section 3.1, lines 350-371: the membership system for \(2E(K_0)\), nonzero preimage ordinates, the raw addition contract \(\operatorname{Add}_{\rm raw}(\iota(a),\iota(b),R)\iff R=\iota(a+b)\) and its branches, and the remarks on typing in multiplication and in the natural predicate.

    Division polynomials

    Section 2.2, lines 210-236: the normalized division polynomials \(f_n\) on \(y^2=x^3+Ax\), \(\psi_n=f_n\) or \(2yf_n\), their recurrences, the multiplication formulas \(x([n]P)=\phi_n/\psi_n^2\), \(y([n]P)=\omega_n/\psi_n^3\), the fixed-scalar ordinate equation \(2y([n]P)\psi_n(P)^4=\psi_{2n}(P)\), and the nonvanishing of \(\psi_n(P)\) on the torsion-free carrier.

    Rational constants and inverses

    Section 2.1, lines 180-188: rational constants as equations \(qc=p\), the generator equations \(8g_0=1641\), \(4g_1=-15465\), \(32g_2=87483\), and inverses as equations \(uv=1\).

    Point references and fixed point codes

    Appendix app:literal, lines 6672-6710: point references \(-1,-2,-3\), the generator equations in every model recipe, the psi_component constructor with \(A=(-11243,7950)\), and the positive fixed point codes \(x_n=x(P_0)-\psi_{n+1}\psi_{n-1}/\psi_n^2\), \(y_n=\psi_{2n}/(2\psi_n^4)\).

    Template witness counts

    Appendix app:literal, lines 6737-6764: parameter and witness counts of the templates, including member (5 parameters, 7 witnesses), raw_add (15, 6) and the five scalar.integer_constant templates (10, 1).

    Translation typing

    Section 10.4, lines 6052-6058: member forces a canonical code in \(D\), raw addition forces the canonical sum, and the fixed scalar graph gives \([m]Q\) on \(D\).

    Generator sources

    Source table, lines 6619-6622: S05 (generator certificate), S06 (rank, torsion, codes) and S07 (exact finite algorithm).

    Sutherland lecture notes

    Bibliography entry sutherland, lines 11140-11143: A. V. Sutherland, Isogeny kernels and division polynomials, MIT 18.783, Lecture 5, Sections 5.5-5.6.

    Silverman

    Bibliography entry silverman, lines 11144-11145: J. H. Silverman, The arithmetic of elliptic curves, second edition, Springer, 2009, Chapters IV and VII.
