    The certificate and its semantics

    This section fixes the constants of the multiplication certificate and translates each of its templates into a mathematical predicate. All statements here are exact equivalences for the constructed templates, proved from the semantics of the expression language; the arithmetic happens in the soundness and completeness sections.

    The certificate uses the prescribed constants \(c_*=4M_0\), \(\ell_0=12P_s^{2916}H_{\rm cat}^{732}\), \(R\), \(A_{\rm ht}\) and \(T\) of the specification.
