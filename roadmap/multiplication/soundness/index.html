    Soundness: accepted triples are true

    Soundness says that every rational triple accepted by the multiplication template consists of codes \(\iota(n_1),\iota(n_2),\iota(n_3)\) with \(n_3=n_1n_2\). It uses the generator certificate (hence Mordell-Weil) to give the inputs indices, the pole obstruction at single primes, and the six-contact height estimate H. It does not use pole completeness or the prime pattern.

    LemmaFrom the local tests to the congruence#✓fully proved

    ZInQ.Mult.tests_to_congruence

    Let \(p>324\) be a prime, \(\mathfrak p\) a prime of \(\mathbb Z[s]\) above \(p\), and \(c\in\mathbb Z\) with \(p\nmid c\).     In the certificate \(c=c_*\), and \(p\nmid c_*\) holds for every \(p\notin S_{\rm mul}\).

    Proof sketch. Let \(\mathfrak q=\sigma\mathfrak p\) (possibly \(\mathfrak q=\mathfrak p\) if \(p\) is inert), \(v=v_{\mathfrak p}\), and let \(\lambda_{\mathfrak p},\lambda_{\mathfrak q}\) be the formal logarithms over the two completions. With \(H=[h]P_0\), \(U_i=[z_i]P_0\), \(V_0=[h-c]P_0\), \(V_i=[z_i-cn_i]P_0\):

    1. The points lie in the formal groups. \(v_{\mathfrak p}(\rho(Q))\ge1\) means \(Q=O\) or \(v_{\mathfrak p}(x(Q))<0\).     2. Full-log ratios. By the full-logarithm ratio lemma, \(\lambda_{\mathfrak p}(U_i)=\alpha_i\lambda_{\mathfrak p}(H)\) and \(\lambda_{\mathfrak q}(V_i)=\beta_i\lambda_{\mathfrak q}(V_0)\) with the rational numbers \(\alpha_i=z_i/h\) and \(\beta_i=(z_i-cn_i)/(h-c)\).
    3. Truncated ratios. By the truncation error at \(p>324\), \(A_i=\alpha_i\,u_i\) in \(K_{\mathfrak p}\) and \(B_i=\beta_i\,u_i'\) in \(K_{\mathfrak q}\) with units \(u_i,u_i'\) congruent to \(1\) modulo valuation \(28\) (when \(U_i=O\) or \(V_i=O\) the ratio is \(0\) and the claims below are trivial).
    4. Integrality. Since \(p\nmid c\), one of \(h,h-c\) is a \(p\)-adic unit. If \(p\mid h\), argue symmetrically from \(\beta_i\). Only now do the relative errors give absolute congruences: in both cases \(\alpha_i,\beta_i\) are \(p\)-integral, \(A_i\equiv\alpha_i\) modulo valuation \(28\) at \(\mathfrak p\), and \(\sigma B_i\equiv\beta_i\) modulo valuation \(28\) at \(\mathfrak p\) (as \(\beta_i\) is rational).
    5. The congruence. With test \(8+i\): \(v_{\mathfrak p}(A_i-\beta_i)\ge25\).
