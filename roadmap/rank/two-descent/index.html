    The complete 2-descent on a congruent number curve

    For a field \(K\) with \(2\ne0\) and \(d\in K^\times\), the curve \(C_d:y^2=x(x-d)(x+d)\) has full rational two-torsion. Its complete 2-descent map \(\delta_d\) sends a point to the pair of Kummer classes at the roots \(0\) and \(d\): \((x,y)\mapsto(x,x-d)\) in \((K^\times/K^{\times2})^2\), with the usual values at the torsion points (Silverman, Proposition X.1.4). At the root \(0\) the formulas are those of the Kummer map \(\alpha_b\) of the two-isogeny descent. The Kummer map \(\kappa_{b,e}\) at a general root \(e\) is a homomorphism by the chord argument. Over \(\mathbb Q\) the descent map has kernel exactly \(2C_d(\mathbb Q)\), by an explicit halving formula. Read in square classes, it agrees with the Kummer map of the coverings, so over \(\mathbb Q\) the pairs \(s(P)\) of squarefree representatives \(r(c)\) of its values lie in \(\mathrm{Sel}_2(C_d)\). A count in finitely generated abelian groups then gives RK9: positive rank and a Selmer group of order \(8\) make every Selmer class come from a rational point.

    Over a general field \(K\), \(d\) is a nonzero element of \(K\) and \(C_d:y^2=x^3-d^2x\) is the curve \(E_{-d^2}\); over \(\mathbb Q\), \(d\) is a positive integer, and \(C_d\) and \(\mathrm{Sel}_2(C_d)\) are those of the Proposition 0 chapter. Throughout, \(u\equiv v\) means that \(u/v\) is a nonzero square.

    Halving and two-torsion

    If \(x\), \(x-d\) and \(x+d\) are squares, then \((x,y)\) is twice a point, by an explicit formula for the half. The points of order dividing \(2\) are \(O\) and the three points \((e,0)\) at the roots.

    Let \(K\) be a field with \(2\ne0\) and \(d\in K^\times\), and let \((x,y)\in C_d(K)\). If \(x=r_1^2\), \(x-d=r_2^2\) and \(x+d=r_3^2\) for some \(r_1,r_2,r_3\in K\), then \((x,y)=2Q\) for some \(Q\in C_d(K)\).
