    Route AP: progressions of good primes in place of the prime pattern

    The completeness of the multiplication template needs every integer to be a sum of three good slopes, the slope representation. The manuscript obtains it from the twenty-two-prime pattern of Proposition 6.2, whose proof uses the Green–Tao–Ziegler theory of linear equations in primes. Route AP replaces the twenty-two-prime pattern by the statement 22P_AP, that the good slope primes contain arbitrarily long arithmetic progressions, through a change in the completeness proof only: the polynomial is unchanged, and so are the slope predicate and soundness. Since 22P_AP follows from Szemerédi's theorem in the primes and the positive density of the good slope primes, both proved here, the twenty-two-prime pattern is no longer a hypothesis of the main theorem.

    The obstacle is the common difference: a progression of many primes has a difference divisible by every small prime to a high power, so its slopes have poles at the old primes \(S_b\), which condition 2 of the slope representation forbids. Lemma P shows that such a pole is harmless: the parameter \(q\) of the slope certificate, chosen \(\ell\)-adically close to a square root of \(R_0^{-1}\), absorbs it.

    Proof sketch. (1) With \(F(u,v)\) the contact numerator, \(R_0=F(u,v)\,v^2/(u-v)^8\). The numbers \(u-cv\) and \(u-v\) are \(\ell\)-units, so \(v_\ell(R_0)=2e\) and \(v_\ell(t-1)=v_\ell(u-v)-v_\ell(v)=-e\). Since \(v\mid F(u,v)-u^6\), the unit \(X=F(u,v)\) is congruent to \(u^6\) modulo \(\ell^e\); Hensel's lemma for \(Y^2-X\) at \(Y=u^3\) applies because \(|u^6-X|_\ell\le\ell^{-e}<|2u^3|_\ell^2\) (for \(\ell=2\) this needs \(e\ge3\)). So \(X=y^2\) in \(\mathbb Z_\ell\), and \(R_0=z^2\) with \(z=vy/(u-v)^4\). (2) Put \(w=z^{-1}\), so that \(R_0w^2=1\). The set is the preimage of a closed ball, which is open in \(\mathbb Q_\ell\); and the rationals \(m/\ell^k\) are dense in \(\mathbb Q_\ell\), which gives the rational ball.
