    The exact precision computation

    The five natural inputs of the precision computation are signed encodings, decoded in the fixed order \((n,\kappa,a,l,j)\).

    TheoremSaturated arithmetic#✓fully proved

    ZInQ.Compiler.Precision.saturatedArithmetic

    For \(L\in\mathbb N\) let \(\sigma_L(x)=\min(x,L+1)\) on \(\mathbb N\). For all natural \(x,y,n\):

    1. \(\sigma_L(x)\le L\iff x\le L\), and more generally \(\sigma_L(x)\le k\iff x\le k\) for \(k\le L\); \(\sigma_L(\sigma_L(x))=\sigma_L(x)\); \(\sigma_L(0)=0\), \(\sigma_L(1)=1\);
    2. \(\sigma_L(\sigma_L(x)+\sigma_L(y))=\sigma_L(x+y)\), \(\sigma_L(\sigma_L(x)\cdot\sigma_L(y))=\sigma_L(xy)\) and \(\sigma_L(\max(\sigma_L(x),\sigma_L(y)))=\sigma_L(\max(x,y))\);
    3. \(\sigma_L(\sigma_L(x)^n)=\sigma_L(x^n)\);
    4. if \(L\ge1\), then \(\sigma_L(x^{\sigma_L(n)})=\sigma_L(x^n)\), hence \(\sigma_L(\sigma_L(x)^{\sigma_L(n)})=\sigma_L(x^n)\);
    5. \(\sigma_L((n+1)!)=\sigma_L(\sigma_L(n!)\cdot(n+1))\), so factorials are computed exactly by iterating saturated multiplication.

    Item 4 is where bases zero and one and exponent zero are separate cases: for \(x\ge2\) an exponent above \(L\) already gives \(x^n>L\).

    Proof sketch. Items 1-3 and 5 by cases on whether the arguments exceed \(L\): if \(x\ge L+1\) and \(y\ge1\) then \(xy\ge L+1\), and \(\sigma_L(x)\cdot y\ge L+1\); if \(y=0\) both sides are \(0\); item 3 by induction on \(n\) from item 2.
