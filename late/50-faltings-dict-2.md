Lemma 1: S normal and U open and dense in S; A_1, A_2 two semiabelian varieties over S; a homomorphism of algebraic groups over U extends uniquely to all of S. Noetherian is not a hypothesis, and nothing more is assumed of A_1, A_2 (no equal dimension, no properness, no polarization). Definition: a semiabelian variety of relative dimension g over S is a smooth algebraic group G -> S whose fibres are connected of dimension g and are extensions of an abelian variety by a torus. Neither commutativity nor splitting of the torus is required, so (iii) is not needed for the definition; the dimension clause is in the print (point (e)). The reading desk also notes: that the extension in the Lean leaf is a homomorphism is one line (the two composites agree on the dense generic fibre of the flat A_1 x_S A_1, and A_2 is separated), and uniqueness among homomorphisms is weaker than the print's uniqueness.
