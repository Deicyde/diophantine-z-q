### The steps

**S0 (not on the row's flag): the given `tp`, `δ` are Shimura's operators.** Theorem 1 speaks of the jacobian of
the standard model and of `ξ_n = {Γ…Γ}`. The Lean speaks of any `X` with `IsX1Gen` and any `tp`, `δ` with Diamond–
Shurman's action. To apply the theorem one needs two things:

* (B1) a `Q`-isomorphism from `X` to Shimura's `V_S` that matches `τ ↦ x_τ` with Shimura's uniformization;
* (B4) that `ξ_p` and `{Γσ_dΓ}` act on point classes by `IsHeckeT` and `IsDiamond`.

Diamond–Shurman's action then pins the operators: kernel lemmas `AfTateLam.HeckePin.isHeckeT_unique` and
`isDiamond_unique` (`ZInQKernel/Contrib/OneProof/HeckePin.lean:787`, `:795`), with inputs `X1TauPoints` and
`ConstOnTauPoints`.

* (B4) checks out against the print: the conventions agree; see the companion report.
* (B1) is not printed in this paper, beyond the group `S` on p. 532, and needs Shimura 1971.

So this row depends on B1 just as `bShStandardModel` does. The docstring says so ("transported … by
BShStandardModel"). But the Lean statement of `BShStandardModel` only asserts existence, and the flag of this row
does not list the identification.

**S1 (flag step 1: `C = I_f J`). Correct; the parts are as follows.**

* **(a) `𝔘 = ker ρ`.** This is immediate from Q3. A homomorphism onto a field kills the radical `ℜ` and all
  simple components but one, and `ρ` restricted to `𝔎_1` is an isomorphism. The print does not write "`ker ρ`" or
  "annihilating `f`".
* **(b) `𝔗` is the `Q`-span of `Z[tp p, δ d]` inside `End(J) ⊗ Q`.**
  * `ξ_n` lies in `Z[ξ_p, {Γσ_pΓ}]`, by three relations:
    * `ξ_n = ξ_m ξ_q`;
    * `ξ_m = Π_{p|m} ξ_p^{e_p}` for `m | N^∞` with `m = Π p^{e_p}`, i.e. `{Γ diag(1,m) Γ}` is that product of the
      `{Γ diag(1,p) Γ}`;
    * `ξ_{p^{k+1}} = ξ_p ξ_{p^k} − p{Γσ_pΓ} ξ_{p^{k−1}}` for `p ∤ N`.
  * In the other direction, `{Γσ_pΓ} = (ξ_p² − ξ_{p²})/p`. Moreover `{Γσ_dΓ} = {Γσ_pΓ}` for a prime
    `p ≡ d (mod N)`, by Dirichlet's theorem.
  * This paper prints none of these Hecke relations. They are in Shimura 1971 Ch. 3 and in Diamond–Shurman Prop. 5.2.4
    (numbers UNVERIFIED). By (1.3) and the injectivity of `δ` on `End(J) ⊗ Q`, it is enough that they hold for the
    operators `[·]_2` on `S₂(Γ)`.
* **(c) `ρ ∘ heckeJ = evF`.** It suffices to check generators: `ρ(T_p) = a_p`, and
  `ρ(⟨p⟩) = (a_p² − a_{p²})/p = χ(p)` (`R16Thm1.coeff_sq_relation`, `R16Thm1.lean:287`). Alternatively, (2.9) with
  the nebentypus.
* **(d) Saturation.**
  * `I_f = heckeJ(ker evF) ⊆ 𝔘 ∩ End(J)`.
  * Conversely, let `α ∈ 𝔘 ∩ End(J)`. Then `mα ∈ Z[tp, δ]` for some `m > 0`, so `mα ∈ I_f` by (c). Also
    `α(J) = (mα)(J)`, because `[m]` is surjective.
  * Hence `C = I_f J`.
* **(e) The conclusion.** `ker ν = I_f J` gives `IsAfQuotient`. `J → J/C` is universal among homomorphisms that
  kill `C`, and the factorization is unique because `ν` is surjective.

**S2 (flag step 2: (ii) for all `n`, from `p` and `p²`). Correct; not printed; routine.**

1. Work in `Hom⁰` for a competitor `(A', ν', θ')`. From (ii) at `p` and at `p²`:
   `p·ν'∘{Γσ_pΓ} = θ'(a_p² − a_{p²})∘ν' = θ'(p·χ(p))∘ν'`.
   So `ν'∘⟨p⟩ = θ'(χ(p))∘ν'`. This is the computation of `R16Thm1.isAfAction_of` (`R16Thm1.lean:300`), done here
   without `IsAfQuotient`.
2. Dirichlet's theorem extends this to every `d`.
3. The recursions of S1(b), together with the same recursions for `a_n` (from the Lean coefficient identity), give
   `ν'∘ξ_n = θ'(a_n)∘ν'` for all `n`.

**S3 (flag step 3: ν-compatible isomorphisms). Essentially printed, in the proof (Q4).**

1. The proof shows that the tangent space of `Ker(ν')` at the origin does not depend on the triple.
2. In characteristic 0, an abelian subvariety is determined by its tangent space. So `Ker(ν') = C = Ker(ν)`.
3. By p. 525's definition, both `(A, ν)` and `(A', ν')` represent `J/C`. The isomorphism `ψ` with `ψ ∘ ν = ν'` is
   unique, hence defined over `Q`.

The research note's sketch argues differently, via `ker ν' ⊇ I_f J` and (iii). That also works, because the Lean
competitor is required to satisfy (iii).

**S4 (not on the flag; routine conversions).**
