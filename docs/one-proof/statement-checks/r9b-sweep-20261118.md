## Integral versus rational: the standard bridge

The proof opening on p. 360 reduces Satz 4 to bijectivity of

    End_K(A) ⊗_Z Q_l → End_π(T_l(A) ⊗_{Z_l} Q_l).

This rational display must not be substituted for the integral printed statement immediately above it. Rational equality alone does not in general identify two integral lattices.

The additional ingredient is saturation of the natural integral Hom image, equivalently its torsion-free cokernel. It is standard and explicitly stated in:

J. S. Milne, *Abelian Varieties*, Theorem 12.5; Lemma 12.6 and the opening of its proof. The author's corrected re-typesetting of the chapter in *Arithmetic Geometry* (1986), available as `AVs.pdf`, preserves the numbering according to the author's edition page. In this copy Theorem 12.5 and Lemma 12.6 are on printed/PDF p. 21, with the theorem's proof on p. 22.

- Author's edition page: https://jmilne.org/math/xnotes/AVs.html
- Author-hosted PDF: https://jmilne.org/math/xnotes/AVs.pdf
- SHA-256: `bffdf81c57c77420b5454b31270007989d12f332abea62424be15c424079fc7c`

Theorem 12.5 states that Hom(A,B) is a finite free Z-module and that, for l different from char(k), the natural integral Tate-module map “is injective with torsion-free cokernel.” Lemma 12.6 proves divisibility: if T_l(f) is divisible by l^n as a Tate-module map, then f is divisible by l^n in Hom(A,B). The proof observes that f kills A[l^n], hence factors through multiplication by l^n. The theorem's proof explicitly uses this lemma to obtain the torsion-free cokernel. These passages were read on page images; the copy is a corrected author re-typesetting, not the original book-page scan.

For completeness, the saturation deduction is:

1. Let H = Z_l ⊗_Z Hom_K(A,B), C = Hom_{Z_l}(T_l(A),T_l(B)), and M be the image of H in C.
2. Milne's theorem gives an injective map H → C and a torsion-free quotient C/M. Its characteristic restriction holds because K is a number field.
3. Rational full faithfulness puts an equivariant φ in M ⊗_{Z_l} Q_l. Clearing the finitely many denominators gives l^n φ in M for some n.
4. The class of φ in C/M is then l-power torsion, so it vanishes. Therefore φ is in M. Naturality supplies the reverse image inclusion.

This explains the rational-to-integral reduction and identifies the standard extra step if one starts only from a rational version of the theorem. It does not change this report to CONSEQUENCE: the cited Korollar 1 itself already prints the integral assertion.
