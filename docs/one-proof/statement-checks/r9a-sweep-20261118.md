2. Abelian variety: `AbVar K` is a group object X in schemes over `Spec K`, with proper, smooth and geometrically integral structure morphism (`I3:620–638`). Commutativity is provided by a theorem, not by an extra unproved field of the structure. This is a genuine abelian variety, not an abstract representation supplied as data.
3. Geometric points: `A.Pt L` consists of K-morphisms `Spec L → A` (`I3:713–718`), carrying the group law of A.
4. Integral Tate module: for M = A(K̄), `Tate.T l M` consists of sequences x_n with x_0 = 0 and l·x_{n+1} = x_n (`I3:520–540`). These equations imply l^n·x_n = 0. Thus it is exactly the inverse limit of l-power torsion, not the direct limit of torsion points. Its Z_l action is reduction of the scalar modulo l^n in coordinate n (`I3:555–598`). `A.T l` specializes this construction to geometric points (`I3:754–757`).
5. Rational Tate module: `A.V l = Q_l ⊗_{Z_l} A.T l` (`I3:759–760`). The reversed order relative to the print is the usual symmetry of tensor factors; it does not change the represented vector space.
6. Galois action: `Field.absoluteGaloisGroup K` is `Aut_K(K̄)` (pinned Mathlib, `Mathlib/FieldTheory/AbsoluteGaloisGroup.lean:40–43`). On points, σ acts by precomposition with `Spec(σ)`, hence by applying σ to coordinates (`I3:722–747`). `Tate.map` applies a homomorphism coordinatewise (`I3:602–608`). `galT` uses that point action, and `galRep` extends it from Z_l to Q_l (`I3:764–799`). This is the covariant Tate-module action, not a cohomological dual or a twist.
7. Semisimple: at the pinned Mathlib revision, `Mathlib/RepresentationTheory/Semisimple.lean:33–35` defines `IsSemisimpleRepresentation ρ` as `ComplementedLattice (Subrepresentation ρ)`. A subrepresentation is a scalar submodule stable under every group element (`Mathlib/RepresentationTheory/Subrepresentation.lean:26–33`). Thus every G_K-invariant Q_l-linear subspace W has a G_K-invariant Q_l-linear complement W', with V_l(A) = W ⊕ W'.

The resulting proposition is: for every number field K, every abelian variety A/K and every prime l, every G_K-invariant Q_l-subspace of V_l(A) has an invariant Q_l-linear complement.
