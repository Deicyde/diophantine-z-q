## 2. Primary statement and sources

**Elaborated target** (`#print`, evidence `Check1.lean`): a closed `Prop` with no universe parameters.
* Binders: `G : Type` with `[Group G]`, `E : Type` with `[Field E] [CharZero E]`, `n : ℕ`, and
  `ρ₁ ρ₂ : G →* GL (Fin n) E`.
* (S1)/(S2): every subspace invariant under all `toLin' (ρᵢ g)` has an `IsCompl` complement that is also
  invariant.
* (T): `Matrix.trace (ρ₁ g) = Matrix.trace (ρ₂ g)` for every `g`.
* Conclusion: `∃ P : GL (Fin n) E, ∀ g, ρ₂ g = P * ρ₁ g * P⁻¹`.

The target has no finiteness of `G`, algebraic closure, finite image, or topology, and none was added.

**Source contract (classical result):** let `k` be a field of characteristic 0 and `A` a `k`-algebra. If
two semisimple `A`-modules are finite-dimensional over `k` and `tr_V(a) = tr_W(a)` for every `a ∈ A`,
then `V ≅ W`.
* Lang, *Algebra* (rev. 3rd ed.), XVII §3 Thm 3.7 / Cor 3.8: UNVERIFIED.
* Curtis–Reiner 1962, §30, as cited by the T19 docstring; the `PkgI6` docstring cites (27.13) and
  (30.16): UNVERIFIED and mutually inconsistent.
* Brauer–Nesbitt, *Ann. of Math.* 42 (1941) 556–590, the namesake: not read.
* Corroboration from a tertiary source only: the public Wikipedia "Brauer–Nesbitt theorem" page gives
  both this module form and exactly the T19 shape (an arbitrary group `G`, `ρᵢ : G → GL_n(E)`,
  semisimple, char 0 with equal traces ⇒ isomorphic). It cites Curtis–Reiner 1962 and Brauer–Nesbitt 1941.
* The T19 form is the char-0 trace specialization. It does not depend on the modular
  characteristic-polynomial theorem.

**Needed assertion and connecting derivation:**
* Take `A = E[G]` acting on `Eⁿ` through `ρᵢ`.
* (S1)/(S2) is exactly complementedness of the `Subrepresentation` lattice. That is the same as
  `IsSemisimpleModule E[G]`, by Mathlib's
  `Representation.isSemisimpleRepresentation_iff_isSemisimpleModule_asModule`, pinned at
  `Mathlib/RepresentationTheory/Semisimple.lean`.
* Trace equality on `G` extends linearly to `E[G]`.
* An `E[G]`-linear isomorphism gives the uniform conjugating matrix `P`, in the orientation stated.

**Adverse cases (all outcomes as predicted before the freeze):**
* `n = 0`: true (`GL (Fin 0) E` is trivial).
* Trivial `G`: true.
* Non-algebraically-closed `E` (e.g. ℝ with `ℤ/4` acting by rotation): true. The argument uses
  `dim_E S ≥ 1` and never needs `End_A S = E`.
* Infinite `G`: true. Only the finite-dimensional image of `E[G]` matters.
* Without `CharZero`: **false**. Checked in Lean (`Adverse.lean`, standard axioms): with the binder
  deleted, the statement fails for `E = ZMod 3`, `n = 3`, `G = (ZMod 3)ˣ`, `ρ₁ = 1`, `ρ₂ u = u·I`.
* Without semisimplicity: false, by the textbook unipotent example (hand-checked).
* So `CharZero` and both complement hypotheses carry weight, and the target includes all three.

## 3. Proof review (independent reading of the five modules)

The proof chain was checked step by step against the pinned Mathlib definitions:
1. **Separating element** (`exists_smul_eq_projection`):
   * Let `c` be the `S`-isotypic component of a semisimple `M` that is finite-dimensional over `k`, and
     `C` the sSup of the other isotypic components.
   * `IsCompl c C` comes from `sSupIndep_isotypicComponents` and `sSup_isotypicComponents`. Both are fully
     invariant, so the projection is `End_A M`-linear.
   * Mathlib's Jacobson density theorem (`Module.Finite.toModuleEnd_moduleEnd_surjective`, which needs
     `M` finite over `End_A M`) realizes the projection as some `a ∈ A`.
   * This replaces my frozen route (central idempotents of the semisimple image algebra). The two are
     equivalent, and neither needs Wedderburn–Artin or algebraic closure.
2. **Key step:**
   * On `V × W`, take `a` for the isotypic component of `S ↪ V`.
   * If `W` has no copy of `S`, then `a` kills `W`, because `snd` maps the component into
     `isotypicComponent W S = ⊥`.
   * `a` fixes `S` and is idempotent on `V`.
   * `IsIdempotentElem.trace_eq_zero_iff` (pinned `Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/Trace.lean:340`, `[CharZero R]`)
     then gives `tr_V(a) ≠ 0 = tr_W(a)`, a contradiction.
3. **Induction** on `finrank k V`:
   * Split `V = S ⊕ S'` and `W = T ⊕ T'` with `T ≅ S`, and cancel characters.
   * The base case uses `trace 1 = dim` and char 0.
4. Representation form (via `MonoidAlgebra.induction_linear`), then matrix form (`matrixRep`,
   complement ⇔ semisimple, `P = toMatrix' f`). The orientation `ρ₂ g = P ρ₁ g P⁻¹` holds with
   `f : ρ₁-space → ρ₂-space`. I checked this by reading the `Units.ext`/`toLin'` computation.
5. GLUE-03 (`brauerNesbittChar0_of_t19`) uses the T19 body only as a hypothesis. Equal dimensions come
   from the trace at `1`, using char 0.

No hypothesis beyond the target's is introduced anywhere. The author note's dependency inventory and
counterexamples match this reading and my frozen reconstruction. Its locators remain UNVERIFIED
(consistent with §2).
