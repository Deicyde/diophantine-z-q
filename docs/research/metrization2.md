## 1. The glue Prop `ProductMapLocalStructure`, and its proof

Statement (`part_nondeg2.lean`): take group models `𝒢₁`, `𝒢₂`, `ℋ` over `𝓞_L` (of any abelian
varieties `A`, `B`, `C` over any `K`), a morphism `m : 𝒢₁ ⊗ 𝒢₂ ⟶ ℋ` in `Over (Spec 𝓞_L)`, an
open `U ⊆ ℋ`, a section `s ∈ Γ(U)` and complex points `p₁`, `p₂` with
`(lift p₁ p₂ ≫ m) ∈ U(ℂ)`. Then there are:
- opens `Vᵢ ∋ pᵢ`;
- regular functions `a_k, c_k` on `V₁` and `b_k, d_k` on `V₂`;

such that `H = Σ c_k(p₁) d_k(p₂) ≠ 0`, and wherever `H(p₁', p₂') ≠ 0` on `V₁(ℂ) × V₂(ℂ)`:
`(lift p₁' p₂' ≫ m) ∈ U(ℂ)` and `s(lift p₁' p₂' ≫ m) · H(p₁', p₂') = Σ a_k(p₁') b_k(p₂')`.

This is metrization's `ProductLocalStructure` (which has `m = e.inv` for an isomorphism
`e : 𝒢P ≅ 𝒢₁ ⊗ 𝒢₂`) with an arbitrary `m`. Metrization's form cannot be applied to the group law
`μ : 𝒢 ⊗ 𝒢 ⟶ 𝒢`. That would need a `GroupModel` whose underlying scheme is `𝒢 ⊗ 𝒢`, hence an
`AbVar` `A × A`, which Mathlib v4.35 cannot form: geometric integrality of products is missing.

### 1.1 Proof (`part_pml1.lean` to `part_pml3.lean`)

- `ev q U s`: the value of `s ∈ Γ(X, U)` at a complex point `q : Spec ℂ → X` of any scheme. It is
  the same formula as `GroupModel.evalAt`, so `𝒢.evalAt p = ev p.left` by `rfl`. Its algebra:
  `ev_comp` (via `Scheme.Hom.comp_appLE`), `ev_res`, `ev_add`, `ev_mul`, `ev_pow`, `ev_sum`. Its
  relation with basic opens: `ev_ne_zero_of_mem` (via `isUnit_res_basicOpen`) and
  `mem_basicOpen_of_ev_ne_zero` (via `Scheme.preimage_basicOpen`, `basicOpen_res`,
  `basicOpen_of_isUnit`).
- `exists_sum_of_isPushout`: in a pushout square of commutative rings, every element is a finite
  sum `Σ inl(α_k) · inr(β_k)`. The proof needs no tensor products: such sums form the subring
  generated by the images, and the universal property gives a retraction onto that subring.
- `core_local`. Setting: `X₁ ×_S X₂` with `S` affine, an open `W`, `t ∈ Γ(W)`, and a complex point
  `(x₁, x₂)` in `W`.
  - Choose affine opens `Vᵢ ∋ xᵢ`.
  - `Q = V₁ ×_S V₂` is affine (Mathlib instance), with `j : Q → X₁ ×_S X₂`. Its ring is the
    pushout of `Γ(V₁)` and `Γ(V₂)` over `Γ(S)` (`isPushout_appTop_of_isPullback`).
  - Complex points of `V₁ × V₂` lift to `Q` (`IsOpenImmersion.lift`).
  - Take a basic open `D(h) ∋ x` of `Q` inside `j⁻¹W` (`IsAffineOpen.exists_basicOpen_le`).
  - `t|_{D(h)} = a₀ / h^N` (`IsLocalization.surj`, `isLocalization_away_of_isAffine`).
  - Use denominator `h^{N+1}` and numerator `a₀ h`, so that `H ≠ 0` forces the point into `D(h)`.
  - Expand both as sums of products and transport sections of `Vᵢ.toScheme` to `Γ(Xᵢ, Vᵢ)`
    (`secOf`, `ev_secOf`).
- `productMapLocalStructure`: apply `core_local` with `S = Spec 𝓞_L`, `W = m⁻¹(U)`,
  `t = m^*s` and `(lift p₁ p₂).left = pullback.lift p₁.left p₂.left` (Mathlib's
  `Over.lift_left`, `rfl`).

## 2. Proof of `jacDetNondegenerate_of` (`part_nondeg1.lean` to `part_nondeg3.lean`)

Setting: `Φ : ℂ^g → 𝒢(ℂ)_τ` is an analytic uniformization with discrete kernel `Λ`. Suppose every
`jacDet Φ f`, for `f : Fin g → 𝔪_ξ`, vanishes. Write `D t = d(germAlong Φ t)(0)` for
`t ∈ 𝒪_{𝒢,ξ}`.

- **Step 1, linear algebra** (`exists_null_of_det_eq_zero`, Mathlib only). Suppose every `g × g`
  matrix with rows taken from a family of vectors in `ℂ^g` has determinant 0. Then a nonzero `v`
  is orthogonal to every member of the family. If the span were all of `ℂ^g`, a linearly
  independent subfamily of size `g` (`exists_linearIndependent'`) would give an invertible
  matrix (`Matrix.linearIndependent_row_iff_isUnit`). Otherwise a nonzero functional kills the
  span (`Submodule.exists_le_ker_of_lt_top`). So `D t v = 0` for all `t ∈ 𝔪_ξ`.
- **Step 2, all regular functions** (`fderiv_evalAt_apply_eq_zero`). Let `b ∈ Γ(V)` with `ξ ∈ V`.
  Subtract `c = π^*(e^*b)`, the pull-back along `π : 𝒢 → Spec 𝓞_L` of `b`'s restriction to the
  unit section `e`. Along every complex point, `c` takes the constant value `baseVal τ (e⁻¹V) (e^*b)`
  (`evalAt_pullback_base`). At the identity, `b` takes that same value (`evalAt_of_left_eq`, since
  `1 = Spec ℂ → Spec 𝓞_L → 𝒢` through `e`, `one_left_eq`). So `b − c` vanishes at `1`, its germ
  lies in `𝔪_ξ` (`germ_mem_of_evalAt_one`: a unit germ has an inverse on a smaller open, and
  `evalAt` is multiplicative), and `d(b ∘ Φ)(0) v = D(germ(b − c)) v = 0`.
- **Step 3, translation** (`hasDerivAt_line_zero`). Let `s ∈ Γ(U)` and `Φ(w) ∈ U(ℂ)`. Apply
  `ProductMapLocalStructure` to `m = μ`, `p₁ = Φ w`, `p₂ = Φ 0 = 1`. Near `0`,
  `s(Φ(w + z)) = s(Φ w · Φ z) = N(z)/D(z)`, where `N` and `D` are complex combinations of regular
  functions near `ξ` along `Φ`, and `D(0) ≠ 0`. By step 2 and the quotient rule,
  `t ↦ s(Φ(w + t v))` has derivative `0` at `t = 0`.
- **Step 4.** Take an affine open `U ∋ ξ`
  (`exists_isAffineOpen_mem_and_subset`) and a ball `B` with `B·v ⊆ Φ⁻¹(U(ℂ))`. By step 3 each
  `t ↦ s(Φ(t v))` has zero derivative on `B`, so it is constant there
  (`IsOpen.is_const_of_deriv_eq_zero`). Two complex points of an affine open with the same values
  are equal (`pts_eq_of_evalAt`, via `IsAffineOpen.SpecMap_appLE_fromSpec`). So `Φ(t v) = Φ 0 = 1`
  and `t v ∈ Λ` for every `t ∈ B`.
- **Step 5.** Since `Λ` is discrete, some neighbourhood of `0` meets `Λ` only in `0`. A small
  `t ≠ 0` gives `t v ∈ Λ`, `t v ≠ 0`. Contradiction.

Finding (not a defect): the hypotheses `A.dim = g` and `IsZLattice ℝ Λ` of `JacDetNondegenerate`
are not used. Metrization's docstring said they "are there only to make it weaker".

## 3. `AnalyticUniformizationOfDim` from T35 (`part_unif0.lean`, `part_unif1.lean`)

Metrization's leaf: for every group model `𝒢` of `A_L` (with `K → L` a number field), every
`τ : L → ℂ` and `g = dim A`, there are a full lattice `Λ ⊂ ℂ^g` and an analytic uniformization
`Φ : ℂ^g → 𝒢(ℂ)_τ` with kernel `Λ`. T35 (`ZInQ.LeafPush2.UniformizationExists`) says the same for
`A(ℂ)_σ`, for every abelian variety `A` over every field `K` and every `σ : K → ℂ`.

Proof of T35 ⇒ `AnalyticUniformizationOfDim`:
1. Apply T35 to `A_L` over `L` with `σ = τ`. Its hypothesis `dim A_L = dim A` holds by g0-dim's
   `G0Dim.dim_baseChange`, which is in metrization's `part_g0dim.lean`.
2. Transport along `θ : A_L(ℂ)_τ → 𝒢(ℂ)_τ`, `q ↦ (Spec ℂ → A_L ≅ 𝒢_L → 𝒢)`. Here `θ` is a
   bijective group homomorphism. Its inverse is `χ(p) = secC ≫ F(p) ≫ gen`, with
   `F = Over.pullback (Spec L → Spec 𝓞_L)` a monoidal functor (`Functor.map_mul`,
   `MonObj.mul_comp`, `MonObj.comp_mul`). The identities `θ ∘ χ = id` and `χ ∘ θ = id` are checked
   with pullback projections.
3. Evaluating `s ∈ Γ(U, 𝒪_𝒢)` at `θ q` is evaluating its pull-back to `A_L` at `q`
   (`evalAt_theta`, by `Scheme.Hom.comp_appLE`). Also `Φ⁻¹(U(ℂ)) = Φ₀⁻¹((toModel⁻¹U)(ℂ))`
   definitionally. So openness and holomorphy transfer without any local structure of base
   change.

The converse fails in general: T35 covers every field `K`, for example `K = ℂ`, while
`AnalyticUniformizationOfDim` only covers number-field models.

**bp-analytic's `UniformizationLeaf`** (`ZInQKernel/HDecomp/Analytic/Uniformization.lean`) is not
equivalent to either, and is not a field of the leaf bundles (h-decomposition §leaf-adopt3 chose
T35). The two differences already recorded in `LeafPush2/UnifBridge.lean` remain:
- **(a) `g` is not pinned to `dim A`.** The only route seen from bp-analytic's leaf to T35 needs
  analytic dimension = Krull dimension: the differentials `𝔪/𝔪² ⊗ ℂ → (ℂ^g)^*` must be injective
  as well as surjective. That is a GAGA/completion fact, glue absent from Mathlib, and this task
  found no shortcut around it.
- **(b) bp-analytic's predicate is stronger.** It adds local holomorphic coordinates at every
  point and continuity of `φ⁻¹`. Its clause (ii) at `0` is close to `JacDetNondegenerate` (now
  proved), and translation would give it at every point (SPECULATIVE; not written). Its
  clause (iii) needs a compactness argument and the Hausdorffness of `A(ℂ)` in the topology of
  regular functions (separatedness).

The base-change half of "bp-analytic ⇒ T35" (from `A/K` at `σ` to `A_ℂ` at `id`) is the same `θ`
transport as above; it was not written. So among the three uniformization statements, T35 is the
one leaf needed on the metrization path. bp-analytic's leaf is only a stronger variant over `ℂ`.
