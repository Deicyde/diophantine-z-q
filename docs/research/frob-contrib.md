## 1. Statement check (independent reading)

### 1.1 `G15Ram.UnramRoots p ℓ` (task unram-roots)

```
def UnramRoots : Prop :=
  ∀ (F : Fld p), FiniteDimensional ℚ_[p] F → ∀ ψ : GQp p → PadicAlgCl ℓ,
    (∀ σ ∈ fixSet F, ∀ τ ∈ fixSet F, ψ (σ * τ) = ψ σ * ψ τ) →
    (∃ F'' : Fld p, FiniteDimensional ℚ_[p] F'' ∧ ∀ σ ∈ fixSet F'', ψ σ = 1) →
    (∀ σ ∈ fixSet F ∩ inertiaSet, ψ σ = 1) →
    ∃ η : GQp p → PadicAlgCl ℓ,
      (∃ F₁ : Fld p, FiniteDimensional ℚ_[p] F₁ ∧ ∀ σ ∈ fixSet F₁, η σ = 1) ∧
      (∀ σ ∈ fixSet F, ∀ τ ∈ fixSet F, η (σ * τ) = η σ * η τ) ∧
      (∀ σ ∈ fixSet F, η σ ^ 4 = ψ σ) ∧
      (∀ σ ∈ fixSet F ∩ inertiaSet, η σ = 1)
```

- **Source.** The `def` block is textually identical to `scratch/local-glue/LocalGlue.lean`
  (the consumer; `diff` of the block from `variable (p ℓ) in` to the last clause is empty), same
  namespace `G15Ram`, same `open PkgI4 (GQp)` / `open LadicCond` (VERIFIED). Its vocabulary
  (`GQp = Gal(ℚ̄_p/ℚ_p)` on `PadicAlgCl p`, `Fld p` = intermediate fields of `ℚ̄_p/ℚ_p`,
  `fixSet F = G_F`, `inertiaSet` = metric inertia `‖σx − x‖ < 1` for `‖x‖ ≤ 1`) is that of the
  build file `ZInQKernel/HDecomp/Conductor/LadicCond.lean` (not pinned upstream), imported unchanged.
  `G_F ∩ I_{ℚ_p} = I_F`, so `fixSet F ∩ inertiaSet` is the inertia group of `F` (STANDARD).
- **Hypotheses not stronger than needed (VERIFIED by reading).** `ψ` is an arbitrary function,
  required only to be multiplicative on `G_F`, trivial on *some* open `G_{F''}` (`F''` need not
  contain `F` or be Galois) and trivial on `I_F`. No continuity, unit-valuedness, `p ≠ ℓ`, or
  Galois hypothesis on `F` is assumed: continuity for the discrete topology is the open-kernel
  clause, and unit values follow (`σ^j ∈ G_{F''}` gives `ψ(σ)^j = 1`). `FiniteDimensional ℚ_[p] F`
  is needed (and is what the docstring says: "finite `F/ℚ_p`"). The docstring of `Fld` says
  "finite Galois", but `UnramRoots` does not require Galois, which makes the theorem stronger.
- **Conclusion not weaker than claimed (VERIFIED by reading).** The docstring claims a character
  `η` of `G_F`, trivial on some open `G_{F₁}` and on `G_F ∩ I`, with `η⁴ = ψ` on `G_F`; the four
  conclusion clauses say exactly this. Values of `ψ, η` outside `G_F` are unconstrained, which is
  harmless (only `G_F` matters for the consumer).
- **Not vacuous (VERIFIED, compiled).** `ZInQKernel/Contrib/UnramRoots/Sanity.lean` instantiates
  the theorem at `p = 2`, `F = ℚ_2` (`⊥`), any prime `ℓ`, on the unramified quadratic character
  `ψ(σ) = 1` if `σω = ω`, else `−1`, where `ω ∈ ℚ̄_2` is a root of `X² + X + 1`. It proves in Lean:
  `ψ` is multiplicative; trivial on `G_{ℚ_2(ω)}` (finite); trivial on metric inertia (inertia
  fixes roots of unity of odd order, unram-roots' `fix_of_mem_inertiaSet_of_pow_eq_one`); and
  **nontrivial**: `X² + X + 1` has no root in `ℚ_2` (reduction to `𝔽_2` for `‖x‖ ≤ 1`, the
  ultrametric inequality otherwise), so by `InfiniteGalois.fixedField_fixingSubgroup` some
  `σ ∈ G_{ℚ_2}` moves `ω`. Then `unramRoots` gives `η` with `η(σ)⁴ = −1`
  (`UnramRootsTask.Sanity.unramRoots_two_nonvacuous`). In particular `inertiaSet ≠ G_{ℚ_2}`, so the
  inertia hypothesis does not collapse `ψ` to `1`.

**Verdict on `UnramRoots`: the statement is the textbook statement (Serre, *Local Fields*: `G_F/I_F`
procyclic generated by Frobenius; locator UNVERIFIED), with no hypothesis stronger than needed and
no conclusion weaker than claimed; it is not vacuous.** The proof is genuine (Mathlib + vendored
Tau Ceti, standard axioms; VERIFIED §3).

### 1.2 The absolute Frobenius `AbsFrobDef.fr` and `AlgFrobRedRed.AbsFrob` (task afr-absfrob)

- **`fr p X hX : X ⟶ X`** for `[Fact p.Prime]` and `hX : (p : Γ(X, ⊤)) = 0`. The hypothesis is
  exactly "`X` is an `𝔽_p`-scheme" (equivalent to a map `X → Spec 𝔽_p`); no `CharP` instance on
  sections is assumed (sections over the empty open are the zero ring), which is the right
  generality. **The conclusion is the absolute Frobenius by definition**: `fr_base : fr.base = 𝟙`
  and `fr_app : fr.app U = (s ↦ s ^ p)` hold by `rfl` (VERIFIED), and `fr` is built with
  `Scheme.Hom.mk`, i.e. it is a genuine morphism of locally ringed spaces (locality of stalk maps
  proved via `frob_stalkMap` and `isUnit_pow_iff`).
- **`fr_naturality (f : Y ⟶ X) hX hY : f ≫ fr X = fr Y ≫ f`.** The hypothesis `hY` is
  **redundant** (it follows from `hX` through `f.appTop`); since it is a `Prop` this costs no
  generality, but I added `fr_naturality'` (in `Sanity.lean`) which derives it (VERIFIED).
- **`fr_Spec R h : fr (Spec R) = Spec.map (r ↦ r ^ p)`.** Full strength (VERIFIED by reading).
- **Not vacuous / not the identity in disguise (VERIFIED, compiled):** `fr_Spec_zmod`: on
  `Spec 𝔽_p` it is `𝟙` (Fermat); `fr_Spec_polynomial_ne_id`: on `Spec 𝔽_p[T]` it is not `𝟙`
  (`T ↦ T^p`, degree `p ≠ 1`).
- **`AlgFrobRedRed.AbsFrob`** (alg-frob-red's glue Prop) is copied `cmp`-identically from
  `scratch/alg-frob-red/AlgFrobRed.lean` (VERIFIED, 6-line block). It asks, for `k : Type` a field
  of characteristic `p` and `X` over `Spec k`, for some `Fr : X ⟶ X` lying over `Spec(Frob_k)` and
  natural for `Spec R`-points with `CharP R p`. Its docstring also says "identity on the space,
  `f ↦ f^p` on sections", which the Prop does not state; but the Prop is not weaker in substance:
  naturality along the open immersions of the nonzero affine opens `Spec R ⊆ X` (these have
  `CharP R p`) determines `Fr` on an open cover, hence uniquely (SPECULATIVE argument, not
  formalized). The proof supplies `Fr = fr`, which does have the stated space/section behaviour.
  Universe: `k`, `R : Type` (universe 0), as the project uses.

**Verdict on AbsFrob: statements faithful; one redundant hypothesis (`hY` in `fr_naturality`),
removed in a corollary; nothing weaker than claimed.**
