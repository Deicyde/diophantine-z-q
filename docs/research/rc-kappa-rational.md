# Rationality of the fixed parametrization scale

**Scoped stop.** The fixed scale `κ` is rational if the rational map of tex 1785–1787 agrees with `phiAn E f Lat κ` on the width-one cusp tail. This local comparison suffices: the leading coefficients of the map's two affine coordinates are `κ⁻²` and `-κ⁻³`; their negative ratio is `κ`. The converse on the entire interior chart follows from the existing algebraization. Thus the base cannot bootstrap rationality by applying its at-scale map construction to `κ` itself. The remaining input is an **independently** supplied rational map and its local comparison at this same scale. No new print or named hypothesis was added.

Use `E := Ecurve ℓ`, `N := newformLevel E` and `X := X0Branch.model N` for the application. The exact sufficient missing statement, in this tree's chart vocabulary, is
```lean
∃ (π : X.X ⟶ Proj E.toProjective.grading)
  (hQ : π ≫ projectiveOverQ E = X.overQ) (T : ℝ),
  ∀ τ : UpperHalfPlane, T < τ.im →
    projectiveValue ((X.complexPoint τ).comp π hQ).val = phiAn E f Lat κ τ
```
It is local and asks for no Jacobian, cusp classes, Hecke relation, or dominance witness. Equivalently for the local comparison one may obtain the `HasSum` expansions of the two affine coordinates at the fixed scale (see the theorem below). The geometric missing step is substantial: obtain the manuscript's based rational morphism on this model and pin down its differential in the chosen uniformization. A construction giving an arbitrary rational map to `E` without a pullback or chart comparison does not supply the statement.

In differential terms let `x,y ∈ ℚ((q))` be the Laurent coordinates of the independently supplied map. Write `θ = q d/dq`, `G = qExpansion 1 f`. The exact sufficient differential identity is
```lean
PullbackIsMultiple E x y (qExpansion 1 f) κ
```
for a nonconstant `x`, i.e. `θx = κ G (2y + a₁x + a₃)` after embedding into `ℂ((q))`. The existing coefficient comparison already handles that input. The chart proof here instead uses the same normalized differential through its primitive: `F(q) = κq + O(q²)`, `x = ℘(F) − b₂/12`, `2y+a₁x+a₃ = ℘′(F)`. The local parameter `-x/y` has linear coefficient `κ`. Seeing only the leading term of `x` gives at most rationality of `κ²`; even realness and a choice of sign would not make an arbitrary rational square root rational. These comparisons are on-the-spot, provenance (c).

If an independent map gives a nonzero rational scalar `r` but is not identified with the already fixed analytic map, that rationality alone does not establish `κ = r`. An alternative sufficient route, on-the-spot (c), would compare scales without equating the maps: if the period subgroup `r · Π(f)` has finite index in `Lat.lattice`, some positive integer `m` satisfies `m · Lat.lattice ⊆ r · Π(f)`. Then `mκ/r` is a multiplier of `Lat`; the existing `IsNonCMLattice Lat` implies it is an integer. Neither this full-rank period comparison nor an independently proved rational reference scale is supplied by the existing setup. No integrality, unit condition, or numerical value of a Manin constant is required by either route. Using the particular map and proving its differential realizes the fixed scale is the manuscript's route.
