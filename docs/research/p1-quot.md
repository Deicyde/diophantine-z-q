### 2.3 Glue C1 from Edixhoven's theorem (helper `QuotientP1/FixedDisjoint.lean`)

New leaf, over genuine definitions:

```lean
def EdixhovenFixedSmooth2 : Prop :=
  ∀ (p : ℕ) [Fact p.Prime] (𝒴 : Over (Spec (CommRingCat.of (Zloc p)))), Smooth 𝒴.hom →
    ∀ (σ τ : Aut 𝒴.left) (hσ : σ.asIso.hom ≫ 𝒴.hom = 𝒴.hom) (hτ : τ.asIso.hom ≫ 𝒴.hom = 𝒴.hom),
      Finite (Subgroup.closure ({σ, τ} : Set (Aut 𝒴.left))) →
      ¬ p ∣ Nat.card (Subgroup.closure ({σ, τ} : Set (Aut 𝒴.left))) →
      Smooth (pullback.fst (equalizer.ι (Over.homMk σ.asIso.hom hσ : 𝒴 ⟶ 𝒴) (𝟙 𝒴)).left
          (equalizer.ι (Over.homMk τ.asIso.hom hτ : 𝒴 ⟶ 𝒴) (𝟙 𝒴)).left ≫
        (equalizer.ι (Over.homMk σ.asIso.hom hσ : 𝒴 ⟶ 𝒴) (𝟙 𝒴)).left ≫ 𝒴.hom)
```

The source is B. Edixhoven, *Néron models and tame ramification*, Compositio Math. 81 (1992), 291-306, Prop. 3.4
(locator UNVERIFIED: no network). It says: for a smooth `S`-scheme `X` and a finite group `G` of `S`-automorphisms of
order invertible on `S`, the fixed-point scheme `X^G` is smooth over `S`.

- Here `S = Spec ℤ_(p)` and `G = ⟨σ, τ⟩`.
- `X^G` represents `T ↦ X(T)^G`. That is the fibre product over `X` of the fixed schemes of the two generators,
  each the equalizer of the automorphism and the identity in schemes over `S`.

Compiled consequences (namespace `HDec.MCRQuot`):

| declaration | content |
|---|---|
| `isClosed_ptOf_of_qbar` | the point of a `ℚ̄`-point of a `ℚ`-scheme locally of finite type is closed (the kernel of `Γ(U) → ℚ̄` is maximal; Jacobson) |
| `eq_of_specializes_generic` | a specialization of the point of a `ℚ̄`-point of `𝒴` lying over the generic point of `Spec ℤ_(p)` is that point |
| `infinite_qbar_closure` | a proper generization of the point of a `ℚ̄`-point has infinitely many `ℚ̄`-points in its closure (Jacobson generic fibre) |
| `exists_common_generization` | two generizations of a point with a domain stalk have a common generization (`Scheme.range_fromSpecStalk`) |
| `fixed_generization` | for `ρ` with finitely many fixed `ℚ̄`-points and smooth fixed scheme, a generization (inside `X^ρ`) of a point in the closure of a fixed point `t` is specialized from `t`; the stalks of `X^ρ` are domains by the vendored FLT theorem `Smooth.isDomain_and_isIntegrallyClosed_stalk_of_isDiscreteValuationRing` |
| **`disjoint_closure_of_two`** | distinct `ℚ̄`-points fixed by `σ`, `τ`, each with finitely many fixed points and with `⟨σ, τ⟩` finite and tame, have disjoint closures in `𝒴` |
| `tameFixedPointsDisjoint_of_edixhoven2` | mcr-glue's L5 as the case `τ = σ` |
| **`cMSectionsDisjoint'_of`** | glue C1 (`CMSectionsDisjoint` without its premises L5, L6) from Edixhoven, glue A, (A-fix), (C1a), (C1d) and `CMStabNontrivial` |
| `cMStabNontrivial_of` | `CMStabNontrivial` (a nontrivial element of `fibreAut` fixes each CM point) from `CMStabObjWeak`, without `CurveBijIso` or `ModelYIrreducible` |

How `disjoint_closure_of_two` is proved:

1. A common point `z` of the two closures lies in `X^{⟨σ,τ⟩}`. That scheme is smooth (Edixhoven), hence flat, over
   `ℤ_(p)`.
2. Flat maps are generalizing (Mathlib `Flat.generalizingMap`). So `z` has a generization `ζ` in `X^{⟨σ,τ⟩}` over the
   generic point of `Spec ℤ_(p)`.
3. In `X^σ` the stalk at `z` is a domain, so `ζ` and the point `x` of `y` have a common generization `ξ`. If
   `ξ ≠ x`, the closure of `ξ` (inside `X^σ`) would carry infinitely many `σ`-fixed `ℚ̄`-points. Hence `x ⤳ ζ`.
4. The same holds for the point `x'` of `y'` and `τ`, so `x' ⤳ ζ`.
5. `ζ` lies in the generic fibre, so `ζ = x` and `ζ = x'`, a contradiction.

In C1 the two automorphisms are the stabilizer elements of the two CM points. They lie in `fibreAut`, which is
finite of order prime to `p` (glue A). The old proof needed cyclic inertia (L6) to make two *involutions* agree and
then L5. The fixed scheme of the group they generate removes both, and also the need for involutions.

## 4. `CMStabObjWeak` (gap-triage A12)

```lean
def CMStabObjWeak : Prop :=
  ∀ P : QML3 Qbar, (HasCM4 P.q ∨ HasCM3 P.q ∨ HasCM24 P.q) →
    ∃ (α : LeafQM.O ≃+* LeafQM.O) (u : (LeafQM.O)ˣ),
      M3.twist α (M3.rightMul u (M3.mk P)) = M3.mk P ∧
      ∃ m : M3 Qbar, M3.twist α (M3.rightMul u m) ≠ m
```

It is mcr-quot's `CMStabObj` without the involution clause (`cMStabObjWeak_of : CMStabObj → CMStabObjWeak`,
compiled). Analysis (SPECULATIVE except where marked):

**`ℤ[i]` and `ℤ[ζ₃]`: the fixing part.** Take `α = id` and `u ≡ (action of φ on A[3])⁻¹ mod 3𝒪`.

- T5 makes `φ ∘ η` a level structure (`φ` is invertible: `φ² = −1`, resp. `φ(1 − φ) = 1`).
- `Level3.torsor` gives `φ(η(a)) = η(a u₀)`, and `exists_unit_congr` lifts `u₀⁻¹` to a unit `u`.
- Then `φ` is an isomorphism `rightMul u P ≅ P`.

**`ℤ[i]`: the clause `T ≠ id`.**

- If `rightMul u` fixed `[rightMul v P]`, an automorphism `c` of `(A, ι)` would act on `A[3]` as `v⁻¹u⁻¹v`.
- `c` commutes with `φ` once the commutant is commutative (the chain's `QMCentralizerCM`).
- VERIFIED by computation in `𝒪/3𝒪` (`scratch/p1-quot/py/conj.py`, with mcr-quot's multiplication table; 72 units):
  for each of the 18 residues with `u² ≡ −1` there is a unit `v` with `v⁻¹u⁻¹v` not commuting with `u⁻¹`.
- So this case reduces to `QMCentralizerCM` and T5. It is not compiled.

**`ℤ[ζ₃]`: the clause `T ≠ id`.**

- VERIFIED by the same computation: for every residue `u` with `u² − u + 1 ≡ 0`, all conjugates of `u⁻¹` commute
  with `u⁻¹`.
- For some pairs of such a `u` and a `ℤ[i]` action element `w₄`, every conjugate also commutes with `w₄`.
- So the CM objects' own orbits under `rightMul` do not witness `T ≠ id`. A witness needs an object with smaller
  automorphism group (a non-CM point) or an Atkin–Lehner-twisted object, and neither is available from the chain's
  leaves.

**`ℤ[√−6]`.**

- The commutant's units are `±1`, so the stabilizer comes from an Atkin–Lehner twist `α = conj(v)` with
  `nrd v = 6`. `T` fixes `[P]` iff some `ψ ∈ Aut(A)` has `ψ ι(a) ψ⁻¹ = ι(v a v⁻¹)`.
- A route: `ker φ` and `ker ι(v)` are `𝒪`-submodules of `A[6] ≅ 𝒪/6𝒪` of order 36, hence equal (unique, since `𝒪`
  is ramified at 2 and 3). Then `φ` factors as `ψ ∘ ι(v)` with `ψ` an automorphism.
- That needs isogeny factorization and degrees in the kernel's abelian-variety layer, which are not there.
- In print this is the moduli form of "`w_m` fixes the CM points by orders containing `√−m`" (Ogg 1983, *Real
  points on Shimura curves*; Rotger 2004; locators UNVERIFIED). The print states it on `X₀^D(ℂ)`, not on
  `(A, ι, η)`.

So `CMStabObjWeak` remains the one project glue under the field.

- Rigidity is used elsewhere in the chain as well (`r1R2fpRep_iff_ab`, `shimuraFamily_iff_ab`). So this field alone
