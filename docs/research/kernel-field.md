**Connection to G4 (the task's request).** g4-weilres, inertia-homs and morph-descend proved the
Hom field (`homField_of_model_leaves'`). Their theorem hides the kernel field `E` and the finite
group `G ≤ GL_r(ℤ)`, which `KernelFieldOut` asks for. `kernelField_core` re-runs
`homField_core`'s proof with both exposed: `E = ℚ̄^{ker ρ}`, `G = ρ(G_ℚ)`, `[E:ℚ] = |G|`, and `E`
unramified outside `T`. `kernelField_of_model` then adds the rank bound `r ≤ 16F₀²` and the
statement that `G_{E⊔K}` fixes every homomorphism between conjugates. That is G4 with only
`HomFreeFinite` and `NeronOggShafarevich` as leaves, because `MorphismsDescend` and
`InertiaFixesHomsMon` are theorems.

G4's three side hypotheses are discharged on a rational fibre `PK/K`:
- `hdim` by g0-dim's `G0Dim.dim_baseChange` (`conj_dim`);
- `hgood` by `LeafQM.AV.QML3.goodReduction` together with inertia-homs' `comap_isDVR` and
  `isUnit_prime_of_liesOver`, using `3 ∈ T` (`good_of_QML3`);
- `hK` by `galUnramifiedOutside_iff`.

**Skolem–Noether descent (the task's second request).** Two parts are proved.

- The algebraic core is `aut_O_inner`: every ring automorphism `α` of `𝒪` (the twist that A7
  leaves) is conjugation by a unit of `B = (2,-3)_ℚ`. The proof extends `α` to `B` through the
  quaternion basis `α(i), α(j)`, then applies T13, which is proved in
  `ZInQKernel/HDecomp/Textbook/SkolemNoether.lean`.
- The NoCM part is made explicit, as the task allows ("an explicit End⁰ = B hypothesis"). It is
  `EndEqOOnHomField`, with FS5's conclusion as its hypothesis. `conjMaps_of_qm` proves that this
  is the only place NoCM enters. Given `End_L(A_L) = ι(𝒪)`:
  - commutation of `m_σ` with `End_L(A_L)` follows from `𝒪`-linearity;
  - a discrepancy that is an endomorphism `δ` up to a nonzero integer is `ι(b)` with `b` central
    (`faithful_O`), hence an integer (`center_O`);
  - the rational discrepancy `c`, its cocycle identity, its inflation, `c(1,1) = 1` and the
    kernel-degree identity then follow (`conjugacyData_of_maps`).

## 3. What remains (named Props), with leaf/glue status

All three are glue: each is a consequence of published theorems through arguments the project
must formalize. None is a leaf.

1. **`EndEqOOnHomField`** (glue; NoCM on the Hom field). Hypotheses: R1fp-R2fp moduli data, a
   `ℚ̄`-point over a rational `j₀` with object `P`, FS5's conclusion `End_𝒪(P.A) = ℤ`, a rational
   fibre `PK/K` over `P`, and a Hom field `L ⊇ K`. Conclusion: every `f ∈ End_L(A_L)` is
   `ι_L(a)` for some `a ∈ 𝒪`. Here `ι_L = endBC ∘ PK.q.iota`, and `endBC` is copied from
   bp-qmmoduli.
   - *Argument* (model check, no human review). T27 (Yu, leaf `PkgI3.QMDichotomyYu`) and FS5 give
     `End⁰(P.A) ≅ B`, as in g0-fibresimple's proof of FibreSimple.
   - `ι(B) ⊆ End⁰` and both have dimension 4, so `End(P.A)` is an order of `ι(B)` containing
     `ι(𝒪)`. It equals `ι(𝒪)` because `𝒪` is maximal (`Structures.lean`'s docstring; maximality
     is not compiled).
   - `P ≅ (PK)_ℚ̄` as QM objects, by R1's naturality and `RationalFibre`.
   - `End_L → End_ℚ̄` is injective (`endBC_injective` in bp-qmmoduli, compiled there).
   - Medium. Missing in Lean: maximality of `𝒪` (finite lattice computation), and the dimension
     count `ι(B) = End⁰`.
2. **`ConjIsogMaps`** (glue; geometric, no NoCM content). Same hypotheses without FS5. There are
   maps `m_σ` on `A_L(ℚ̄)` with the clauses `ConjIsogMapsAt`:
   - Galois action on `G_L`, and inflation;
   - `𝒪`-linearity;
   - surjective with finite kernel;
   - the discrepancy `e·m_σ m_τ = δ∘m_στ` with `δ ∈ End_L(A_L)`, `e ≠ 0`;
   - algebraicity `m_σ(x) = F_σ∘x∘Spec σ`.

   *Argument* (model check):
   - A7 (`LeafQM.AV.conj_twist_of_rational`, compiled, valid for every R1fp-R2fp triple) gives
     `^σP ≅ P` up to a twist `α_σ ∈ Aut 𝒪`.
   - `aut_O_inner` writes `α_σ = u_σ(·)u_σ⁻¹`. Then `μ_σ = ι(Nu_σ)∘(iso)` is a `B`-linear isogeny.
   - It is defined over `L`, because `L` is a Hom field and `G_L`-fixed morphisms descend.
   - For `δ = μ_σ ^σμ_τ ψ`, take a quasi-inverse `ψ μ_στ = [e]` (Mumford §8; the same input as
     g0-split's `ReverseIsogeny`).
   - `F_σ` is `μ_σ` composed with the identification of the underlying schemes of `^σA` and `A`.
   - It is true for CM fibres too, since nothing in it needs NoCM.
   - Large. It is points-level and scheme-level plumbing over the existing `conjData`/`conj`
     machinery, plus two textbook inputs: the quasi-inverse of an isogeny, and Galois descent of
     morphisms (`MorphismsDescend` gives the existence half).
3. **`G0FibreSimple.FibreComTrivial`** (FS5, g0-fibresimple's piece, unchanged). For `u ∉ 𝒞`,
   `End_𝒪(P.A) = ℤ`.

## 4. Findings

- **`KernelFieldExists` needs NoCM, through clause 3 of `ConjugacyData`** (mathematical argument,
  not compiled). If `End⁰(A_ℚ̄) = M₂(F)` with `F` imaginary quadratic (a CM fibre), then
  `f ↦ μ_σ ^σf μ_σ⁻¹` restricts to `σ|_F` on the centre `F`. It is not inner when `σ` moves `F`,
  so no `m_σ` commutes with all of `End_L(A_L)`. The quantifier over all triples therefore
  carries FS5's pinning issue. The split above isolates this issue in FS5 alone: `ConjIsogMaps`
  and `EndEqOOnHomField` hold for every triple (model check).
- **`KernelFieldOut` does not use its argument `P`, and links `G` to `E` only through
  `[E:ℚ] = |G|`.** Neither is a defect.
  - The degree bound needs only the cardinality.
  - `ConjugacyData` on `A_{E⊔K}` (clauses 2-3) forces the `μ_σ` to be defined over `E ⊔ K`.
  - `kernelField_of_model` produces the genuine `E`: the kernel field of the action on the
    endomorphism lattice of `Res_{K/ℚ}A`.
- **Clause-order check of `ConjugacyData`** (VERIFIED by reading, together with g0-mod-field's
  compiled `descentDatum_bot`). With `Spec` contravariant, `m_{σk} = m_σ m_k` and
  `m_{kσ} = m_k m_σ` are consistent with `F_{σk} = F_{kσ} = F_σ` and `F_k = id`.
