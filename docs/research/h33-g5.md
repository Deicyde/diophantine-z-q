## 1. Result

**G5 is now a Lean proof.** TYZ define their CM point as `P_n = [h, 1] ∈ X_U(ℂ)`, an adelic point. The kernel's leaf
uses `τ_n ∈ ℍ` instead. Lean now proves that these are the same point, and the leaf is restated with TYZ's adelic
points. The restated leaf is equivalent in Lean to h33-d1's leaf and discharges the binder `h33`. Everything below
is VERIFIED: compiled, standard axioms only, no `sorry`.

1. **The level `U₀(N)` and its determinant** (`AdelicX0/StrongApprox.lean`).
   - `U0 N` is TYZ's `U₀(N) = {g ∈ GL₂(ℤ̂) : N ∣ c}` (L1179–1181), defined as a subgroup of `GL₂(𝔸_f)`. Here
     `𝔸_f` is Mathlib's `FiniteAdeleRing ℤ ℚ` and `ℤ̂` is Tau Ceti's `integralAdeles`.
   - `det_U0 : det U₀(N) = ℤ̂ˣ`.
   - `sl2_reduction_surjective`: `SL₂(ℤ) → SL₂(ℤ/N)` is onto. This is in Tau Ceti; Mathlib does not have it.
2. **Strong approximation** (`strong_approx`): `GL₂(𝔸_f) = GL₂(ℚ)⁺ · U₀(N)` for `N ≠ 0`. The proof takes four
   steps:
   1. Clear denominators. Tau Ceti's `mul_nonZeroDivisor_mem_integralAdeles` makes `A = Mg` and `B = M'g⁻¹`
      integral, with `AB = D`.
   2. Approximate `A ≡ m (mod D)` by an integer matrix. `ℤ` is dense in `ℤ̂` (Tau Ceti's `closure_range_algebraMap`).
   3. Take a basis `γ` of the lattice `mℤ² + Dℤ²` (Mathlib's `Submodule.basisOfPid`; `lattice_basis_pos`). Then
      `γ⁻¹A ∈ GL₂(ℤ̂)`.
   4. For `k ∈ GL₂(ℤ̂)`, lift `k · diag(1, det k⁻¹) mod N` to some `σ ∈ SL₂(ℤ)`. Then `σ⁻¹k ∈ U₀(N)`.
3. **The bijection** (`gamma0OrbitsEquiv`).
   - `Sh U` is the double coset space `GL₂(ℚ)⁺ \ (ℍ × GL₂(𝔸_f)/U)`, a quotient type. It is TYZ's uniformization
     (L1518–1520) without the cusps.
   - `toSh U τ = [τ, 1]`.
   - `toSh_eq_toSh_iff`: `[τ, 1] = [τ', 1]` iff `τ' ∈ Γ₀(N)τ`. The key input is `GL₂(ℚ)⁺ ∩ U₀(N) = Γ₀(N)`, which
     uses `ℚ ∩ ℤ̂ = ℤ`.
   - `toSh_surjective` follows from strong approximation.
   - Together these give `Γ₀(N) \ ℍ ≃ Sh (U0 N)`.
4. **`P_n ↦ τ_n`** (`AdelicX0/CMPoints.lean`).
   - `UTYZ` is TYZ's `U = {g ∈ U₀(32) : 4 ∣ a − d}` (L1183–1185), proved to be a subgroup.
   - `tyzMat5` and `tyzMat7` are TYZ's images of `√−n` (L1225–1229), with `δ = tyzDelta n`.
   - `fixed_tyzEmb5_iff` and `fixed_tyzEmb7_iff`: the fixed points of these matrices in `ℍ` are exactly
     `(tyzForm n).tauH`, resp. `(tyzForm7 n).tauH`. So the fixed point exists and is unique.
   - `tyzP5` and `tyzP7` are `P_n = [h, 1] ∈ Sh UTYZ`, with `h` the fixed point (L1236–1240).
   - `f0_tyzP5` and `f0_tyzP7`: `f₀(P_n) = [τ_n, 1] ∈ Sh (U0 32)`. Under `gamma0OrbitsEquiv` this is the
     `Γ₀(32)`-orbit of `τ_n`.
5. **The restated leaf and its bridge** (`TYZThm33Adelic.lean`).
   - `IsShPointAt N ι P x` says that the `ℂ`-point `x` of the model is the point `P ∈ Sh (U0 N)`.
   - `isPointAt_smul_iff`: hgz-steps' `IsPointAt` is `Γ₀(N)`-invariant. The proof uses the `Γ₀(N)`-invariance of
     `j(τ)` and `j(Nτ)`, plus the fact that the Möbius map is a local homeomorphism.
   - Hence `isShPointAt_toSh_iff` (`IsShPointAt [τ, 1] x ↔ IsPointAt τ x`) and `isShPointAt_iff_exists`.
   - The leaf `TYZThm33AdelicSome` uses TYZ's `f₀(P_n)`.
   - `tyzThm33AdelicSome_iff`: the new leaf is equivalent to `H33D1.TYZThm33CurveSome`.
   - `tyzThm33_of_adelic (hCM) : TYZThm33AdelicSome → TYZSplit.TYZThm33` discharges the binder `h33`.
6. **G7** (§4 of `TYZThm33Adelic.lean`).
   - `TYZPt` is TYZ's model `2y² = x³ + x` over `ℚ̄`: the point `O` or an affine solution.
   - `tyzEquiv : TYZPt ≃ PtA` is the bijection `(x, y) ↦ (2x, 4y)`, `O ↦ O`.
   - `tyzToA_iTYZ`: it commutes with `[i] : (x, y) ↦ (−x, iy)`, with the same `i` on both sides.
   - `tyzToA_neg`: it commutes with negation.
   - `collinear_tyzToA` and `vertical_tyzToA`: it maps lines to lines, so the chord-and-tangent laws with origin `O`
     correspond.
   - The docstring constant in `TYZThm33Alg.lean` line 65 is fixed: `8(2y² − x³ − x) = (4y)² − (2x)³ − 4·2x`, where
     it said 16.
7. **G2 at the analytic level** (§5 of `TYZThm33Adelic.lean`).
   - `shFunEquiv`: functions on `Sh (U0 N)` are the same as `Γ₀(N)`-invariant functions on `ℍ`.
   - `exists_shFn`: every `P(j, j_N)/Q(j, j_N)` descends to `Sh (U0 N)`.
   - `qExp_mem_X0Field`: if a function on `Sh (U0 N)` is meromorphic, of moderate growth at the cusps, and has a
     rational `q`-expansion, then that expansion lies in `ℚ(j, j_N)`. This is `gamma0QExpansion_proof` read through
     the bijection.
   - §5b, if it compiled (see §6): `x0Field_eq_adjoin_ratFnSh`, which says `X0Field N` is the field generated by
     these `q`-expansions.

## 2. The leaf


`TYZ33BodyAd` is h33-d1's `TYZ33Body`, with one change in the point hypotheses on `w`. They now read
`∀ x, IsShPointAt 32 ι (shMap UTYZ_le (tyzP5 n hn)) x → toC (w n) = ptOf curveA (x ≫ i₀)` (the same for `tyzP7`).
That is, `w n = i₀(f₀(P_n))` for TYZ's `P_n = [h, 1] ∈ X_U(ℂ)`.

**Rating: printed form (LEAF)** (SPECULATIVE until a statement check).
- `P_n` is now TYZ's literal definition: `[h, 1]`, with `h` the fixed point of TYZ's matrix, in TYZ's double coset
  space for TYZ's `U`.
- `f₀` is the natural projection, and `i₀` is an isomorphism of curves on a model of `X₀(32)`, as in h33-d1.
- The leaf is equivalent in Lean to h33-d1's leaf, so it asks for neither more nor less.
- `δ = tyzDelta n` is one admissible choice of TYZ's `δ` (L1229: "an integer such that"). The leaf is therefore an
  instance of the printed statement.
- Nothing in it is invented for the purpose:
  - `Sh`, `U0`, `UTYZ` and `tyzP5`/`tyzP7` are concrete definitions over Mathlib's finite adeles.
  - `IsShPointAt` is meaningful: every `P` is some `[τ, 1]`, and the condition is independent of the choice of `τ`
    (`isShPointAt_iff_exists`).
