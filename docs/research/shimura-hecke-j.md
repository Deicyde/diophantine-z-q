## ON THE SPOT (provenance (c))

The lower row of a lift of the unit class 1 has entries `0,1` modulo `N`.
The change of lattice basis for `g = (A B; C D)` identifies the lattice of
`τ` with `(Cτ+D)` times the lattice of `gτ`. Under that homothety,
`1/N` is carried to `1/N` plus a lattice element. Thus both defining
coordinates in `AtTau` agree along this action, including at the exceptional
Weber branches. The Möbius homeomorphism transports the punctured limits.
This handles the arbitrary lift in the upper branch of `IsXi`, which is
absent from the older prime formula.

For the upper representatives, following `(τ+j)/n` by `(τ+i)/m` gives the
offset `j + ni` at index `mn`. Euclidean division gives the bijection on
offsets, so the products of classes agree with multiplicities. At a bad prime
power, `1` is the only eligible divisor, so the same calculation gives
the entire action. At a good prime, the second branch has a singleton offset
and is exactly the scaling term of `IsHeckeT`.

To compare two `IsXi` operators, choose one admissible family of lifts by
Bézout and use the same realization of every output parameter in both
formulas. Their actions on the input differences agree. The existing
`end_eq_of_classes` argument reduces faithfulness to detecting constancy of
a pointed morphism on these test points. Realizing two specific parameters
is enough for constancy. The continuous analytic `j` takes distinct values
at `i` and `ρ`, so its image on the connected upper half-plane is uncountable
and meets the transcendental values. A test point there lies over the
generic point of the rational curve, since any value at a closed point is
algebraic over ℚ. The point over `i` lies over a closed point: evaluation at
the generic point embeds the function field, so `j(i) = 1728` there would
make `j_X` constant. A proper morphism agreeing at the two test points
sends the generic point to a closed point. Reducedness then gives
factorization through its value at the rational base point. This proves
pinning conditional on realization, without a generation theorem for the
complex Jacobian. Once actions are pinned, prime-power and coprime induction
constructs a witness satisfying the formula at each positive index. Pinning
proves that the witnesses obey the ring laws without a separate commutativity
input.

## Smallest remaining inputs

1. **Analytic point dictionary.** For each model in the fixed proposition
   and each upper-half-plane parameter, prove that a unique complex point
   satisfies `AtTau`. Existence suffices for pinning; single valuedness
   is also used to show that the identity satisfies the index-one formula.

   A proof can use the tree's holomorphic extensions of the generic
   coordinates in `ZInQKernel/Contrib/OneProof/WeberField.lean`.
   Identify the fraction field with
   the field generated by their germs, and reduce the local ring of
   finite-value germs at the parameter to `ℂ`. Properness centers that
   valuation on the curve, and comparison with the stalk and its residue
   map is needed to verify the `RegAt` conclusion. For uniqueness one
   would recover the same valuation and residue map from the finite
   values and use the uniqueness of the center on a separated curve.
   The existing `HeckePin.X1TauPoints` names the existence part. The
   germ/stalk comparison is a missing piece of analytic-to-algebraic
   curve theory.
2. **Two orbit counts.** On the free abelian group on
   `Γ₁(N) \ ℍ`, let `Rₙ[τ]` be the sum from the triangular
   representatives of `IsXi`, and `Dₚ[τ] = [γₚτ]` for the diamond.
   Prove the two identities on basis points (or just on basis
   differences): `RₘRₙ = Rₘₙ` for coprime positive `m,n`, and
   `Rₚ Rₚ^(k+1) = Rₚ^(k+2) + p Dₚ Rₚ^k` for `p ∤ N`,
   where each power in a subscript is an index. Multiplicities matter.
   Applying these sums to differences through the chosen `AtTau` points
   would give the two remaining closure hypotheses of
   `exists_xi_of_compositeActions` using `IsHeckeT` and `IsDiamond`.

   A route is row Hermite reduction with the first column congruence
   `(1,0)` mod `N`. At coprime indices the intermediate sublattice
   is unique. At a good prime, the products divisible entrywise by
   `p` have an extra `p` factorizations; dividing their matrices
   by the scalar `p` leaves the indicated diamond class.
   Tau Ceti has the prime and upper-branch coset families, abstract
   multiplicity machinery, and a composite product table at `Γ₀(N)`
   in `ZInQKernel/Contrib/TauCeti/NumberTheory/HeckeRing/GL2/Gamma0/Diagonal/Composite.lean`.
   It does not identify the fixed all-index family on the
   `Γ₁(N)` orbits with that table while retaining the diamond.
   The character-space or q-expansion equalities alone do not
   imply either identity on orbits.

With these inputs, the per-model assembly gives the unchanged

the print-only discharge. The print-only example and the combined
`6 LEAF / 23 GLUE` dry run do not apply at this stop. No fold was performed.
