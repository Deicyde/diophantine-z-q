## 0. Main result

**`X₀(32)` over `ℚ` is the elliptic curve `curveX0 : y² = x³ + 4x`** (= `CurveA.curveA`, Cremona 32a1,
the optimal curve of conductor 32, `ℚ`-isomorphic to TYZ's `A : 2y² = x³ + x`). Its relation to the
congruent-number curve `C₁ : y² = x³ − x` (= `TYZDecomp.curveC 1`, Cremona 32a2) is **proved in Lean**:

* `X₀(32) ≇ C₁` over `ℚ` (`not_exists_variableChange_X0_C1`);
* `X₀(32) ≅ C₁` over `ℚ(i)` (`isoVC_smul`, and the additive bijection `iota`, `iotaInv`);
* `X₀(32)` and `C₁` are `2`-isogenous over `ℚ` in both directions: `φ = ι ∘ (1 + [i]) : A → C₁`,
  `ψ = (1 − [i]) ∘ ι⁻¹ : C₁ → A`, additive, `ψ ∘ φ = [2]`, `φ ∘ ψ = [2]`, with the rational formulas
  `φ(x, y) = (y²/4x², y(x² − 4)/8x²)`, `ψ(x, y) = (y²/x², y(x² + 1)/x²)` (`φ(0,0) = ψ(0,0) = O`), both
  `Gal(ℚ̄/ℚ)`-equivariant.

**Bridge to cm-points, proved:** their leaf `CM.CMComplexConj` (complex conjugation maps the Heegner
point of `[A, B, C]` to that of `[A, −B, C]`) is a theorem, `X0.cmComplexConj : CM.CMComplexConj`
(`ZInQKernel/Contrib/TYZGenus/X0CM.lean`, written against `CM.lean` at cm-points commit ba214e17), from
`phiC_neg_conj` (the eta quotients have real `q`-expansions); no algebraicity input is needed.

Also (`ZInQKernel/Contrib/TYZGenus/X0Pkg.lean`, against cm-points' `Package.lean` at ba214e17): the range
of their pinned `τ` (`Package.tauG y = y • (2, −4)`) is exactly `A[(1+i)³]` (`tauG_range_iff`), so their leaf
`Package.Lemma318` is equivalent to "every torsion point of `A(ℍ'_n)` lies in `A[(1+i)³]`" (`lemma318_iff`),
i.e. TYZ Lemmas 3.16 + 3.18 without Tian's description of `τ` (TYZ L1386–1399).

Also proved: `A[(1+i)³]` is exactly `{O, (0,0), (2,±4), (−2,±4i), (±2i,0)}`, hence **half of TYZ Lemma 3.16**
(`A[(1+i)³] ⊆ A(ℚ(i))`), so the leaf `CurveA.TYZLemma316` is equivalent to its rank-zero half
`A(ℚ(i)) ⊆ A[(1+i)³]` (`tyzLemma316_iff`).

**The modular parametrization** is given explicitly by eta quotients over Mathlib's `ModularForm.eta`:

```
x(τ) = η(2τ)²η(16τ) / (η(4τ)η(32τ)²) + 2 = q⁻² + 2q⁶ − … ,
y(τ) = −η(8τ)⁴η(16τ)² / (η(4τ)²η(32τ)⁴) = −q⁻³ − 2q + … ,
f(τ) = η(4τ)²η(8τ)² = q − 2q⁵ − 3q⁹ + 6q¹³ + 2q¹⁷ − … ,
```

with `phiC : ℍ → A(ℂ)`, `τ ↦ (x(τ), y(τ))`. The identities that make `phiC` the modular parametrization
`X₀(32) ≅ A` are stated as nine leaves over genuine definitions (§3), each VERIFIED numerically here (exact
integer `q`-series arithmetic to `q⁷⁹`, and floating point at the cusps): `y² = x³ + 4x`; `Γ₀(32)`-invariance;
`Γ₀(32)\ℍ ≅ A(ℂ) ∖ A[(1+i)³]`; `dx/2y = 2πi f dτ` (Manin constant 1); `f = Σ aₙ(A)qⁿ` with `aₙ` from
Mathlib's `L(A, s)`; `f ∈ S₂(Γ₀(32))`; the cusp dictionary (agreeing with the PARI modular-symbol table of
`rv-tyz-inputs.md` §1.5a and TYZ's/Tian's table, L1393–1404 as quoted there); the identification with the
Weierstrass uniformization of the Eichler integral `F(τ) = Σ (aₘ/m) qᵐ` (the same expression as cm-points'
`CM.modParam`); and the Fricke involution `W₃₂ : P ↦ T − P`, `T = (2, −4)`. Proved analytic pieces:
holomorphy of `x, y, f` on `ℍ`, no zeros of `y, f` on `ℍ`, invariance of `x, y, f` under `T ∈ Γ₀(32)`,
real `q`-expansions (`φ(−τ̄) = conj φ(τ)`), and the Fricke transforms of `x, y, f` in closed form, in
particular `f(−1/(32τ)) = −32τ²f(τ)` (`f` is a `W₃₂`-eigenform with eigenvalue `−1`).

## 3. What a proof of the analytic leaves would need (SPECULATIVE)

* The eta transformation law `η(γτ) = ε(γ)√(cτ+d) η(τ)` with **explicit** multiplier (Rademacher/Dedekind
  sums), or Newman's theorem on eta quotients. Mathlib (pinned) has `ModularForm.eta`, holomorphy,
  non-vanishing and `logDeriv η = (πi/12)E₂` only. Tau Ceti main has `DedekindEta/Transformation.lean`
  (`eta_smul`, with an abstract multiplier `etaMultiplier γ`, `etaMultiplier_pow_twentyFour`), Eichler
  integrals (`EichlerIntegral/`), modular symbols and periods (`ModularSymbols/`) and `SturmBound`; it is on
  Lean v4.36.0-rc2 and not vendored here. With an explicit multiplier and TC's Sturm bound, EQ, INV, DIFF,
  CUSP become finite `q`-expansion checks; BIJ additionally needs the valence formula on `X₀(32)`.
* NEWFORM is the modularity of a CM curve (Hecke character of `ℚ(i)` of conductor `(1+i)³`) plus the
  identification of its theta series with `η(4τ)²η(8τ)²`; Hecke's continuation of `L(A, s)` is already a
  theorem of the fork, but the Fourier side is not.
