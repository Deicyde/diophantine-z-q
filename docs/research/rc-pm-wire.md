# (O.7) from the pinned rational map and its good model

`PMWire.oddStagedOfGoodRows` fills `Wiring.PreLeaves.odd_staged` at the same `withParamIndices a` as the quotient lane. The special values are still the reductions of the unique Néron extension of the fixed rational parametrization. The integral Atkin–Lehner operator is the unique extension of the pinned rational one; the signed relation is proved using that tie. The source has no integral or special-fibre cusp field. The elliptic translation is the reduction of the actual rational point `π(W∞) − π(∞)`. Its torsion is obtained by applying the existing Elkik print to the represented geometric cusp classes of `J₀(N)`.

The generic comparison is proved from the chart function field, in both model designs. For a proper model, take the compactification to be the generic source itself. For an open model the comparison is with the smooth proper compactification and restricts to a dense open immersion on the generic source. The CM comparison uses the lift to that open and the same map and residue embedding as before. No integral modular model or conductor-one reduction is constructed here. No new printed leaf or dependency on a scheme-construction design is introduced.

The focused Lean audit verifies the exact type of `odd_staged` and counts **30 binders versus 30** in the quotient lane. The difference is inside the inputs: `G` has a chart and a pinned extension in place of a free comparison and operator, and has no modular cusp. `relations` is Hecke data only. `cusps` is the one existing printed proposition at the represented classes, paired with the choice of the fixed multiplier for the row translations. The normalization at infinity is proved from the supplied scale witness `a.hr`.
