## 1. General lemmas (Mathlib-only, `import Mathlib`)

All in namespace `AlgebraicGeometry` unless stated; VERIFIED.

**`StalkPullback.lean`** (stalks of fibre products):
- `StalkGen p q t`: every `r ∈ 𝒪_{T,t}` is `c / w` with `c, w` in the subring generated by the images
  of the stalk maps of `p : T ⟶ X`, `q : T ⟶ Y`, and `w` a unit.
- **`pullback_stalkGen f g z : StalkGen (pullback.fst f g) (pullback.snd f g) z`** for any
  `f : X ⟶ S`, `g : Y ⟶ S`, `z ∈ X ×_S Y`: the surjectivity half of "`𝒪_{X×_S Y,z}` is a
  localization of `𝒪_{X,x} ⊗_{𝒪_{S,s}} 𝒪_{Y,y}`" (Stacks 01JT, EGA I 3.6, UNVERIFIED locators).
  Proof: affine charts `Spec A → X`, `Spec B → Y` over `Spec R → S` (`exists_affine_chart_over`),
  the open chart `Spec (A ⊗[R] B) → X ×_S Y` (`pullbackSpecIso`, `pullback.map` of open
  immersions, `Scheme.Pullback.range_map`), `IsAffineOpen.isLocalization_stalk`, and transport
  (`StalkGen.of_comp`, `StalkGen.comp_openImmersion`, `StalkGen.comp_of_isIso`, `StalkGen.symm`).
- `RationalOver f x` (residue field of `x` generated by `𝒪_{S,f x}`); `rationalOver_of_section`,
  `rationalOver_of_retraction`.
- **`maximalIdeal_eq_of_stalkGen`**: for a commutative square `p ≫ f = q ≫ g` with `StalkGen p q t`
  and `p t` rational over `s`: `𝔪_t = 𝔪_x 𝒪_t + 𝔪_y 𝒪_t`.
  **`exists_sub_mem_maximalIdeal_of_stalkGen`**: every element of `𝒪_t` is `≡ q^♯ b (mod 𝔪_t)`.
- `maximalIdeal_stalk_spec_field`, `stalkMap_comp_eq_zero_of_maximalIdeal_eq_bot`,
  `mem_maximalIdeal_of_iso`, `stalkMap_congr_point_apply`.
- **`isOpenImmersion_of_section_of_maximalIdeal_eq_bot`**: if `q : P ⟶ Spec k` is locally of finite
  type over a field and `u` is a section of `q` whose point has a field as local ring, then `u` is an
  open immersion (via `isClosedImmersion_of_comp_eq_id`, `QuasiFiniteAt ↔` open point of the
  fibre, `IsOpenImmersion.of_isIso_stalkMap`).
- `Scheme.Hom.stalkMapAt f (h : f x = y)` (stalk map between named points) with `_rfl`, `_comp`,
  `_congr`, `_id`, `map_stalkMapAt_maximalIdeal`, `exists_stalkMapAt_eq` (removes most
  `stalkCongr` bookkeeping).

**`CotangentProduct.lean`** (pure algebra, namespace `IsLocalRing`):
- **`sub_mem_maximalIdeal_sq_of_product`**: local rings `R`, `O₁`, `O₂`, maps `φᵢ : Oᵢ → R`, local
  retractions `ψᵢ : R → Oᵢ` (`ψᵢ ∘ φᵢ = id`), cross terms `ψ₁ ∘ φ₂`, `ψ₂ ∘ φ₁` mapping `𝔪` into `𝔪²`,
  `𝔪_R = φ₁(𝔪)R + φ₂(𝔪)R`, residue field of `R` generated by either image. Then
  `x ≡ φ₁(ψ₁ x) + φ₂(ψ₂ x) (mod 𝔪_R²)` for `x ∈ 𝔪_R`, i.e. `𝔪_R/𝔪_R² ≅ 𝔪₁/𝔪₁² ⊕ 𝔪₂/𝔪₂²`.

**`CotangentPullback.lean`** (assembled from the two files above plus `cot_pullback.lean.part`):
- **`cotangent_span_pullback`** (cotangent space of a base change, spanning half): for `z ∈ X ×_S Y`
  with `fst z` rational over `s` and `𝒪_{Y, snd z}` a field (e.g. `Y = Spec L'`, `L'` a field
  extension of `κ(s)`): `𝔪_z/𝔪_z²` is spanned over `𝒪_z` by the classes of `fst^♯ 𝔪_x`, and every
  element of `𝒪_z` acts on it as an element of `𝒪_{Y, snd z}`.
- **`sub_mem_sq_pairPt`** (cotangent space of a product): sections `eX`, `eY` of `f`, `g`, a point `s`
  with `𝒪_{S,s}` a field (`S = Spec k`, or the generic point of an integral base such as
  `Spec 𝓞_L`); at `z = (eX s, eY s)`, with `i₁ = (id, eY ∘ f)`, `i₂ = (eX ∘ g, id)`:
  `c ≡ p₁^♯(i₁^♯ c) + p₂^♯(i₂^♯ c) (mod 𝔪_z²)`; with `iᵢ^♯ ∘ pᵢ^♯ = id` and vanishing cross terms
  this is `𝔪_z/𝔪_z² ≅ 𝔪_x/𝔪_x² ⊕ 𝔪_y/𝔪_y²` (EGA IV 17.2.3-type statement, UNVERIFIED locator).

**`LocalStructure.lean`** (assembled from `StalkPullback.lean` and `local_structure.lean.part`):
- `evalPt p V s` (value of a section at a point `Spec F → X`, same formula as
  `GroupModel.evalAt`), `secOf`, `evalPt_secOf`, `exists_fin_sum_tmul(₂)`,
  `SpecMap_mem_basicOpen_iff`, `exists_chart_point`.
- **`exists_localStructure_pullback`**: for `f : X ⟶ Spec R`, `g : Y ⟶ Spec R`, any
  `W : X ×_R Y ⟶ P`, `s ∈ Γ(P, U)` and `F`-points `p₁`, `p₂` (`F` a field) with `(p₁, p₂) ∈ W⁻¹U`:
  there are opens `V₁ ∋ p₁`, `V₂ ∋ p₂` and sections `a_k, c_k` on `V₁`, `b_k, d_k` on `V₂` such that
  `H = Σ c_k ⊗ d_k` does not vanish at `(p₁, p₂)` and, at every `F`-point of `V₁ × V₂` where `H ≠ 0`,
  the point lies in `W⁻¹U` and `s · H = Σ a_k ⊗ b_k` (chart `Spec (A ⊗[R] B)`, basic open `D(h)`,
  `s = a/hⁿ`, pure tensors evaluate to products).

**`StalkTensor.lean`** (assembled from `StalkPullback.lean` and `stalk_tensor.lean.part`), the
requested lemma in full:
- **`pullback_stalk_tensor_isLocalization`**: for `z ∈ X ×_S Y` over named points `x`, `y`, `s`
  (`f x = s = g y`), the ring map `Φ : 𝒪_{X,x} ⊗_{𝒪_{S,s}} 𝒪_{Y,y} → 𝒪_{X ×_S Y, z}`,
  `Φ(a ⊗ b) = fst^♯ a · snd^♯ b`, is onto up to units and `Φ t = 0 ⇒ u t = 0` for some `u` with
  `Φ u` a unit; **`pullback_stalk_isLocalization`** states this as Mathlib's
  `IsLocalization (Φ⁻¹ 𝔪_z).primeCompl`, i.e. `𝒪_z = (𝒪_x ⊗_{𝒪_s} 𝒪_y)_{Φ⁻¹𝔪_z}` (Stacks 01JT,
  EGA I 3.6.5, UNVERIFIED locators). Kernel half: write `t = (γ u ⊗ γ v)⁻¹ ι(t₀)` with
  `t₀ ∈ A ⊗[R] B` (affine charts), `t₀` dies in `(A ⊗[R] B)_{t₀'}`, so some `w ∉ t₀'` kills it.

**`CotangentBaseChange.lean`** (assembled from `StalkPullback.lean`, `stalk_tensor.lean.part`,
`cot_bc.lean.part`), the second requested lemma in full:
- `IsLocalRing.mem_sq_of_map_mem_sq` (pure algebra: for a localization `φ : B → R` at an ideal `J`
  with `φ(J)R = 𝔪_R` and `B/J` residually a field, `φ p ∈ 𝔪_R² ⇒ p ∈ J²`).
- **`cotangent_baseChange_injective`**: if `𝒪_{S,s}` and `𝒪_{Y,y}` are fields (`S = Spec L`,
  `Y = Spec L'`, or the generic points of `Spec 𝓞_L`, `Spec 𝓞_{L'}`) and `x` is rational over `s`, the
  map `𝒪_y ⊗_{𝒪_s} 𝔪_x/𝔪_x² → 𝔪_z/𝔪_z²`, `c ⊗ da ↦ c · d(fst^♯ a)`, is injective. It is built from
  Mathlib's `Ideal.tensorCotangentHom` (flat base change of `I/I²`) and `pullback_stalk_tensor_isLocalization`.
  With `cotangent_span_pullback` (onto), `T*_z ≅ L' ⊗_L T*_x`.

## 2. How each Prop is proved

- **`CotangentSpanBC`.** `𝒢' ≅ 𝒢 ×_{𝓞_L} 𝓞_{L'}` through `e`; `pullback_stalkGen` transported along
  `e.hom.left` (`StalkGen.comp_of_isIso`); `ξ = e(η)` rational over the generic point (unit section,
  `rationalOver_of_section`); the stalk of `Spec 𝓞_{L'}` at its generic point is a field
  (`Scheme.functionField`), so `𝔪_{ξ'} = π^♯(𝔪_ξ) 𝒪_{ξ'}` by `maximalIdeal_eq_of_stalkGen`. Scalars:
  every `r ∈ 𝒪_{ξ'}` acts as some `b ∈ 𝒪_{Spec 𝓞_{L'}, η}`, `b = a/s` with `s ∈ 𝓞_{L'} ∖ 0`;
  `s ∣ N ∈ ℤ ∖ 0` (`Ideal.under_lt_under_of_integral_mem_sdiff`) and `N` is a unit in both local rings
  (nonzero global functions are units at the generic point, `germ_injective_of_isIntegral`), so
  `r · π^* v = (a s') · π^*(N⁻¹ v)` lies in the `𝓞_{L'}`-span.
- **`UniformizationLiftAnalytic`.** `exists_localStructure_pullback` for `𝒢.G.hom`, `bO L L'`,
  `W = e.inv.left`, at the components `(β Φ' z₀, τ')` of `Φ' z₀`; the second component is constant
  in `z`, so near `z₀` (on the open set where `β Φ' z ∈ V₁(ℂ)` and `H ≠ 0`, open by the hypothesis on
  `β ∘ Φ'`) `s ∘ Φ' = N/H` with `N`, `H` holomorphic (sums of products of functions holomorphic by the
  hypothesis and constants). Openness of `dom` and holomorphy are local.
- **`ProductLocalStructure`.** Immediate from `exists_localStructure_pullback` with `W = e.inv.left`
  (`(lift p₁ p₂ ≫ e.inv).left = pullback.lift p₁.left p₂.left ≫ e.inv.left`, `evalAt = evalPt`).
- **`KerUnitOpenOfCotangentSurj`.** Nakayama (`Submodule.le_of_le_smul_of_le_jacobson_bot`,
  `𝒪_{A,0}` Noetherian): surjective cotangent map gives `𝔪₀ = f^♯(𝔪₀) 𝒪₀`. In
  `A[f] = A ×_{f,A,e} Spec k`, at the unit point, `maximalIdeal_eq_of_stalkGen` (the `Spec k` side is
  rational via the retraction `A → Spec k` of `e`) gives `𝔪 = fst^♯(𝔪₀)𝒪`, and
  `fst^♯ f^♯ = snd^♯ e^♯` kills `𝔪₀` (`𝔪_{Spec k} = 0`), so the local ring is a field; then
  `isOpenImmersion_of_section_of_maximalIdeal_eq_bot`.
- **`CotangentMulN`.** On `A ×_k A` at the origin, `sub_mem_maximalIdeal_sq_of_product` with
  `φᵢ = pᵢ^♯`, `ψ₁ = (id, 0)^♯`, `ψ₂ = (0, id)^♯` gives `μ^♯ a ≡ p₁^♯ a + p₂^♯ a (mod 𝔪²)`; applying
  `(f, g)^♯` gives `cot(f + g) = cot f + cot g` on `End A`; `cot 0 = 0`, `cot 1 = id`; then
  `n = n • 1` (`zsmul_one`) and `map_zsmul`.

## 3. Remaining Props and what each lacks

| Prop | status | missing lemma(s) |

| `OmegaBaseChange` (height-bc BC-omega) | open | (i) conormal base change at closed points, `ker(e'^♯_{s'}) = ker(e^♯_s) · 𝒪_{𝒢', e'(s')}` (provable with `pullback_stalkGen` and the argument of `maximalIdeal_eq_of_stalkGen`, not done); (ii) the local–global lattice step `ω_{𝒢'} = ⋂_{s'} omegaAt' s' ⊆ 𝓞_{L'} · π^*(⋂_s omegaAt s)` (localization of submodules of an `L`-space at primes of a Dedekind domain, and their base change) |
| `ProductHodgeLattice` (metrization) | open | the cotangent splitting `ω_{𝒢P} ≅ ω₁ ⊕ ω₂` is now available in general form (`sub_mem_sq_pairPt` at the generic point of `Spec 𝓞_L`, unit sections) but not yet assembled on `GroupModel`s; still missing: `finrank (hodgeSpace (g₁+g₂)) = 1` (same exterior-power/localization issue as above), the lattice splitting `ω_{𝒢P} = ω_{𝒢₁} ⊕ ω_{𝒢₂}` (conormal of a product at closed points + local–global), and multiplicativity of `secIndex` for tensor products of rank-one lattices |
| `ProductMetrizationOfProductUnif` (product-height S-alg) | open | everything `ProductHodgeLattice` lacks, plus the coefficient/Jacobian compatibilities of the product uniformization |

## 5. Notes for integration

- Each assembled file contains a verbatim copy of the owner's source file, so the proved Props are
  literally the owners' definitions. For `MulIsogeny`, the definition copied (via mul-rigidity's
  `Mul.lean`) is over `ZInQKernel.HDecomp.Objects`' `PkgI3.AbVar`; G0Assemble's copy has the same text

- Lean pitfalls met (recorded in the log): `rw` fails with "motive is not type correct at implicit
  transparency" whenever a point appears as `(f ≫ g) x` versus `g (f x)`, or a domain as
  `(ptC τ).left` versus `Spec ℂ`; use `exact`, `calc`, `h ▸ e` or `Category.assoc` terms instead.
  `Scheme.Hom.stalkMapAt` with `subst` on the point equation avoids most `stalkCongr` algebra.
