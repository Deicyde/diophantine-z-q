# Correcting the dyadic Kato source

**Source correction.** The reading desk found three mismatches in the
earlier Lean transcription of Kato 1999, pp. 314-319, read (AI reading
of the published pages). Theorem 0.8 bounds the kernel of localization
at every prime dividing the base level, its hypothesis (iii) requires
purity, and its torsion conclusion is primewise. The full-open H²
length estimate from the earlier draft is unavailable. These are
errors in the earlier transcription, distinct from the gap in the
1999 application's predecessor at tex 1884–1893 and 7206–7241.
