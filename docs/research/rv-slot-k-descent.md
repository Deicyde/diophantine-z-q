# Referee report on the descent lemma of Route K: the 2-descent behind the slot construction

This report reviews the proof of the descent lemma DL in `docs/research/slot-k.md` §2. It
concentrates on the 2-descent on which that proof rests: Proposition 0 of
`docs/research/descent-alt.md` §4.1, its restatement in slot-k §2.1, and the notion of a
certificate (slot-k §1.2), checked against the use of Kriz's theorem in `docs/research/pc-audit.md`
and `docs/research/rv-route-k.md` §4.3. Every step was rederived from first principles; nothing
labelled VERIFIED in slot-k or descent-alt was taken on trust. The computations below use an
independent exact 2-descent written for this review. It shares no code with the earlier notes.

Each step examined carries one of three marks:

- **CORRECT**: rederived and checked here;
- **GAP**: missing or wrong, with what fails and how to repair it;
- **UNCHECKED**: not examined.

## Verdict

**DL is proved as written.** The 2-descent behind it (Proposition 0) is correct in all four classes
of `d` mod 8. The construction needs only the class `d ≡ 5 (mod 8)`. The certificate is the right
notion: by Kriz's Theorem 10.13 and the corank lemma it yields a rational point with abscissa in
`βℚ*²`. Gluing Lemmas 1 and 2, the base Lemma 3 and steps 0–6 of the theorem were rederived by hand
without finding an error.

The findings are all of minor or cosmetic severity: imprecise citations, one implicit hypothesis, and
an incomplete written proof of the place-2 rows in descent-alt. None affects the validity of DL. The
repairs are listed in §5. They are recommended before the argument enters the manuscript or the
Lean hypothesis CR₃.

Computational evidence (§4), all with the independent descent:

- Proposition 0 agrees with the point-based descent on 30 random curves covering all four classes
  mod 8: the same Selmer space and the same first-coordinate projection. PARI's `ellrank` agrees on
  the Selmer rank of the 10 curves it was run on.
- The slot construction, implemented independently from the text of slot-k §2.4, was run on 30
  admissible inputs. Both curves `C_n` and `C_{nθ}` were certified (`dim Sel₂ = 3` and
  `β ∈ π₁`) in every case.
- With the wrong sign choice the descent detects that `β` drops out of `π₁(Sel₂(C_{nθ}))`.
  This shows that the test can fail.

## 1. The 2-descent from first principles

Throughout, `d > 0` is odd and squarefree with prime set `S`, and `C_d : y² = x(x − d)(x + d)`.
Put `e₁ = 0`, `e₂ = d`, `e₃ = −d`.

### 1.1 The Kummer map and the global group (CORRECT)

The curve has full rational 2-torsion. The complete 2-descent (Silverman, *The Arithmetic of
Elliptic Curves*, X.1.4) identifies `H¹(ℚ, E[2])` with `(ℚ*/ℚ*²)²` by `P ↦ (x − e₁, x − e₂) = (x, x − d)`,
with the usual values at the 2-torsion points:

- `(0, 0) ↦ ((e₁ − e₂)(e₁ − e₃), e₁ − e₂) = (−d², −d) ≡ (−1, −d)`;
- `(d, 0) ↦ (e₂ − e₁, (e₂ − e₁)(e₂ − e₃)) = (d, 2d²) ≡ (d, 2)`;
- `(−d, 0) ↦ (−d, −2d)`.

These agree with slot-k's notation. The Selmer group lies in `ℚ(S ∪ {2}, 2)²`, where
`ℚ(S ∪ {2}, 2)` is generated by `−1`, `2` and the primes of `d`. Two facts are needed:

- (i) at an odd `p ∤ d` the curve has good reduction, so the local image is the unramified subgroup.
  So no Selmer class is ramified at `p`;
- (ii) conversely, every element of `ℚ(S ∪ {2}, 2)²` is a unit pair at such `p`, hence satisfies the
  local condition there.

slot-k §2.1 states only (ii); (i) is the standard half of X.1.4. Write
`a = (−1)^{a₀}2^{a₁}Π_{x∈S} x^{u_x}` and `b = (−1)^{b₀}2^{b₁}Π_{x∈S} x^{v_x}`, as in slot-k. Then
`Sel₂(C_d)` is the set of `(a, b)` that satisfy the local conditions at `∞`, at 2 and at the primes
of `d`.

### 1.2 The odd primes of `d` (CORRECT)

Let `p | d` and `d_p = d/p`. Since `p` is odd, `#E(ℚ_p)/2E(ℚ_p) = #E(ℚ_p)[2] = 4`. Write classes in
`ℚ_p*/ℚ_p*²` as (valuation mod 2, residue bit of the unit part). The torsion images `(d, 2)` and
`(−1, −d)` have valuation vectors `(1, 0)` and `(0, 1)`. So they are independent, and the local image
is their span: `{(1, 1), (d, 2), (−1, −d), (−d, −2d)}`.

A pair `(a, b)` with valuations `(u_p, v_p)` at `p` therefore lies in the local image if and only if
it equals `u_p(d, 2) + v_p(−1, −d)`. That is, the unit parts must have the residue bits
`u_p[d_p/p] + v_p λ_p` (for `a`) and `u_p μ_p + v_p[−d_p/p]` (for `b`). The unit part of `a` at `p`
is `(−1)^{a₀}2^{a₁}Π_{y≠p} y^{u_y}`. Its residue bit is `a₀λ_p + a₁μ_p + Σ_{y≠p} u_y[y/p]`. With
`[d_p/p] = Σ_{y≠p}[y/p]` the condition becomes

```
Σ_{y≠p} [y/p](u_y + u_p) + λ_p v_p = a₀λ_p + a₁μ_p,
```

which is slot-k's `(E1_p)`. In the same way the condition on `b` becomes

```
μ_p u_p + Σ_{y≠p} [y/p](v_y + v_p) + λ_p v_p = b₀λ_p + b₁μ_p,
```

which is `(E2_p)`. In descent-alt's matrix notation these are `Au + D_λv = a₀λ + a₁μ` and
`D_μu + (A + D_λ)v = b₀λ + b₁μ`, with `A = L + diag(L𝟏)`. They agree.

### 1.3 The real place (CORRECT)

`E(ℝ)` has two components, since all three roots are real, so `#E(ℝ)/2E(ℝ) = 2`. Points on the
identity component have `x > d`, with class `(+, +)`. Points on the other component have
`−d < x < 0`, with class `(−, −)`. The image is `{(+,+), (−,−)}`, and the condition is `a₀ = b₀`,
which is row `(R)`.
