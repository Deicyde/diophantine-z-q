## 3. The leaf: which clauses are printed, and why the U form is never stronger

`ShimuraMainThmIDeligneY3AbU := ∃ Y Φ, R1fpPropsAb Y Φ ∧ ∃ Ψ : ℍ → (B⊗𝔸_f)^× → Y(ℂ), DeligneBodyAt Y Φ Ψ ∧
ModuliAtUnits Y Φ Ψ`. Its clauses, and the printed statement each one comes from (all locators UNVERIFIED):

| clause | content | printed source |
|---|---|---|
| (R) `R1fpPropsAb Y Φ` | `Y` is a proper smooth curve over `ℚ` and the fine moduli scheme of abelian surfaces with `𝒪`-action and full level-3 structure (universal family; universal property for families over proper smooth curves; naturality of `Φ`) | Representability of the PEL moduli problem (Mumford, GIT, Thm 7.9 with level `≥ 3`); properness because `B` is a division algebra (Shimura 1967; Milne §§5-8). This is the same clause list as the kernel's leaves `deligneComponentsY3PrintedAb`, `r1r2Ab` and `gonzalezRotgerX6Ab`, all on `R1fpPropsAb`. |
| 1 | `Ψ` is onto, with fibres the `DCRel` classes: `B^×_+\(ℍ × (B⊗𝔸_f)^×)/K₃ ≃ Y(ℂ)` | Deligne, Sém. Bourbaki 389 (1971), §4: the moduli interpretation of `Sh_K(G, X)` for PEL data, here `G = B^×`. Also Milne, *Introduction to Shimura varieties*, §8. |
| 2 | `τ ↦ Ψ(τ, g)` is holomorphic | the same theorem: the bijection in 1 is an isomorphism of complex analytic spaces |
| 3 | moduli clause at `g = 1`: the object at `Ψ(τ, 1)` is `ℂ²/ι(𝒪)(τ,1)ᵀ` with its `ι` | the same moduli interpretation, at `[τ, 1]` |
| U (`ModuliAtUnits`) | the same at every `u ∈ 𝒪̂^×`, as a QM pair only | the same, at `[τ, u]`: `L_u = B ∩ 𝒪̂u⁻¹ = 𝒪` |
| 4 | reciprocity: `σ Ψ(z, g) = Ψ(z, q̂(s)^ε g)` at CM points, with signs `ε, δ` existential | Shimura, Ann. of Math. 85 (1967), Main Theorem I. Deligne 1971, §§2-3, states it as the defining property of the canonical model, and §4 shows that the moduli scheme is that model. |

**Why the U form is never stronger than the print** (SPECULATIVE; standard reasons, rayclass-cft §2 and its
review):
* Every clause is weaker than the printed statement under the reading "`Y₃` is `Sh_{K₃}(B^×, ℍ^±)` over `ℚ`" (the
  same reading as the leaf `deligneComponentsY3PrintedAb`):
  * the biholomorphism is replaced by "bijective, and holomorphic in `τ`";
  * the moduli clause is kept only at `g ∈ 𝒪̂^×`, and describes only the QM pair, not the level structure;
  * the reciprocity conventions are existential;
  * the field-of-moduli clause is dropped.
* **Clause U.** In Deligne's moduli description, `[τ, g]` is `(ℂ²/ι(L_g)(τ,1)ᵀ, ι, η_g)` with `L_g = B ∩ 𝒪̂g⁻¹`,
  and `L_u = 𝒪` for `u ∈ 𝒪̂^×`.
* **Consistency with clause 1.** `L_{γgk} = L_g γ⁻¹`, and `ι(ℓγ⁻¹)(γτ, 1)ᵀ` is a scalar multiple of
  `ι(ℓ)(τ, 1)ᵀ`. rayclass-cft's review checked that no convention makes U false while clause 3 holds.
* **The added content is real.** U is not implied by clause 3: rayclass-cft §3 shows that the points `Ψ(τ, 1)`
  fill only one of the two geometric components of `Y₃`. So U adds the moduli clause on the second component,
  which the print also gives.

**Composite, not one print.** Clause 3 together with (R) is H-154's `ShimuraHolFamily` (the shimura-cft review's
point). The statement as a whole combines three printed results:
1. the representability theorem (R);
2. Deligne's complex moduli interpretation (1, 2, 3, U);
3. the canonical-model property, i.e. Shimura's reciprocity law (4).

Deligne 1971 §4 may state 2 and 3 as one theorem ("the PEL moduli scheme is a canonical model"; UNVERIFIED). Even
then, (R) is a separate representability input.

**Why it is one Prop and not three.** The clauses share the witness `(Y, Φ)` and the map `Ψ`. A split such as
"for every witness and every `Ψ` satisfying 1-3 and U, clause 4 holds" would be **false** (SPECULATIVE, a
standard construction). Take `k ∈ 𝒪̂^×` and set `Ψ'(τ, g) = Ψ(τ, g)` when `nrd g` lies in the class of `1` in
`π₀ = (ℤ/3)^×`, and `Ψ'(τ, g) = Ψ(τ, gk)` on the other class.
* `Ψ'` still satisfies 1-3 and U: `DCRel` preserves the class of `nrd g`, and `K₃` is normal in `𝒪̂^×`.
* It breaks clause 4 at every `σ` that swaps the two components.

A right translation applied uniformly on both components would not break clause 4, because it commutes with the
left action of `q̂(s)`. So reciprocity holds only for the normalized `Ψ`, and the shared existential is needed.

So the spec enters the new field with kind `composite`.

## 4. Class number one from the chain (VERIFIED, compiled)

`ClassNumberOneB : ∀ g ∈ (B⊗𝔸_f)^×, ∃ γ ∈ B, nrd γ ≠ 0, ∃ u ∈ 𝒪̂^×, g = γu`. This is rayclass-cft's statement,
unchanged; only its docstring now says GLUE, proved. Proof (`classNumberOneB_proof`, about 120 lines):
1. **Denominators.**
   * `exists_den`: every finite adele `a` has `d ≥ 1` with `d·a ∈ ℤ̂`. The proof uses Mathlib's
     `mul_nonZeroDivisor_mem_adicCompletionIntegers` at the finitely many bad places, then the product of the
     denominators, converted to `ℤ` through `Rat.ringOfIntegersEquiv`.
   * Coordinatewise this gives `D` with `D g, D g⁻¹ ∈ 𝒪̂` (`exists_den_B`).
2. **The ideal.**
   * `I = {x ∈ B : bEmb(x) g ∈ 𝒪̂}` is an additive subgroup and is stable under left multiplication by `𝒪`.
   * `D·I ⊆ 𝒪`, using `D g⁻¹ ∈ 𝒪̂` and the integrality test `inO_of_bEmb_mem`.
   * The chain's `principal_of_lattice` gives `I = 𝒪α`.
   * `α ≠ 0`, because `D ∈ I`.
3. **Approximation.**
   * `exists_int_approx`: if `a ∈ ℤ̂` and `N ≥ 1`, then `a = m + N b` with `m ∈ ℤ` and `b ∈ ℤ̂`.
   * At each prime `p | N`, Mathlib's `PadicInt.appr` (through `padicIntEquiv`) gives a residue modulo
     `p^{v_p(N)}`. The Chinese remainder theorem `Ideal.exists_forall_sub_mem_ideal` combines them into `m`.
   * Then `N | (a_p − m)` in `ℤ_p` at every `p` (`padic_div`), so `b = (a − m)/N` is integral.
   * Apply this to the coordinates of `D g⁻¹`, with `N = D²c`, where `c = |nrd(Dα)|` satisfies `c α⁻¹ ∈ 𝒪`. The
     result is `x₀ = m/D ∈ B` with `bEmb(x₀) = g⁻¹ − Dc·w`, `w ∈ 𝒪̂`.
4. **The unit.**
   * `x₀ g = 1 − c·w·(Dg) ∈ 𝒪̂`, so `x₀ ∈ I`, and `x₀ = qα` with `q ∈ 𝒪`.
   * `u = α g ∈ 𝒪̂`, because `α ∈ I`.
   * `u⁻¹ = g⁻¹α⁻¹ = q + D·w·(cα⁻¹) ∈ 𝒪̂`.
   * So `g = α⁻¹ u`, with `γ = α⁻¹` and `nrd γ ≠ 0`.
