**ON THE SPOT (c).** The affine complement construction works without a torsion group scheme. An $N$-torsion abscissa polynomial has simple roots when $N$ is a unit. Divisor-level roots are included by multiplication in the point group. For the quadratic chart an even divisor shares the two-torsion factor, which cancels, and an odd divisor has no nonidentity point of order 2. Monic descent then gives a polynomial identity over the universal domain, hence over arbitrary bases. A surjection of étale algebras is also étale over its source, and its closed immersion is open, giving the kernel idempotent. The product of these idempotents is the locus where none of the proper-divisor equations holds.

**ON THE SPOT (c).** For the p-free quotient at fixed $N$, the prime exclusion supplies the inverse of $\lvert H\rvert$. The geometric quotient-point argument is the composition of finite-group orbits of generators with scaling orbits of short equations. This construction and the short chart stabilizer calculation supply the geometric roadmap at tex 8470–8479. There are no printed-form leaves in this work.
