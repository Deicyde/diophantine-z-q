## The proof (all steps compiled)

Notation: `g : X ⟶ Spec ℚ`, `Ω = g.relativeDifferentials`, `e : K(X) ≃+* F`, and for a nonempty open `U`,
`ψ U : Γ(X, U) →+* F` the germ at the generic point followed by `e`.

| step | Lean | content |
|---|---|---|
| (a) | `Φ`, `Φ_smul_D`, `induction_affine` | for `U` affine nonempty, `Φ U : Γ(Ω, U) →+ Ω[F⁄ℚ]` is `kaehlerSectionsEquiv⁻¹` followed by `KaehlerDifferential.map ℚ ℚ Γ(U) F`; `Φ U (a • d b) = ψ a • D (ψ b)`; sections over affines are generated by the `a • d b` |
| (b) | `kaehlerToFractionFieldInjective`, `smooth_sections`, `isFractionRing_ψ`, `Φ_injective` | `Ω_{A/ℚ} → Ω_{K/ℚ}` is injective for a smooth domain `A` with fraction field `K` (`Ω_A` projective hence torsion-free; `Ω_K` is its localization, `KaehlerDifferential.isLocalizedModule_map`); `Γ(U)` is smooth over `ℚ` (`HasRingHomProperty.appLE`) with fraction field `F` |
| (c) | `Φ_res`, `Φ_eq_of_affine`, `ΦG`, `ΦG_eq` | restriction compatibility; the global `ΦG : Γ(Ω, ⊤) →+ Ω[F⁄ℚ]` agrees with `Φ V` on every affine `V` (two nonempty opens share the generic point) |
| (d) | `eq_of_affine_restrict`, `ΦG_injective`, `exists_glue` | sheaf locality and gluing of `Ω` along affine opens (`TopCat.Sheaf.eq_of_locally_eq'`, `existsUnique_gluing'`) |
| (e) | `valuationCenter`, `Φ_mem_regularAt`, `ΦG_mem_holo` | image ⊆ `holoDiff F`: a valuation subring `w ⊇ ℚ` of `K(X)` gives a valuative square; the lift `Spec w → X` sends the closed point into an affine `V`, whence `germ(Γ(V)) ⊆ w` (Mathlib `UniversallyClosed.eq_valuativeCriterion`) |
| (f) | `isClosed_fromSpec`, `valuationRing_stalk`, `imageVS`, `key_identity`, `mem_range_Φ_of_regular` | local–global: for `𝔪 ⊂ Γ(V)` maximal the point is closed (`X` Jacobson), its stalk is a valuation ring (regular of dimension one), its image in `F` is a valuation subring `w_𝔪 ⊇ ℚ`; `ω ∈ regularAt F w_𝔪` gives `t ∉ 𝔪` with `ψ t • ω ∈ im Φ V` (`(s u²) • (c • D d) = a • (u • D b − b • D u)` for `c = a/s`, `d = b/u`); so the ideal `{t | ψ t • ω ∈ im Φ V}` is the unit ideal |
| (g) | `mem_range_ΦG_of_regular` | the local preimages agree on overlaps (injectivity of `Φ W`) and glue to a global section |
| (h) | `h0DifferentialsEqHolo` | `H⁰ = Γ` (`H.zeroLinearEquiv`), `ℚ`-linearity from `map_rat_smul`; `finrankH ℚ 0 = finrank ℚ (holoDiff F) = fieldGenus F` |

Compared with modular-split's outline (a)–(e) (section 3.4): the projective rank-one local–global principle is
replaced by the elementary ideal argument in (f), and `regularAt` is used directly (no identification with the
image of `Ω_{𝒪_x}` is needed). modular-split's estimate of "well over 1000 lines" turned out to be about 500
lines of proof.

## Statement remarks

* The statement is correct as written (it is now a theorem) and not vacuous: its hypotheses are satisfied by
  any smooth proper geometrically connected curve over `ℚ`, and with the library's Serre duality the left side
  is the arithmetic genus.
* The proof does not use geometric connectedness, and it uses properness only in step (e).
* The docstring of the copied `def` still says "GLUE (named, unproved)", because the copy is verbatim; the
  theorem `h0DifferentialsEqHolo` is the proof.
