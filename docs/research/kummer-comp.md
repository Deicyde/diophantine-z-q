## 3. The proof

The statement: let `E/ℚ(X)` be finite, `B` the integral closure of `ℚ[X]` in `E`, and
`r = P/Q ∈ ℚ(s)` non-constant with `Q(s₀) ≠ 0` and `r(s₀) = t`. Let `𝔓` be a prime of `B` over
`X − t` and `φ : B/𝔓 → ℚ̄`. Then there are:
- a compositum `E' = E(s)` along `X ↦ r` (`KummerShape`), with `[E' : ℚ(s)] ≤ [E : ℚ(X)]`;
- a prime `𝔓'` of the integral closure `B'` of `ℚ[s]` in `E'` over `s − s₀`;
- `φ' : B'/𝔓' → ℚ̄` with `ℚ(φ(B/𝔓)) ⊆ ℚ(φ'(B'/𝔓'))`.

The Lean proof follows ts-kummer's §4 plan, with `O = ℚ[s][1/Q]` (`Localization.Away Q`) in place
of the local ring at `s₀`.

### 3.1 Abstract core: `compositum_core`

Hypotheses:
- `A`, `B`, `O` domains; `B` free and integral over `A`;
- `f : A → O` injective;
- `α : O → F`, `β : B → F` maps to a domain `F` with `α ∘ f = β ∘ algebraMap` and `β` surjective.

Conclusion: there are
- a field `E'` with `O ↪ E'` and `h : B ↪ E'` compatible with `f`, and generated as a field by
  the two images;
- a prime `𝔐` of `integralClosure O E'` with `𝔐 ∩ O = ker α`;
- an injective `ψ : F → integralClosure O E' ⧸ 𝔐` with `ψ ∘ α = mk ∘ algebraMap`.

Proof:
1. Let `T = O ⊗_A B` and `χ : T → F`, `o ⊗ b ↦ α(o)β(b)`. Then `χ` is surjective and
   `N = ker χ` is prime.
2. Take a minimal prime `𝔫 ≤ N` (`Ideal.exists_minimalPrimes_le`). `T` is `O`-free, so nonzero
   elements of `O` are non-zero-divisors of `T`, and therefore lie outside `𝔫`
   (`notMem_nonZeroDivisors_of_mem_mem_minimalPrimes`).
3. Set `E' = Frac(T/𝔫)`.
   - `B → E'` is injective: a nonzero `b` divides a nonzero element of `A`
     (`Ideal.under_ne_bot_of_integral_mem`).
   - The image of `T` is generated by `O` and `B`, and is integral over `O`.
4. Lying over (`Ideal.exists_ideal_over_prime_of_isIntegral`) for `T → integralClosure O E'`
   gives `𝔐` with `𝔐 ∩ T = N`.
5. `ψ = (T/N → C/𝔐) ∘ (T/N ≅ F)⁻¹`. It is injective (`Ideal.quotientMap_injective'`).

### 3.2 Degree bound: `compositum_finrank`

Let `ι : E → E'` carry `K` into `K'` along `σ`, and let `E'` be generated by `ι(E)` and `K'`.
Take a primitive element `a` of `E/K`.
- `ι a` is a root of `(minpoly K a).map σ`.
- `K'⟮ι a⟯` contains `ι(E)` and `K'`, so it is `⊤`.
- Hence `[E' : K'] = deg minpoly_{K'}(ι a) ≤ deg minpoly_K(a) = [E : K]`.

### 3.3 The concrete statement: `kummerCompositum`

1. `f : ℚ[X] → O`, `X ↦ P/Q`, is injective because `P/Q` is transcendental over `ℚ`
   (`RatFunc.transcendental_of_ne_C`; non-constant by hypothesis).
2. `F = B/𝔓`, `β = mk`, and `α` is evaluation at `s₀` followed by `ℚ → B/𝔓`. They are compatible
   because `a − a(t) ∈ (X − t) = 𝔓 ∩ ℚ[X]`.
3. Apply `compositum_core`. Then:
   - `E'` gets its `s`-side `ℚ[X]`-algebra through `O`, and its `ℚ(s)`-algebra by
     `IsFractionRing.lift`;
   - `ι = IsFractionRing.lift h`;
   - `σ : ℚ(X) → ℚ(s)` lifts `X ↦ P/Q`.
4. `KummerShape`:
   - `ι(X) = P/Q`, from `rO · Q = P` in `O`;
   - generation: `O`'s image lies in the subfield generated by `s`, since `O`'s elements are
     `p/Q^k`.
5. `𝔓' = 𝔐 ∩ B'`. Its contraction to `ℚ[s]` is `ker(eval_{s₀}) = (s − s₀)`.
6. `φ' = φ'' ∘ (B'/𝔓' → C/𝔐)`, where `φ''` extends `φ` along `ψ` (`IsAlgClosed.lift`).
   `C/𝔐` is integral over `F`, because `C = integralClosure O E'` and `ψ ∘ α = mk ∘ algebraMap`.
7. `range φ ⊆ ℚ(range φ')`. Each `c ∈ C` has `Q^k c ∈ B'`
   (`IsIntegral.exists_multiple_integral_of_isLocalization`), and `Q^k ≡ Q(s₀)^k ∈ ℚ^×` mod `𝔐`.
   So `φ''[c] = Q(s₀)^{-k} φ'[Q^k c]`.

The residue-field identification that ts-kummer's plan listed as an obstacle is not needed in
this form. Step 7 shows that `C/𝔐` is generated over `ℚ` by the image of `B'/𝔓'`, which is
enough for the `IntermediateField.adjoin` conclusion.
