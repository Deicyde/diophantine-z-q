# CM field of definition on the normalized \(X_0(N)\)

**Scoped stop.** No proved theorem or allowed rated print in the tree identifies the singular moduli of an imaginary quadratic order with elements of Tau Ceti's ring class field. `PreparedParam.CMFieldOfDefinition (X0Branch.model N)` remains open. The closest statement, `MainTheoremCMj`, is an assumed textbook statement in the archived modular-function scout. The existing idelic reduction also assumes CM reciprocity, sourced to the excluded Shimura 1971 fallback. No Lean declaration, hypothesis, or print was added.

There is a second gap in the suggested argument: the two \(j\)-coordinates generate the **function field**, but can coincide at distinct points of its **normalization**, including points satisfying the target's Heegner and coprimality conditions. Ring class field membership for the pair would descend the plane coordinates. The chosen normalized branch also needs a descent argument.

## Smallest missing statements and what they would supply

Fix an imaginary quadratic field \(K\), an order \(O\), and a complex embedding \(\kappa\) of \(K\). Write \(H=\mathrm{ringClassField}(O)\). The arithmetic comparison needed for the two coordinates is:

> There is an embedding \(\iota:H\hookrightarrow\mathbb C\) over \(\kappa\) such that for every invertible proper fractional \(O\)-ideal \(\mathfrak a\), \(j(\mathbb C/\kappa(\mathfrak a))\) lies in \(\iota(H)\).

Use one embedding for all the ideals. This is only the membership clause; the full relation would also send \(j(\mathfrak a)\) to \(j(\mathfrak b^{-1}\mathfrak a)\) via `ringClassArtinEquiv O` with arithmetic Frobenius. Generation, degree and equivariance are not needed to descend the two coordinates. Conversely, a statement just about the principal \(j(O)\) without the other classes or a reciprocity theorem does not supply the two values for arbitrary forms. This is a substantial part of classical CM theory even after the class field and lattice dictionary; no attempt at a fresh proof was started.

The lattice calculation (on the spot, provenance (c)) is small. If \(\tau\) comes from \([A,B,C]\), \(N\tau\) has primitive equation \([A/N,B,NC]\) with discriminant \(d\). A common prime divisor not dividing \(N\) contradicts primitivity of the original form. A common prime dividing \(N\) would divide \(B\), hence \(d=B^2-4AC\), contrary to \((d,N)=1\). Put \(\omega=(-B+\sqrt d)/2\). Up to homothety the two lattices are \((A,\omega)\) and \((A/N,\omega)\) in the same order \(\mathbb Z[\omega]\). The oriented ideal \(\mathfrak n=(N,\omega)\) has norm \(N\) and is invertible since \((N,d)=1\). It satisfies \(\mathfrak n(A/N,\omega)=(A,\omega)\): its products give \(A,N\omega,(A/N)\omega,-B\omega-AC\), and \((N,B)=1\) then gives \(\omega\). Thus the scaled lattice represents the \(\mathfrak n^{-1}\) translate. This is a paper calculation, with no Lean theorem claimed.

For the branch comparison the exact missing condition, after the arithmetic input, is that every automorphism of \(\mathbb C\) over \(\iota(H)\) fixes the **chosen point on the normalization** above the descended plane pair. Equivalently the evaluation of each regular section of the finite \(j\)-chart at the chosen point lies in \(\iota(H)\). The finite chart is affine and lies over the affine \(j\)-line; this last condition would let us factor its evaluation map through \(H\), produce the point over \(H\), and match base change with the chart. The identifications of the two generator germs are already `X0Model.fieldGerm_j` and `fieldGerm_jN`; `X0Model2.complexPoint_jProjection` and `X0Branch.affineEval_factor` / `affineEval_injective` give the projection and affine point identities. One route to the fixedness would be to descend the distinguished subgroup \(E[\mathfrak n]\) using the order action and compare it with the normalized \(X_0(N)\) chart. This needs the arithmetic order action on the model and the rational moduli-to-chart comparison. It is a separate, medium to large step, and the proposed route is (c). The existing normalized-branch comparison compares complex germs; it is not a Galois comparison.

The exact obstruction to arguing only from field generation can already be seen with \(N=2,d=-7\). Set \(\tau_\pm=(\mp1+i\sqrt7)/4\), the roots of \([2,\pm1,1]\). Then \(2\tau_-=2\tau_++1\) and \(-1/\tau_\pm=2\tau_\mp\), so all four \(j\)-values coincide. But the middle coefficient of a primitive form of even leading coefficient is invariant modulo \(4\) under \(\Gamma_0(2)\): for a determinant-one substitution with lower-left entry \(\gamma\) even, its coefficient is \(2A\alpha\beta+B(\alpha\delta+\beta\gamma)+2C\gamma\delta\equiv B\bmod4\). The two coefficients \(1,-1\) differ modulo \(4\), so `X0Branch.complexPoint_eq_iff` separates the points. This is an on-the-spot check, not a counterexample to class field descent. At an isolated smooth plane point the normalization step can instead use a local presentation with denominators nonzero there. A proof for all levels cannot assume such isolation.
