## 3. The glue Props

### 3.1 `HHprimeFrobSq` (residue degree `≤ 2`): PROVED in §8–§9; the original sketch, kept for reference

Let `p` be odd, `p ∤ n`, and inert in every `K_{d₀} = ℚ(√−d₀)` with `d₀ ∣ n`, `d₀ ≡ 5 (mod 8)`.

1. In `K_{d₀}`, `p𝓞_K` is prime and generated by `p ∈ ℤ ⊂ 𝒪_{d₀,4} = ℤ + 4𝓞_K`, and it is prime to the
   conductor. Its class in `Pic(𝒪_{d₀,4})` is trivial, so it splits completely in `H_{d₀,4}/K_{d₀}`
   (Artin reciprocity for ring class fields; Tau Ceti `ringClassArtinEquiv` and the splitting law
   `RingClass.mem_splitSet_iff`). Primes of `H_{d₀,4}` over `p` therefore have residue degree `2`, and so
   do primes of `H'_{d₀}`.
2. On `L_n(i) = ℚ(i, √q : q ∣ n)`, which is multiquadratic, Frobenius has order `≤ 2`.
3. `p` is unramified in each factor: `H'_{d₀}/K_{d₀}` is unramified outside `2`, and `p ∤ 2n`. Each
   factor is normal over `ℚ` (`normal_Hprimebar`, `Lni` normal), so `Gal(ℍ'_n/ℚ)` embeds into the
   product of the factors' Galois groups. Frobenius at `𝔓` therefore has order `≤ 2`, and
   `𝓞/𝔓 ⊆ F_{p²}`.

The Lean proof (§8, §9) follows this sketch. It uses Frobenius elements instead of residue-field
composita, and the fundamental identity instead of an explicit unramifiedness argument.

### 3.2 `SqrtTwoNotInHHprime` (`√2 ∉ ℍ'_n`)

TYZ prove this as Step 3 of Lemma `torsion2`. The statement is read from the notes (rv-nt-tyz.md B4,
cm-bridge.md §4), not from the source; HUMAN CHECK.

**Proved here for one factor.** `sqrtTwo_not_mem_HHprime_of_single` covers `n` whose only divisor
`d₀ ≡ 5 (mod 8)` is `n` itself, e.g. `n` prime or `n = 21`. Then `ℍ'_n = H'_n` and ring-class's
`not_adjSqrt_two_le_Hprime` applies after transport through `embed n`. So `lemma318_at_single` proves
Lemma `torsion2` at such `n` from the leaf alone.

**Why the compositum needs class field theory.** Put `M = ℍ'_n(√2) = ⨆ H_{d₀,4}` (`Hprime_sup_sqrt_two`).
The required element `τ ∈ Gal(M/L_n(i))` must restrict to `σ_{1+2ϖ}` on every `H_{d₀,4}` at once. The
fields `H_{d₀,4}` can share subfields beyond `L_n(i)(√2)`, so these restrictions have to be compatible
there. Group theory alone cannot see this, and a Chebotarev argument is circular, since the needed
prime exists iff `√2 ∉ ℍ'_n`. What makes the `σ_{d₀}` compatible is that they are norms of one
element of `L_n(i)`. Two routes follow.

**Route A (global, recommended for formalization; SPECULATIVE until reviewed, numerically checked).**
Let `F = L_n(i) = ℚ(i, √q* : q ∣ n)`, `q* = ±q ≡ 1 (mod 4)`, so that `∏_{q ∣ d₀} q* = d₀` and
`√−d₀ = i·∏_{q ∣ d₀} √q*`.
1. *An explicit element.* Put `β = i·∏_{q ∣ n} (1 + √q*)/2 ∈ 𝓞_F` and `α = 1 + 2β`.
   * `Gal(F/K_{d₀})` is the group of sign changes `(i, √q_j*) ↦ (±i, ±√q_j*)` that fix `√−d₀`.
     Summing over it, with `θ_j + θ_j' = 1` and `θ_j − θ_j' = √q_j*`, gives
     `Tr_{F/K_{d₀}}(β) = i·∏_{q∣d₀} √q* = √−d₀`.
   * Since `∏(1 + 2x_g) ≡ 1 + 2Σx_g (mod 4)`, `N_{F/K_{d₀}}(α) ≡ 1 + 2√−d₀ ≡ −(3 + 2√−d₀) (mod 4𝓞_{K_{d₀}})`.
   * Hence `N(α)·(3 − 2√−d₀) ≡ −(9 + 4d₀) (mod 4)` lies in `ℤ + 4𝓞_{K_{d₀}}`, and the class of
     `N_{F/K_{d₀}}(α)𝓞_K` in `Pic(ℤ + 4𝓞_{K_{d₀}})` is `σ_{d₀}`, using `σ² = 1` and `σ = [(3 − 2√−d₀)]⁻¹`.
     This holds for every `d₀` at once.
   * VERIFIED by exact computation for 8 values of `n` and 15 pairs `(n, d₀)`, including
     `n = 1365 = 3·5·7·13` (`scratch/tyz-318/trace_check.py`).
2. *A prime of `F` in the class of `α`.* Take a prime `𝔓` of `𝓞_F`, prime to `2n·N(α)`, with
   `[𝔓] = [α𝓞_F]` in `Pic(ℤ + 4𝓞_F)`. It exists by Tau Ceti's Chebotarev density for
   `ringClassField (conductorOrder F 4)`; this is ring-class's `exists_prime_picClass_eq` with `K_d`
   replaced by `F`.
3. *Norms.* `N_{F/K_{d₀}}` maps principal ideals with generator in `ℤ + 4𝓞_F` to ones with generator in
   `ℤ + 4𝓞_K`. Hence `[N_{F/K_{d₀}}𝔓] = [N(α)𝓞_K] = σ_{d₀}`, with `N_{F/K_{d₀}}𝔓 = 𝔭^f`.
4. *Frobenius.* Let `h ∈ Gal(ℚ̄/F)` be a Frobenius at a prime above `𝔓`. On `H_{d₀,4}` it is an
   arithmetic Frobenius with exponent `N𝔓 = N𝔭^f`. So is `Art([𝔭])^f`, by Tau Ceti's
   `isArithFrobAt_ringClassArtinEquiv_picClass`. As `𝔭` is unramified, they are equal:
   `h|_{H_{d₀,4}} = Art([𝔭^f]) = σ_{d₀}`, transported as in §9.
5. *Conclusion.* `h` fixes `L_n(i)` and every `H'_{d₀}`, so it fixes `ℍ'_n`. But
   `h(√2) = σ_{d₀}(√2) = −√2`, since `√2 ∉ H'_{d₀}` is the fixed field of `σ_{d₀}`. Hence `√2 ∉ ℍ'_n`.

Route A uses only Tau Ceti's proved reciprocity at primes, Chebotarev, and elementary algebra in the
multiquadratic field `F`, which is the bulk of the work. SPECULATIVE estimate: one or two tasks.

**Route B (local class field theory; the first sketch, AI review: sound).**
* `L_n(i) ⊗ ℚ₂ = ℚ₂(ζ₁₂)` at every place: some `q* ≡ 5 (mod 8)`, via `√n` itself, gives `ℚ₂(√−3)`.
* Every `K_{d₀} ⊗ ℚ₂` is the same field `ℚ₂(√3)`, since `−d₀/3 ≡ 1 (mod 8)`. Its integers are
  `ℤ₂[√3] = ℤ₂[√−d₀]`.
* The classes of `3 + 2√−d₀ = 3 + 2u√3` (`u` odd) modulo `(ℤ₂ + 4𝓞)^×` coincide:
  `(3 − 2√3)/(3 + 2√3) = −7 + 4√3 ∈ 1 + 4𝓞`.
* `ℚ₂(ζ₁₂)/ℚ₂(√3)` is unramified, so the local norm is onto on units. Take a local unit `x` with norm
  `3 + 2√3`. By norm functoriality of the Artin map, `Art_{L_n(i)}(x)` restricts to
  `σ_{1+2ϖ}^{±1} = σ_{1+2ϖ}` on every `H_{d₀,4}`.
* This needs idelic Artin maps with base-change functoriality, which are not in Tau Ceti as vendored.
  It also needs the transport of Artin symbols through `embed d₀`; that is fine because
  `H_{d₀,4}/ℚ` is Galois.
