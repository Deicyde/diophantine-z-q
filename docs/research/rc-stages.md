## Exact reduction identity

For a selected datum `dat : HeegnerDatum E f Lat κ (Dw * hstar) 1 hstar`, let `P = genusPoint dat`, let (Q) be the rational point of (A=E^{(D_w)}) corresponding to (LP), and let (d) be a compatible tower of divisions of (Q). At stage (i), use the same place that exhibits the chosen (g_i) as arithmetic Frobenius, and write (M=M_i). The statement required of the existing stage-map interface is
```text
ev i Dw dat (L • genusPoint dat) =
  b_M (proj M (c_Q (g_i * g_i)))   in ZMod (2 ^ M).
```

Here b_M(proj M t) is b(t) reduced modulo 2^M. The left side is a `StageKummer` evaluation in the ring class field of the datum; it is the input to `kummerLift` and `lamOf`. The Lean statement is `WitnessSpecialization`. The formula only requires the identity on the fixed multiplied witness. It does not require a comparison on every point or for every discriminant to deduce (O.2).

To construct that evaluation geometrically, restrict the Chebotarev place to the finite fields containing the genus point, division point and torsion; compare their reductions in one geometric residue field. The remaining work is a bridge across three interfaces: place restriction and a common residue model; Frobenius equivariance of reduction on the chosen division point and the prime-to-p torsion; and reduction of the split twist identification. For the full stage map on genus data, at an inert prime of (K_D) the ideal generated by the rational prime is principal, so in conductor one it splits in the Hilbert class field; descent to the resulting quadratic residue field is also needed. This requires several compatibility and descent lemmas. The tree already has `Affine.Point.reductionHom`, injectivity on prime-to-residue-characteristic torsion, an equivalence on torsion over the completion and the good reduction inertia results in vendored Tau Ceti.

The existing genus-point nontorsion result `genusPoint_index_ne_top` uses Cai–Shu–Tian and the tree's Artin factorization input.
