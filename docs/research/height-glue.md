## 1. `AnalyticUniformizationUnique`

The kernel Prop (`ZInQKernel/HDecomp/Faltings/Models.lean`, imported, never restated): two analytic
uniformizations `Φ, Φ' : ℂ^g → 𝒢(ℂ)_τ` (homomorphisms, onto, kernels `Λ, Λ'`, chart preimages
open, regular functions holomorphic along them) differ by a `ℂ`-linear automorphism `T` of `ℂ^g`.

**The proof** (`AUUnique.lean` and `part_main.lean`, assembled into `HeightGlue.lean`):

1. **The analytic topology** `anTop 𝒢 τ` on `𝒢(ℂ)_τ`: generated by the sets
   `{p ∈ U(ℂ) | s(p) ∈ V}` (`U` Zariski open, `s ∈ Γ(U, 𝒪)`, `V ⊆ ℂ` open). On an affine open it is the
   usual analytic topology (the generators of the coordinate ring give the embedding into `ℂ^n`).
   Every analytic uniformization is continuous for it (`continuous_of_isAnalyticUniformization`).
2. **Hausdorff, proved** (`analyticPointsT2`, no hypotheses). For `p ≠ q` take an affine `U ∋ r = p q⁻¹`
   and, if `1 ∈ U(ℂ)`, a regular `s` with `s(r) ≠ s(1)` (`pts_eq_of_evalAt`, metrization2). Apply
   metrization2's `productMapLocalStructure` to the division morphism `𝒢 ×_{𝓞_L} 𝒢 → 𝒢`,
   `(x, y) ↦ x y⁻¹`: near `(p, q)`, `s(x y⁻¹) = N(x, y)/H(x, y)` with `N, H` finite sums of products of
   regular functions, continuous for the product of the analytic topologies. A product neighbourhood
   on which `x y⁻¹ ∈ U(ℂ)` and `|s(x y⁻¹) − s(r)| < δ/2` contains no diagonal point.
3. **Immersion, proved** (`isImmersiveAtZero_of_discrete`): every analytic uniformization with a
   discrete kernel (no lattice condition, no `g = dim A`) detects every tangent vector at `0` by a
   regular function: metrization2's steps 2-5 of `jacDetNondegenerate_of` (translation through the
   group law, constancy along a line, discreteness).
4. **Factorization** (`exists_linear_factor`). Let `Φ₀` have a full lattice `Λ₀`. Then:
   - `ℂ^{g₀}/Λ₀` is compact (`IsZLattice.isCompact_range_of_periodic`);
   - the induced continuous bijection onto `𝒢(ℂ)_τ` (Hausdorff by step 2) is a homeomorphism;
   - `ℂ^{g₀} → ℂ^{g₀}/Λ₀` is a covering (`AddSubgroup.isAddQuotientCoveringMap_of_comm`), so any
     analytic uniformization `Φ` lifts to a continuous `F` with `Φ = Φ₀ ∘ F`, `F(0) = 0`
     (`IsCoveringMap.existsUnique_continuousMap_lifts`);
   - `F` is additive (`F(z+w) − F(z) − F(w)` is continuous with values in the discrete `Λ₀`), hence
     `ℝ`-linear, and `ℂ`-linear by step 3 and the chain rule over `ℝ`;
   - `F` is onto, by Baire: `Φ` onto gives `ℂ^{g₀} = range F + Λ₀` with `Λ₀` countable.
5. **Linear algebra** (`exists_linearEquiv_comp_eq`): two surjections `ℂ^g → ℂ^{g₀}` differ by an
   automorphism of `ℂ^g`. So `Φ = Φ₀ ∘ M`, `Φ' = Φ₀ ∘ M'` give `T` with `Φ' = Φ ∘ T`, and
   `z ∈ Λ' ↔ T z ∈ Λ` follows from the kernels.

`Φ₀` comes from the kernel's `AnalyticUniformizationExists` (AN3), so
`analyticUniformizationUnique_of_exists : AnalyticUniformizationExists → AnalyticUniformizationUnique`.
With metrization2's transport of T35 (`analyticUniformizationOfDim_of_uniformizationExists`) and
`abVar_dim_finite`, `analyticUniformizationExists_of_t35` gives AN3 from T35.

A first version (`AUUnique.lean`, kept) proved AN3U from two leaves, `ImmersiveUniformizationExists`
(AN3 plus an immersion clause) and `AnalyticPointsT2` (SGA 1 XII 3.1). Steps 2 and 3 then proved both:
`immersiveUniformizationExists_of : AnalyticUniformizationExists → ImmersiveUniformizationExists`
and `analyticPointsT2 : AnalyticPointsT2`. Corollary (`isImmersiveAtZero_of` in `AUUnique.lean`):
every full-lattice uniformization is immersive at `0`.

  surjective linear maps (§1 step 4), so `g = dim A`. (unif-dims proved the same from T35 by a
  2-torsion count, `dim_eq_of_isAnalyticUniformization`; either proof works.)
- **`NeronMetrizable` from T35**: height-split's `neronMetrizable_of` applied to the three.

## 3. H-3: `QMPairEmbeddingCorrespondence`

The target (height-split, `R5Count.lean`, copied verbatim into `QMCorr.lean` §0) is an `Equiv`, for
an imaginary quadratic order `R ⊆ k` with conductor prime to 6, between two sets:
- the isomorphism classes of QM pairs over `ℚ̄` with `End_𝒪(A) ≅ R`;
- the optimal embeddings `R ↪ 𝒪` modulo `𝒪^×`.

**New definitions** (`QMCorr.lean` §1, all proofs compiled):
- `rho : B →ₐ[ℚ] M₂(ℂ)`, the real splitting of `B = (2, −3)_ℚ`: `i ↦ diag(√2, −√2)`,
  `j ↦ [[0, −3], [1, 0]]`. The basis identities `i² = 2`, `j² = −3`, `ji = −ij` are proved.
- `HalfPlanes = ℂ ∖ ℝ`.
- `cmSetoid`: the orbits of `𝒪^×` acting through `ρ`. `τ ~ τ'` iff `ρ(u)(τ, 1)ᵀ = c(τ', 1)ᵀ` for some
  `u ∈ 𝒪^×` and `c ≠ 0`. It is proved to be an equivalence relation.
- `ordAt τ`: the CM order `{b ∈ 𝒪 : (τ, 1)ᵀ is an eigenvector of ρ(b)}`, proved to be a subring of `B`.

**The three Props:**
- **LEAF `QMUniformizationC`** (Shimura 1967; Voight GTM 288 Ch. 43; locators UNVERIFIED): an `Equiv`
  `Ψ` between the classes of QM pairs over `ℂ` (the kernel's `pairSetoid ℂ`) and `𝒪^× \ (ℂ ∖ ℝ)`, with
  `End_𝒪(A) ≅ ordAt τ` whenever `Ψ[A] = [τ]`. This is the moduli interpretation:
  - `(A, ι) ≅ (ℂ²/ρ(𝒪)(τ, 1)ᵀ, ρ)`, because `H₁` is free of rank one over `𝒪` (class number one);
  - `QMIso` corresponds to `τ' ∈ 𝒪^× τ`, since an `𝒪`-linear isomorphism is a scalar;
  - `End_𝒪(A_τ)` is given by the eigenvalue.
- **LEAF `QMDescentCM`**: for some `ε : ℚ̄ → ℂ`, base change of QM pairs (the kernel's relation
  `IsBaseChangeOfPair`) has four properties:
  - (a) it exists;
  - (b) it preserves `End_𝒪` up to isomorphism;
  - (c) it preserves and reflects isomorphism (rigidity: Conrad 2006 §3, the kernel's
    `T11b_Rigidity`);
  - (d) a QM pair over `ℂ` whose `End_𝒪` is isomorphic to `R` comes from `ℚ̄` (CM abelian varieties
    are defined over `ℚ̄`: Shimura–Taniyama; locator UNVERIFIED).

  Clauses (a)-(c) are consequences of the base-change construction and rigidity rather than one
  printed theorem; they are listed as a leaf because the base change of QM structures is not in the
  build.
- **GLUE `CMPointEmbeddingCorrespondence`** (quaternion arithmetic, unproved): the `𝒪^×`-orbits of
  `τ` with `ordAt τ ≅ R` are in bijection with the optimal embeddings modulo `𝒪^×`. This is the
  normalized embedding:
  - fix `σ₀ : k ↪ ℂ`; then `φ_τ` is the unique embedding with `λ ∘ φ_τ = σ₀`, where `λ` is the eigenvalue
    at `(τ, 1)ᵀ`;
  - `φ_{uτ} = u φ_τ u⁻¹`;
  - conversely, of the two fixed points of `φ(k^×)` exactly one is normalized.

**Proved** (`QMCorr.lean` §3):
- `nonempty_qmClasses_equiv`: QM classes over `ℚ̄` ≃ QM classes over `ℂ` with `End_𝒪 ≅ R`, from
  `QMDescentCM`;
- `nonempty_qmClassesC_equiv`: those ≃ `CMPoints k R`, from `QMUniformizationC`;
- `qmPairEmbeddingCorrespondence_of`, composing the two with the glue.
