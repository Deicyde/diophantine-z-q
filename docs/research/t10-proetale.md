## Smallest remaining statements

At each `A/K` and invertible prime `ℓ` in the row, set `Eσ = etalePull (galAut A σ) ℓ`. The missing statements are:

1. A canonical `ℤ_[ℓ]`-linear equivalence `C : X.EllAdicCohomology ℓ 1 ≃ₗ[ℤ_[ℓ]] EtaleH1 X ℓ`, with `C (galH A ℓ σ x) = Eσ (C x)`. A degree-one proof can work with finite torsors and needs two pieces:
   - **Finite stage:** prove `ProFiniteH X ℓ n 1 ≃+ FiniteH1 X ℓ n` for every `n`. Identify the continuous-function sheaf for discrete finite coefficients with the relevant constant sheaf, then compare degree-one Ext via finite torsors. The forward local lift calculation is available; the inverse construction, functorial classification and finite étale representability/descent across the pro-étale covers remain. The big étale derived evaluation and its precomposition maps must be identified with the same torsors. The comparison must be natural both for coefficient maps (reductions and scalars) and for the scheme isomorphism, including the source and coefficient identifications used by `transportₗ`.
   - **Integral stage:** identify the row's coefficient sheaf with the inverse limit of the finite pro-étale sheaves and prove that `x ↦ (proReduction X ℓ n 1 x)ₙ` is a bijection onto compatible families. The maps to the individual finite groups and compatibility with transport are already proved. In the derived approach prove the needed product/limit result on this site, then use the proved H⁰ difference calculation. In a torsor approach lift compatible torsors with their transition isomorphisms and prove the resulting inverse limit is locally trivial in the pro-étale topology. The tower of finite covers suggests using affine transition limits, but the needed weakly étale cover assertion and the cohomological identification are not supplied by those limits alone.
2. For the **same** geometric Milne family `φ`, prove `φ (Eσ y) = (A.galT σ⁻¹).dualMap (φ y)`. The draft gives naturality only for homomorphisms over `K`; the maps in this square induce nonidentity maps of the geometric base in general. A checked print could supply the stronger naturality. Alternatively, identify its family with the finite multiplication-cover construction and show that changing the geometric base transports that construction and its transition maps. The graph calculation fixes which automorphism acts on points in this approach.

The cited algebraic comparison is Bhatt–Scholze, *The pro-étale topology for schemes* (2015), with §5.6 reported in the earlier audit (locator UNVERIFIED). Mathlib already has both sites, the relevant Ext groups, set-level Mittag-Leffler results and several criteria for sequential limits in coherent sites. The missing theorem is the application to degree-one cohomology on these specific sites and coefficients. A proof of the finite and integral stages looks like upstream-scale work even in degree one (SPECULATIVE: order of thousands of lines, since it needs classification, descent and limit infrastructure). With a verified semilinear naturality statement, matching the exact conventions is small; formalizing the geometric cover construction if the print does not supply it could be substantial as well. Once the comparison and the same-family square are supplied, `compose_transport_squares` on each abelian variety proves the unchanged row.

## Known issues

- As recorded above, the 2008 citation needs a separate justification of equivariance. The reported 1986 locator is *Abelian varieties*, Cornell–Silverman, *Arithmetic Geometry*, Theorem 15.1(a) (UNVERIFIED). The draft is left as a draft and does not assume equivariance by declaring the isomorphism canonical.
- The manuscript uses rational Tate duality in characteristic zero. The fixed Lean row includes arbitrary fields and integral coefficients for an invertible prime. If a geometric statement is formulated using a separable closure, positive characteristic needs the dictionary to the algebraic closure, or an application directly over the algebraically closed base with the corresponding identification of points.
- Finite H¹ groups would give a Mittag-Leffler property for their own tower. That alone does not prove an identification with the cohomology of the limit sheaf. In degree one the Milnor kernel uses lim¹ of **H⁰**, addressed here at the level of its difference operator.
