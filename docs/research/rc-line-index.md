# Appendix F: line indexing of the ideal neighbours

For an Eichler order `R` of level `N` and discriminant `ℓ₀` in the existing ideal model, `lineIndex` gives `Eichler.LineIndex R p hp` at each prime `p ∤ Nℓ₀`. It sends `some t` to the subideal cut out by the line through `(1,t)` and `none` to the one cut out by `(0,1)`, using the chosen class representatives. The equivalence counts actual kernels, so different lines may give the same target class. The map `neighbours` supplies `AppF.ThetaStage.nb`; `adjoint_good` supplies `AppF.ThetaStage.adjoint` with `classWeight` for a definite algebra:
\[
N_{ij}\,w_j=N_{ji}\,w_i.
\]

The proof is on-the-spot (c) throughout the new lattice and matrix steps. A local generator identifies the completed ideal with the regular matrix module. The diagonal lattice already surjects onto its residue space: in the local span, each scalar can be replaced modulo `p` by an integer. The existing index `[I:pI]=p⁴` then identifies the reduction kernel. Right multiplication by a matrix unit extracts a column and puts it in either position. Consequently a right submodule consists of two copies of the same column space, so dimension two forces that space to be a line. Taking the integral preimage preserves the relative index, and every intermediate subideal is recovered from its image.

At `q ≠ p`, multiplication by the unit `p` in `ℤ_q` shows that the neighbour and the original ideal have the same local span. At `p`, the column lattice is the kernel of a surjective additive map to `𝔽_p`, hence has index `p`. Its basis matrix is `diag(p,1)` for the vertical line or the matrix with columns `(1, t̃)` and `(0,p)` for a lift `t̃` of the slope. Its determinant is `p`, so it gives an ambient local unit. Multiplying this unit by the generator of the original completed ideal gives a generator for the completed neighbour. Finally, `Eichler.adjoint` applies the existing arrow equivalence and its reversal to the line indices.

All four files compile with sequential `lake env lean` runs. The [axiom audit](../../scratch/rc-line-index/axioms.md) records 66 checks covering every new theorem and the main definitions. It reports only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`.

## Known issues

- No error was found in the manuscript steps checked here. The good-prime splitting is extracted from the local profile in `IsEichler`; the existence of that profile and the explicit order's local structure remain separate. The equivalence with adelic or geometric classes is outside this result.
- The ideal correspondence works also at `p = 2` when `2 ∤ Nℓ₀`. This does not extend the manuscript's odd-prime Legendre-symbol calculation to `2`.
- No new print or citation is used. The coefficient calculations, theta frame compatibility, and the modular-parametrization identity are outside this run.
