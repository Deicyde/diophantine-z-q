# The exact-order cover and the p-free model

The checked construction reaches the finite-group quotient of the exact-order cover. The cover commutes with arbitrary base change. The geometric unit action is defined by the Jacobian coordinate formulas over a coefficient domain, with the group laws, the action on field-valued generators, and scalar extension to arbitrary coefficient rings. Levels one and two use the identity action. The finite-group invariant algebra has arbitrary base change, flatness over the coefficient chart, and a field-point bijection with actual cyclic subgroups in each specified coefficient fibre. `DetectorCyclicLevelAlgebra` pins this first quotient at the detector, in characteristic zero and over the algebraic closure of the detector residue field. The short scaling formulas check the weights on coefficients and points. The remaining construction starts by descending this scaling to an internal grading of the exact-order algebra. The full quotient, its smoothness, and its bijection with isomorphism classes of pairs remain open.
