## T13. Skolem–Noether

Statement (`PkgI6.SkolemNoether`): for `A` a finite-dimensional central simple `K`-algebra and `B`
a finite-dimensional simple `K`-algebra, any two `K`-algebra maps `f, g : B → A` are conjugate by a
unit of `A`. Reference: Reiner, *Maximal orders*, Thm 7.21 (UNVERIFIED locator; the statement is
STANDARD).

Lean: `TSkolemKW.skolemNoether : SkolemNoether`, `hsynSkolemNoether : HSynSkolemNoether`,
`leafSkolemNoether : LeafSkolemNoether`. Proof, in three steps (all VERIFIED, compiled):
1. `isSimpleRing_tensor`: `C ⊗[K] B` is simple when `C` is central simple and `B` is simple (no
   finiteness needed). Minimal-length argument in a basis of `B`: an element of a nonzero ideal
   with fewest basis terms is normalized to have coefficient `1` somewhere (the ideal of such
   coefficients is two-sided in `C`, hence `C`); commutators with `C ⊗ 1` are shorter, hence
   zero; so all coefficients are central, i.e. scalars, and the element is `1 ⊗ b` with `b ≠ 0`;
   then `B` simple gives `1 ∈ I`.
2. `nonempty_linearEquiv_of_finrank_eq`: over a simple Artinian `K`-algebra `R`, two modules of
   the same finite `K`-dimension are isomorphic. Mathlib's `IsSimpleRing.isIsotypic` makes each a
   power of a simple module; the two simple modules are isomorphic (isotypy of `M × N`); compare
   dimensions.
3. `A` is a module over `R = Aᵐᵒᵖ ⊗[K] B` in two ways, `(a ⊗ b) · x = f(b) x a` and
   `g(b) x a` (through Mathlib's `AlgHom.mulLeftRight`); `R` is simple (step 1) and Artinian; an
   `R`-isomorphism `Φ` (step 2) is `x ↦ u x` with `u = Φ(1)`, `u f(b) = g(b) u`, and `u` is a unit
   because `Φ` is bijective.

Mathlib v4.35.0 has no Skolem–Noether and no simplicity of tensor products of simple algebras
(VERIFIED by grep). ATLAS (`~/aq/atlas-lean`, `MathlibExt.Algebra.Central.SkolemNoether`) has the
automorphism case only; it was not used (the proof here is independent and proves the two-map
form). Tau Ceti's `skolemNoether` (prereq-survey) was not available offline.

## T19. Brauer–Nesbitt in characteristic 0

Statements: `PkgI6.BrauerNesbittChar0` (two finite-dimensional representations of a group over a
field of characteristic 0, semisimple as `k[G]`-modules, with equal traces, are isomorphic as
`k[G]`-modules) and `HSyn.BrauerNesbitt` (the same for `ρ₁, ρ₂ : G →* GL_n(E)` with semisimplicity
as "every invariant subspace has an invariant complement" and conclusion "conjugate by some
`P ∈ GL_n(E)`"). References: Curtis–Reiner (1962) (27.13), (30.16); Lang, *Algebra*, XVII
Cor. 3.8 (UNVERIFIED locators; the statement is STANDARD).

Lean: `TSkolemKW.brauerNesbittChar0 : BrauerNesbittChar0`,
`hsynBrauerNesbitt : HSynBrauerNesbitt`. The core is a general theorem:

- `brauerNesbitt_module`: for any `k`-algebra `R` (`char k = 0`), two finite-dimensional
  semisimple `R`-modules `M, N` with `tr_k(r|M) = tr_k(r|N)` for all `r ∈ R` are isomorphic.

Proof (VERIFIED, compiled):
1. `isNilpotent_of_trace_pow_eq_zero`: in characteristic 0, if `tr(f^j) = 0` for all `j ≥ 1`
   then `f` is nilpotent. Cayley–Hamilton and `tr(χ_f(f)) = n·χ_f(0)` give `det f = 0`; `f`
   restricted to its range has the same traces of powers (`tr(ιh) = tr(hι)`), and the range is
   smaller; induct. (Not in Mathlib v4.35.0, VERIFIED by grep.)
2. `exists_injective_of_trace_eq`: if `S ⊆ M` is simple, then `S` embeds in `N`. The annihilator
   `J` of `N` has `tr_M(t^j) = tr_N(t^j) = 0`, so acts nilpotently on `M` (step 1); if `t s = s`
   with `t ∈ J`, `s ≠ 0`, nilpotency gives a contradiction, and `J s` is `0` or `S`; so `J`
   kills `S`. Then `S` is a quotient of `R / ann N`, which embeds in `N^d` (`d = dim N`), so by
   semisimplicity `S` is a submodule of `N^d`, and a coordinate gives `S ↪ N`.
3. Induction on `dim M`: split `S` off both sides (complements exist by semisimplicity), subtract
   traces.

The `k[G]` form follows by linearity of `r ↦ tr(r|M)` on `MonoidAlgebra k G`; the matrix form
by `Representation.mapSubmodule` (invariant subspaces ≃o `k[G]`-submodules) and `toMatrix'` of
the module isomorphism. No Brauer–Nesbitt exists in Mathlib v4.35.0 or ATLAS (grep, VERIFIED),
nor in Tau Ceti per prereq-survey.

## T18. Frobenius density

`HSyn.FrobeniusDense` (for every open subgroup `U ⊆ G_ℚ`, `σ ∈ G_ℚ` and finite `S`, there are
`p ∉ S`, a place `v` of `\bar ℚ` above `p` (a `ValuationSubring`) and an arithmetic Frobenius `τ`
at `v` with `τ⁻¹σ ∈ U`) is proved from `PkgI6.ChebotarevExists` (for `M/ℚ` finite Galois and
`σ ∈ Gal(M/ℚ)`, some prime `Q` of `𝓞_M` over some `p ∉ S`, unramified, has Frobenius `σ`):

- `TSkolemKW.HS.frobeniusDense_of_chebotarev : ChebotarevExists → FrobeniusDense` (VERIFIED).

It uses glue-c10's lift of a finite-level Frobenius to `G_ℚ` at a prime of `\bar ℤ`
(`GlueC10.frobDense`, copied verbatim from `Leaves.lean`, compiles here) and three new pieces:
- `zbarLoc`: the localization `\bar ℤ_𝔓` at a prime `𝔓` over `p` is a valuation subring of
  `\bar ℚ` (Prüfer property: for `x ∈ \bar ℚ` pass to `E = ℚ(x)`, where `(𝓞_E)_{𝔓 ∩ 𝓞_E}` is a
  DVR by Mathlib's `IsLocalization.AtPrime.isDiscreteValuationRing_of_dedekind_domain`;
  `exists_frac_or_inv`);
- `liesOver_and_isArithFrob`: an arithmetic Frobenius at `𝔓` stabilizes `zbarLoc` and satisfies
  `σx − x^p ∈ nonunits` on all of it, and `p` is a nonunit;
- the Krull-topology step (an open subgroup contains `Gal(\bar ℚ/E)` for a finite `E`).

So the H decomposition needs Chebotarev only in the finite form `ChebotarevExists`, which is what
C10's glue already uses.

Sanity check of that leaf (`ChebotarevCyclotomic.lean`, VERIFIED): `chebotarevAt_cyclotomic`
proves the body of `ChebotarevExists` for every cyclotomic field `M` (`IsCyclotomicExtension {n}
ℚ M`), from Mathlib's Dirichlet theorem (`Nat.forall_exists_prime_gt_and_eq_mod`), the
discriminant of `ℚ(ζ_n)` with Dedekind's criterion (`not_dvd_discr_iff_isUnramifiedIn`), and
`𝓞 = ℤ[ζ]` (`IsCyclotomicExtension.Rat.adjoin_singleton_eq_top`), the Frobenius congruence being
checked on the generator `ζ` and propagated through `ℤ[ζ]`. So the formalized leaf (Mathlib's
`IsArithFrobAt`, `Algebra.IsUnramifiedAt`, `Q.under ℤ = (p)`) is the intended statement where it
can be tested; the case C10 needs (non-abelian `M`) stays open. **Open**: `ChebotarevExists` itself (Neukirch VII (13.4), UNVERIFIED
locator). Its proof needs Chebotarev's theorem for cyclic extensions of number fields (Deuring's
reduction), i.e. L-functions or class field theory over number fields, which Mathlib v4.35.0 does
not have (it has Dirichlet's theorem for `ℚ`). The decomposition uses it for arbitrary finite
Galois `M` (C10: the field cut out by `r₃ ⊕ ρ_{f,3}`), so no special case suffices. Tau Ceti's
`NumberField.Chebotarev.hasNaturalDensity_frobeniusPrimeSet` (prereq-survey) would imply it; a
port was not possible offline, and its statement was not compared with ours.

## T17. Kronecker–Weber

`HSyn.KroneckerWeber` (a finite abelian Galois `L/ℚ` embeds in some `ℚ(ζ_n)`) is **not proved**.
Not in Mathlib v4.35.0; ATLAS v1 `Atlas/NumberTheoryI/code/KroneckerWeber.lean` has 22 `sorry`
and its gap-free `merely-true` variant does not contain the main theorem (VERIFIED by grep);
Tau Ceti's `kroneckerWeber` (prereq-survey) was not available offline. The decomposition uses it in
C8 for the fixed field of `ker ψ`, `ψ` a finite-order character of `G_ℚ` (a cyclic extension of
arbitrary order), so no small special case suffices. A route through the local leaf T16a
(`PkgI6.LocalKroneckerWeber`) plus Minkowski's theorem would remove T17 as a separate leaf, but
needs completions of number fields and the comparison of decomposition groups with local Galois
groups, which Mathlib v4.35.0 lacks (SPECULATIVE estimate: thousands of lines).

Proved (`KroneckerWeberUse.lean`, VERIFIED): glue-assembly §2.1's "finite image ⇒ Dirichlet
character", listed there as not written, from T17 and, for `ψ` with values in a field (the case
of C8: `ψ` takes values in `\bar ℚ₅^×`), from the cyclic case alone.
- `KroneckerWeberCyclic`: T17 for `L/ℚ` with cyclic Galois group;
  `kroneckerWeberCyclic_of_kroneckerWeber` shows it is a consequence of T17. **This is the form of
  the leaf the decomposition needs**; for crowdsourcing it is no easier than T17 in practice
  (every known proof proves the abelian case), but it is the exact use.
- `ker_ge_of_kroneckerWeberCyclic`, `eq_of_agree_on_roots_cyclic`: as below, for `ψ : G_ℚ →* Eˣ`
  (`E` a field), from `KroneckerWeberCyclic`: `Gal(L/ℚ) ≅ G_ℚ / ker ψ ≅ im ψ ⊆ Eˣ` is cyclic
  (Mathlib's `isCyclic_of_injective_ringHom`).
- **`dirichletCharacter_of_kroneckerWeberCyclic`** (the final form): for `ψ : G_ℚ →* Eˣ` with
  open kernel there are `m ≥ 1` and `χ : DirichletCharacter E m` (Mathlib's) with
  `ψ σ = χ (cycChar m σ)` for all `σ`, where `cycChar m : G_ℚ →* (ℤ/m)ˣ` is Mathlib's
  `modularCyclotomicCharacter` on `\bar ℚ`. Uses `cycChar_surjective` (proved: irreducibility of
  cyclotomic polynomials over `ℚ` through `IsCyclotomicExtension.autEquivPow`, lifted to `G_ℚ` by
  `AlgEquiv.liftNormal`).
- `ker_ge_of_kroneckerWeber`: `ψ : G_ℚ →* G` (`G` commutative) with open kernel kills every `σ`
  fixing the `m`-th roots of unity, for some `m ≥ 1` (fixed field of `ker ψ` is finite abelian
  by infinite Galois theory; T17 embeds it in `ℚ(ζ_m)`; normality puts it inside the field
  generated by `μ_m ⊂ \bar ℚ`);
- `eq_of_agree_on_roots`: so `ψ σ` depends only on the action of `σ` on `μ_m` (`ψ` factors through
  `Gal(ℚ(μ_m)/ℚ) ≅ (ℤ/m)ˣ`);
- `isOpen_ker_of_continuous`: a continuous `ψ` with finite image into a `T1` group (C8: values in
  `\bar ℚ₅^×`) has open kernel.
