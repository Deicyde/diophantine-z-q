## 3. The proof (all VERIFIED, compiled)

**Step 1 (`Reduction.lean`): from `UnramRoots` to procyclicity.** The new Prop
`UnramRootsTask.UnramProcyclic p` says: for finite `F/ℚ_p` there is `φ ∈ G_F` such that
- (F1) for every finite `K ⊇ F`, every `σ ∈ G_F` is `φ^k τ g`, with `τ ∈ G_F ∩ inertiaSet` and
  `g ∈ G_K`;
- (F2) for every `n ≥ 1` there is a homomorphism `χ : G_F → ℤ/n` with `χ(φ) = 1`, trivial on
  `G_F ∩ inertiaSet` and on some open `G_K`.

This is textbook: `G_F/I_F ≅ Ẑ`, generated by Frobenius (Serre, *Local Fields*, Ch. I §7 Prop.
20-21 and Ch. III §5; Neukirch, *ANT*, Ch. II (9.9); locators UNVERIFIED). It is now proved
(step 3).

`unramRoots_of_unramProcyclic` derives the conclusion in four moves:
1. `ψ(φ)` is a root of unity. Pigeonhole on the finitely many `ℚ_p`-embeddings of `F ⊔ F''`
   gives `φ^j ∈ G_{F''}`.
2. Choose `ξ` with `ξ⁴ = ψ(φ)` (`ℚ̄_ℓ` is algebraically closed). Put `n = 4j` and
   `η(σ) = ξ^{χ_n(σ)}` on `G_F`.
3. `η⁴ = ψ` follows from (F1), applied with `K = F ⊔ F'' ⊔ K_n`.
4. `η` is trivial on inertia and on `G_{F ⊔ K_n}` by (F2).

**Step 2a (`Metric.lean`, only Mathlib): metric inertia and roots of unity.**
- `fix_of_mem_inertiaSet_of_pow_eq_one`: metric inertia fixes every root of unity of order prime
  to `p`. If `u^N = 1` and `‖u − 1‖ < 1` with `p ∤ N`, then `u = 1`, because `‖N‖ = 1` and
  `Σ u^i ≡ N`.
- `mem_inertiaSet_of_fix`: conversely, a `σ ∈ G_{ℚ_p}` that fixes every root of every
  `X^{p^m} − X` is in metric inertia. The proof is a Teichmüller argument:
  - the minimal polynomial of an `x` with `‖x‖ ≤ 1` has integral coefficients
    (`spectralValue_le_one_iff`), so its residue in `𝒪_{ℚ̄_p}/𝔪` is algebraic over `𝔽_p`;
  - Frobenius pigeonhole on the finitely many roots of the reduced polynomial gives
    `‖x^{p^m} − x‖ < 1`;
  - `X^{p^m} − X = ∏ (X − t)` in `ℚ̄_p`, so some root `t` has `‖x − t‖ < 1`;
  - `‖σx − x‖ = ‖σ(x−t) − (x−t)‖ < 1`, since automorphisms preserve the spectral norm.

**Step 2b (`TCPart.lean`): Frobenius data from Tau Ceti.** `frobData_exists F` gives `q = #𝓀[F]`,
a power of `p` greater than 1, together with:
- a lift `φ ∈ G_F` (`AlgEquiv.liftNormal` of `maximalUnramifiedFrobenius F ℚ̄_p`) with `φx = x^q`
  on the roots of every `X^{q^f} − X`;
- for every `σ ∈ G_F` and `f ≥ 1`, a `k` with `σx = x^{q^k}` on those roots. This comes from
  `σ|_{F_f} ∈ zpowers(Frob)` and the finiteness of `Gal(F_f/F)`.

`FrobData` itself is stated without Tau Ceti.

**Step 2c (`part_procyclic.lean`, Mathlib only): `UnramProcyclic` from the Frobenius data.**
- **Uniqueness of the exponent.** The exponent `k` is unique mod `n` on the roots of
  `X^{q^n} − X`. Take a primitive `(q^n − 1)`-th root of unity; `q^k ≡ q^{k'} (mod q^n − 1)`
  implies `k ≡ k' (mod n)`.
- **(F2).** Set `χ_n(σ) = k mod n`. It is trivial on metric inertia (step 2a, since `p ∣ q^n`)
  and on `G_K` for `K = F ⊔ ℚ_p(roots of X^{q^n} − X)`.
- **(F1).** Let `E = F(R)` (`R` = the roots of all `X^{q^f} − X`, `f ≥ 1`) and `M` the normal
  closure of `K`. Let `H₀` be the image of `Gal(Ω/E)` in `Gal(M/ℚ_p)`; its fixed field is `M ∩ E`
  by `InfiniteGalois.fixedField_fixingSubgroup`.
  1. A basis of that fixed field gives a level `N` with `M ∩ E ⊆ F(R_N)`.
  2. Let `σ' = φ^{-k}σ`. It fixes `F(R_N)`, so `σ'|_M ∈ H₀` by the finite Galois
     correspondence. That gives `τ ∈ Gal(Ω/E)` with `τ|_M = σ'|_M`.
  3. `τ` is in metric inertia by step 2a: the roots of `X^{p^m} − X` lie in `R`, since `q` is a
     power of `p`.
  4. `g = τ⁻¹σ'` fixes `M ⊇ K`, and `σ = φ^k τ g`.
