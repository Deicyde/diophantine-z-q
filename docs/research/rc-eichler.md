# Appendix F: the order layer at one stage

**Scoped stop.** The ideal model uses the locally principal fractional right ideals of a full order in the definite quaternion algebra. The class set is finite, its chosen left orders have rank-four integer bases, and their unit groups embed in $B^\times$, have norm one, and have orders dividing $c_0$. This supplies the class, unit and mass fields of both theta-stage records in the ideal model. Duality and the mass identity are proved for the actual index-$p^2$ ideal correspondence once its kernels are indexed by the lines. To get the manuscript's stage, the remaining order-layer work is to realize the local Eichler profile at level $N$, identify the ideal model with the adelic double cosets, and construct the line indexing at a good prime.

The finite class proof is on-the-spot (c). The positive definite norm cuts a finite set out of each lattice under a norm bound, so a nonzero right ideal $I$ has a shortest vector $x$. The equivalent ideal $J=x^{-1}I$ contains $R$, and every nonzero element of $J$ has norm at least one. Reduce $J$ modulo $R$ into a fundamental parallelepiped on a basis of $R$. In a fine enough grid two representatives in the same cell have difference of norm less than one, so they coincide. This bounds $[J:R]$ uniformly; the factorial of the number of cells clears every such $J$ into $R$. There are only finitely many sublattices between $R$ and this fixed dilate $(1/d)R$. In particular $dJ$ is integral and lies between $dR$ and $R$. The integral representatives have bounded additive index, and integral right ideals of bounded positive index form a finite set. No reduced ideal norm API or covolume calculation is needed.

## Remaining statements

1. **Local Eichler profile and existence.** For $\ell_0$ prime and $N>0$ prime to $\ell_0$, find a global order satisfying `IsEichler ell₀ N` in the algebra supplied by `AppF.VoightMainThm1461`. For the explicit algebra first prove `AppF.RamifiedExactlyAtEll0Infty ell₀ (-1) (-ell₀)` (the finite-place clauses remain), and prove maximality of `explicitOrderAt`. At a split place the standard profile should be the intersection of the endomorphism orders of $\mathbb Z_p^2$ and $mathbb Z_p\oplus p^e\mathbb Z_p$. For level $N$, choose these local lattices at the finitely many $p\mid N$ and patch their orders inside the global maximal order. This is a substantial local classification and lattice patching step, including the dyadic split case. `IsEichler.local_split` extracts the good-prime case of the profile; it does not deduce the profile from ramification.
2. **Adelic identification.** For a finite adele $z=(z_p)\in\widehat B^\times$, put $I(z)=\{x\in B:x\in z_pR_p\text{ for all }p\}$. Prove it is a full right ideal and $I(z)_p=z_pR_p$; conversely a locally principal ideal is recovered by intersecting its completions, and its local generators can be chosen integral units almost everywhere. Then $I(z')=uI(z)$ iff $z'=uzr$ for some $r\in\widehat R^\times$. The same completion/intersection result gives $R_l(I(z))=B\cap z\widehat Rz^{-1}$. This is the needed arithmetic equivalence, independent of the geometric part of the Jetchev–Kane citation. It needs a local-global lattice lemma and the finite restricted product, rather than a new print.
3. **Line indexing.** For $p\nmid N\ell_0$ and $I$ locally principal, identify $I/pI$ as a right module with $M_2(\mathbb F_p)$ over the reduction of $R$. The dimension-two right submodules consist of matrices with both columns in a fixed line (apply the matrix units). Their integral preimages are the index-$p^2$ subideals, locally unchanged away from $p$; at $p$ their column lattice is $\Lambda_L\subset\mathbb Z_p^2$ of index $p$, so choosing its basis shows that they are locally principal. These identifications supply `LineIndex R p hp`, with `some t` the line through $(1,t)$ and `none` the line through $(0,1)$, as in `RC/AppF/Lines.lean`. The outstanding proof is the reduction and completion bridge and the matrix-module classification. Merely choosing any $p+1$ elements of the quotient would lose kernel multiplicity.
