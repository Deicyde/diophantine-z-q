The pair reading on the rational quotient agrees with the actual reading of `Pinned.Y0LevelPoints` after each detector base change. The supplied fixed compact parametrization restricts along the immersion and agrees on point evaluations.

The full `GenericFibreComparison` remains open. The actual pair readings agree over the generic j-line. The two pointwise generic operator comparisons also remain open. `GoodRow.source` has the scheme identification it needs. `W_generic` still requires comparison with the fixed rational involution.

The bijection intertwines every automorphism over `LeafModCurves.Qj`. The iterated Vélu quotient invariant descends to `Y0RationalGenericQuotientJ` in `Y0RationalGenericJ`, with the actual quotient value at every geometric point.

The actual quotient invariant generates the generic field of degree ψ(N) over `LeafModCurves.Qj`. Its minimal polynomial is the specialization Φ_N(X,j). The field isomorphism `genericModularFieldEquiv` sends the first j-coordinate to the actual `jq` and the quotient invariant to `jqN N` in `modularFunctionField N`.
