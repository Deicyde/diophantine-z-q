For the core in `ZInQKernel/Contrib/OneProof/WildPeriodCoprimeCore.lean`, write
`v = w.valuation` and call `σ` wild if `v(σ x − x) < v(x)` for all nonzero `x`.
Here (K) and (Ω) are fields with a (K)-algebra structure on (Ω).

**ON THE SPOT (provenance (c)).** This argument is in neither the manuscript nor
Silverberg–Zarhin. A wild element preserves the valuation by the strict ultrametric
inequality. The valuation form of `wildSet_mul` and `wildSet_pow` then follows,
as in `TailC.lean`. For averaging, put `d = σ x − x`. If `σ^m x = x`, the sum
of the `σ^i d` for `0 ≤ i < m` telescopes to zero, so
`∑ (σ^i d − d) = −m*d`. If `d ≠ 0`, every term has valuation strictly less
than `v(d)`; `Valuation.map_sum_lt` contradicts `v(−m*d) = v(d)` when `v(m) = 1`.

For the point, the affine argument of `ReductSplit.exists_fixing_field`
(`TailZ7.lean`) works over any algebraic extension: take the field generated by
the images of the finitely many affine coordinates. This gives the conclusion
of `G15Ram.ptFinite` without the filtered colimit scaffolding and without
identifying `Ω` with a chosen algebraic closure. In the normal closure of that
field, factor the order of the restriction of `σ` as `ℓ^b * N`, with `ℓ ∤ N`.
Then `(σ^N)^(ℓ^b)` fixes the field. The unit comparison carries `ℓ^b` from `O`
to `w`, so averaging with the wild element `σ^N` shows that `σ^N` fixes the field
itself. Iteration gives the desired period at the point and coprimality with every
`ℓ^n`. Neither the inertia hypothesis nor the torsion hypothesis is needed by the
core; both are kept in the exact statement.

## What remains

The reader at `ZInQKernel/OneProof/H/TailZ85.lean:99` uses the row through
`SZRaynaud.raynaudWildNF_of_leaves`. Its inputs already include
`SilverbergZarhinThm35`, which takes the rationality of full prime-to-residue-characteristic
torsion of order at least 3 as input and produces semistable reduction.
`HasSemistableReductionSZ` and the p. 405 definition describe the special fibre.
Neither describes the action of inertia on the generic fibre's Tate module.
`SZRaynaud.hasSemistableReduction_of_sz` supplies the geometric semistability
dictionary, but supplies no action statement. The Néron–Ogg–Shafarevich row handles
good reduction, and the de Rham and potentially semistable representation rows concern
the representation at its coefficient prime. They do not give this prime-to-residue-characteristic
implication. The Faltings height base change row gives a height identity, and the
Néron model base change proofs give model comparisons.

The earlier `sga7_IX_3_5_ForwardGeom` producer still exists as a conditional
theorem, not as an input of `frozenTarget`. The old field was removed from the live
route with the D2 reroute; `TailZ85.lean` specifically records the replacement by
`elliotWildInertiaTrivial`. `SZRaynaud.elliot_of_semistableUnipotent` consumes the
square-zero condition on each Tate vector; `ReductSplit.unipSq_of_tate` projects it
to each coordinate, and `PkgI2.fixed_of_unipSq_of_iterate` combines it with the
period. The smallest missing input for this argument is therefore:

> In the setting of `ElliotWildInertiaTrivial`, for each such wild inertia element
> `σ` and each `t : TΩ A Ω ℓ`, `galTΩ A ℓ σ (galTΩ A ℓ σ t)
> − 2 • galTΩ A ℓ σ t + t = 0`.

The analogous identity directly on every Tate coordinate would also suffice.
There is no need for the converse criterion, for the tame inertia action, or for
a theorem over arbitrary residue fields. Proving the missing assertion directly
would require a comparison linking the Tate module and the semiabelian special
fibre (for example a filtration with inertia acting trivially on the graded
pieces). The checked period proof needs none of that theory.
