## Known issues

- **1986 versus 2008 (UNVERIFIED).** No mathematical difference is established offline in the degree-one geometric isomorphism: both local descriptions give integral classical étale `H¹` as the linear dual of the Tate module for an invertible prime. The reported locators differ: Theorem 15.1(a) for the 1986 chapter, Part I, Theorem 12.1(a) for the 2008 notes. `OPEN.md` describes the notes' theorem over a separably closed field and reports that equivariance is stated without proof separately in Remark 12.5 (UNVERIFIED). The exact hypotheses, whether ordinary naturality is explicit, and whether or where the 1986 print gives semilinear compatibility are unverified. Neither source is being credited with a pro-étale statement. The manuscript uses a rational dual form over a characteristic-zero local field; the fixed Lean row is integral over arbitrary fields.
- If the 1986 theorem is formulated with a separable closure, check the closure dictionary in positive characteristic: `Kbar/K^sep` is purely inseparable, étale cohomology is invariant under the resulting universal homeomorphism, and prime-to-characteristic finite étale torsion has the same geometric points. If it is formulated for abelian varieties over a separably closed field, it can be applied over `Kbar` itself using the pullback identification of its points with `A(Kbar)`. The closure distinction is absent in the manuscript's characteristic-zero application.
- The row's docstring attributes the result to naturality in the scheme. This does not verify that Milne's naturality includes semilinear maps or that the comparison is compatible with Lean's particular transport. The existing code also does not prove the group law for `galH`; the pointwise row and the two squares above do not need it.
