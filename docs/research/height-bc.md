Notation:
- `π^*` is `bcCot : ω_{A_L} → ω_{A_{L'}}` (cotangent spaces at the origin), semilinear over

- `ψ` is `bcHodge = L' ⊗ ⋀^g π^*`;
- `β` is `bcPts : 𝒢'(ℂ)_{τ'} → 𝒢(ℂ)_{τ'|_L}`, `p' ↦ π ∘ p'`.

## 1. What is proved (all VERIFIED in `Final.lean`; 28 `#print axioms`, all standard)

**Part A. The Arakelov reduction of H4a.**
- `modelHeightBaseChangeGE_of_descentHalf` is the descent half of H4a from a geometric statement
  (`HodgeLineDescentHalf`). The proof builds a metrization of `𝒢` from one of `𝒢'`:
  - the lattice is `⋀^g ω_𝒢`;
  - at each `σ : L → ℂ` it takes the data of `𝒢'` at a place `τ'` above `σ` that maximizes
    `log ‖ψ w‖²_{τ'}`;
  - the maximum over a fibre is at least the average, and `sum_embeddings_comp` does the
    bookkeeping.
- New lemmas used there:
  - `exists_comp_eq`: every `σ` extends to `L'`;
  - `exists_ringOfIntegers_of_algebraMap`;
  - `secIndex_ne_zero_of_baseChange`: a finite index descends, because `Λ` is then a fractional
    ideal in coordinates.
- `modelHeightBaseChange_of_descent`: H4a (`=`) from `HodgeLineBaseChangeDescent`. The `≤` half
  reuses g0-stablebc's proved `metrizationBaseChange_of_hodgeLine`. The statement is copied
  verbatim and checks unchanged over the kernel's objects.

**Part B. The natural map** (new constructions, all compiled):

- `bcProj_unit` (`e' ≫ π = b ≫ e`, from `IsMonHom e.hom` and `Functor.obj.η_def`) and
  `bcProj_genericUnit` (`π(ξ') = ξ`);
- `bcMap` (`𝒪_{𝒢,ξ} → 𝒪_{𝒢',ξ'}`), with `bcMap_germ`, `bcMap_mem_maximalIdeal` and
  `bcMap_structMap`;
- `bcCot`;
- `bcAlt` and `bcExt` (`⋀^g π^*`, through restriction of scalars by `Module.compHom`);
- `bcHodge` (`ψ`, through `TensorProduct.liftAddHom`), with `bcHodge_smul` (`L`-semilinear) and
  `bcHodge_wedge` (`ψ(wedge f) = wedge'(π^* f)`);

## 2. Findings

- **(a) `HodgeLineBaseChange` alone does not give H4a.** It gives only the inequality
  `h(𝒢') ≤ h(𝒢)`. The other inequality needs two more clauses for the same `ψ`:
  - the converse line clause. An injective semilinear `ψ` does not bound `dim_L` (think of
    `L ↪ L'`);
  - descent of uniformizations from `𝒢'(ℂ)_{τ'}` to `𝒢(ℂ)_{τ'|_L}`.

  `HodgeLineBaseChangeDescent` is `HodgeLineBaseChange` with these two clauses added. H4a (`=`)
  follows from it (`modelHeightBaseChange_of_descent`). This is not a defect of either statement:
  `HodgeLineBaseChange` was designed for the `≤` half that g0-stablebc needs.
- **(b) The descent direction is formal once `β` is a group isomorphism**, and that is now proved
  (`ptsBaseChangeBij`, `ptsBaseChangeMul`). The forward direction is not formal. Its content is
  that regular functions on opens of `𝒢'` that are defined over `L'`, and not pulled back from
  `𝒢`, are holomorphic along `Φ'`; this is `UniformizationLiftAnalytic`. It is the hardest of the
  four.
- **(c) The coefficient-map clause splits into an analytic part and an algebraic part.**
  - The analytic part is now proved for every group model (`germDerivation`,
    `jacobianSemilinear`): the Jacobian of an analytic uniformization is a semilinear function of
    the wedge.
  - The algebraic part concerns the base change: the pulled-back differentials span
    (`CotangentSpanBC`).
  - The same machinery (`evalAt_one_eq_zero`, `germAlong_add_mul`) is what the project's
    "germ along `Φ`" definition needs to behave as intended. It may be reused by other glue, such
    as h5-glue's analytic transport and the uniqueness half AN3.
- **(d) g0-stablebc's `Main.part` elaborates unchanged over the kernel's objects**, given one alias
  `abbrev ZInQ.DefFaltings.AbVar K := PkgI3.AbVar K` for the fully qualified name in g0-split's
  statement (`G0WithBC.lean`). The integration of g0-stablebc therefore needs no proof changes.
- **(e) `OmegaBaseChange` needs less than its first justification suggested** (review 2). It uses
  separatedness, quasi-coherence of the conormal and the base-changed ideal of the section. It
  does not use smoothness or flatness.
