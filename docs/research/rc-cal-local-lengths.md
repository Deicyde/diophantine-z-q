The local-action version of HKL2 derives all three genuine local length fields from `MilneLocalDualityOrder` and the action on the actual local coefficients. The two moving lengths are `v₂(r + 1 − χa_r)`; each active length is two. With the retained presentations, `Recorded.genuine_length_iff_local_det` identifies these lengths with the local block determinant valuations. The resulting consumer keeps the same calibration target and the same uniform constant. No new print or GLUE hypothesis is introduced, and `PostLeaves.calibration` is outside this change.

At precision `i`, the coefficients are `M_i = (E[2^i] ⊗ A_i)(χ_h)`, where `A_i = (ℤ/2^i)[t]/t^i`. At a moving place, inertia fixes both factors and the unramified quotient is topologically generated by arithmetic Frobenius. The existing Tate basis and its finite-level comparison identify the fixed points with the kernel of `χFr − 1`. Its determinant is `r + 1 − χa_r`. On a finite module the kernel and cokernel of an endomorphism have equal order, so `card_coker_acoef` (which uses `smith_matrix`) gives the claimed power of two. At an active place the chosen inertia element acts by minus one, so invariants are killed by two; conversely all elements killed by two are invariant because the untwisted two-torsion is rational and the sign acts trivially on elements killed by two. The same finite-module count applied to `2·1₂` gives `2^{2i}`. Applying the existing `length_H2_of_H0` to the central character at each place proves all three fields.

The local actions concern which Galois elements act, how they act on torsion, and what the quadratic character does. They can be supplied independently of the chosen presentation or determinant coordinate. This is the reduction in proof burden relative to assuming the numerical lengths and to the entire calibration leaf. As logical propositions, the local actions and the Milne hypothesis imply the lengths, so “strictly weaker” cannot mean reverse logical implication. A length alone has no information about which inertia elements act, or even the Frobenius trace and determinant individually.

The existing printed form is `MilneLocalDualityOrder` (ADT I.2.3, locator as recorded in the tree). The good-reduction step uses the vendored Silverman VII.3.1 and VII.4.1 results as already used by `fullTorsion_of_goodReduction`; no new Silverman premise appears. The rank-two determinant expansion, the cardinal comparison, the coordinate reconstruction, and the formal comparison of the Frobenius predicates in the chosen embeddings are on-the-spot deductions (provenance (c)).
