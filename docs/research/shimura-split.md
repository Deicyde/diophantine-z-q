## 1. Main result

The kernel now has rigidity: a pointed morphism of abelian varieties is a homomorphism
(`MulRigidity.isMonHom_of_geometricallyIntegral`, `ZInQKernel/Contrib/FrobChain/Rigidity.lean`). With it this task
proves the bridge between fields of moduli and residue fields. leaf-qm-fp §2.4 had called that bridge impossible
"because Mathlib does not have rigidity".

* **Field of moduli = residue field.** VERIFIED: compiled, standard axioms (`Bridge.lean`).
  * `fieldOfModuli3_eq_residueImage`: on a fine witness, `R1fpProps Y Φ → Φ ℚ̄ y = [P] → fieldOfModuli3 P = residueImage y`.
  * `fieldOfModuliPair_eq_residueImage`: on a coarse witness, `X6Props … → Ψ x = [P] → fieldOfModuliPair P = residueImage x`.
  * The proof has two steps:
    1. Two base changes of one QM object (or QM pair) along one field map are isomorphic (`bc_unique3`,
       `bc_uniquePair`, via rigidity).
    2. `σ` fixes the residue image of `y` iff `y ∘ Spec σ = y` (`fixing_iff`). Infinite Galois theory then
       gives the result (`InfiniteGalois.fixedField_fixingSubgroup`).
* **So two of the eight rows are theorems from the row `r1r2`, with no new hypothesis** (VERIFIED):
  * `r1rec_of_r1r2 : R1R2fpRep → R1rec`;
  * `r5fp_of_r1r2 : R1R2fpRep → R5fp`.

  The bundle's `R1R2fpRep` already carries their point forms `R1recProps` and `R5ptProps`.
* **Every other row except `r3` is split.** Each new leaf is the published statement on the canonical model (an
  existential over a witness of the moduli clause lists), or a textbook theorem. The glue is compiled:
  * the orbit computation for `y3Connected`;
  * the GAGA-level step for `shimuraFamily`;
  * the surjectivity clause of Baba–Granath;
  * the coarse comparison, `HeckeOfFine`, and transports.
* **Finding** (VERIFIED by `Iff.rfl`). qm-glue's LEAF-form `ConicCanonical` is the row `X6Kummer.X6ConicModel`
  verbatim, so H-43 had not been reduced; `ConnectedCanonical` is likewise the row `TSHecke.Y3Connected`.
  * H-43 now rests on `r1r2`, T5 and a smaller leaf `ElkiesConicX6`. That leaf is the conic model and Hauptmodul of
    one witness, without the row's Hecke, CM-existence, reciprocity, surjectivity and González–Rotger clauses.
  * H-59 now rests on Deligne's components leaf and a compiled orbit computation.
* **Not split: `r3` (`R03_LinYang`).** See §4.

## 3. The rows in detail

### 3.1 The bridge, and `r1rec`, `r5` (`Bridge.lean`)

* **`bc_core`.**
  * *Statement.* Let `B, B'` be pullbacks of `A` along `Spec f`, with identity sections lying over `η_A`. Then
    they are isomorphic over `A` by mutually inverse homomorphisms.
  * *Proof.* `IsPullback.isoIsPullback` gives the iso of schemes. The identity section goes to the identity
    section by the uniqueness half of the pullback. The kernel's rigidity makes both maps homomorphisms.
* **`bc_unique3` and `bc_uniquePair`.**
  * They add `ι`-equivariance and the level clause, by pullback uniqueness.
  * `bc_transport3` and `bc_transportPair` move a base change along an isomorphism of the target.
* **`fixing_iff`.** It uses the kernel's `specToEquivOfField_comp` and `IntermediateField.adjoin_induction`.
* **The bridge.** By naturality (the fine case) or the Galois clause of `X6Props` (the coarse case), together with
  the two lemmas above:
  * `stab3 P` (resp. `stabPair P`) equals the fixing subgroup of the residue image;
  * its closure is itself;
  * the fixed field of the fixing subgroup is the field (`ℚ̄/ℚ` is Galois).
* **Consequences.**
  * `r1rec_of_point`, `r5fp_of_point`: the object forms from the point forms on one witness.
  * `point_of_r1rec`, `point_of_r5fp`: the converses, on any witness.
  * So `R1rec` is equivalent to `ReciprocityCanonical` (given a fine witness), and `R5fp` to `GonzalezRotgerX6`.

**Effect on the bundle.**
* The fields `r1rec` and `r5` can be dropped: `r1rec_of_r1r2` and `r5fp_of_r1r2` derive them from `r1r2`.
* Alternatively, keep them and drop the reciprocity and González–Rotger inputs of `r1r2` (`r1r2fpRep_of_split6`
  takes `R1rec`, `R5fp`).

### 3.4 `shimuraFamily` (`ShimuraFam.lean`)

**Holomorphy.** "Holomorphic `φ : ℍ → Y(ℂ)`" is written with the regular functions of `Y` (`IsHolFamily`):
* preimages of Zariski opens are open;
* for every regular `f ∈ Γ(Y, U)`, the value `regVal U f (φ z)` is locally an analytic function of `z`.

This is the standard notion: an affine open embeds in `ℂ^n` by finitely many regular functions, and the analytic
topology is the induced one (SPECULATIVE, but standard).

**Leaf.** `ShimuraHolFamily` is Main Thm I in this form: **one** family, with the moduli clause of `IsR1bFamily`
copied verbatim.

**The step to every `π` (`holP1_of_isHolFamily`).**
* `π : ToP1` is given by charts `U, V, f, g`.
* Where `x ∈ U`, `π(φ z) = f(φ z)` (`val_of_mem`).
* Where `x ∈ V`, `π(φ z) = 1/g(φ z)`, with `∞` exactly where `g` vanishes (`val_of_memV`). This uses `fg = 1` on
  `U ∩ V`, `basicOpen g = U ∩ V`, and the restriction compatibility of evaluations `eval_res`.
* Continuity at a pole uses `tendsto_invP1` (`w ↦ 1/w` into `P¹(ℂ)`).

So the flagged composition step (qm-split: "the 'every `π`' reading is a consequence of the printed statement
plus that composition step") is now a Lean proof, and the leaf is the printed statement.

### 3.5 `y3Connected` (`Y3Conn.lean`)

**Definitions** (Mathlib only):
* `baseQbar Y = Y ×_{Spec ℚ} Spec ℚ̄`;
* the action `galAct Y σ = id × Spec σ` and the induced map `galActπ₀` on `ConnectedComponents`;
* `chi3 σ = modularCyclotomicCharacter Qbar card_mu3 σ`, where `card_mu3 : Nat.card μ₃(ℚ̄) = 3` is proved.

**Leaf `DeligneComponentsY3`.** Some fine witness has `c : π₀(Y_ℚ̄) ≃ (ℤ/3)^×` with `c(σ·z) = χ₃(σ)·c(z)`. Because
`χ₃` has order 2, the sign convention of the reciprocity map does not matter.

**Proof** (`y3Connected_of_deligne`).
* `exists_chi3_neg`: some `σ` sends a primitive cube root of unity `ω` to `ω²`. This uses
  `Normal.minpoly_eq_iff_mem_orbit` and `cyclotomic_eq_minpoly_rat`, and then
  `modularCyclotomicCharacter.spec` with `IsPrimitiveRoot.pow_inj` gives `χ₃(σ) = −1`.
* `units_zmod3` (`decide`): `(ℤ/3)^× = {t, −t}`.
* A clopen `U ⊊ Y`, `U ≠ ∅`, pulls back along the surjective `Y_ℚ̄ → Y` to a clopen `V` that is stable under
  `galAct`. The labels of the components meeting `V` form a nonempty proper `χ₃`-stable subset of `(ℤ/3)^×`, which
  is a contradiction.

The computation `π₀ = ℤ̂^×/nrd(K₃) = (ℤ/3)^×` (local quaternion algebras, strong approximation) stays inside the
leaf, so the leaf is a consequence of Deligne's theorem and not its printed form.

## 4. Not split: `r3` (`R03_LinYang`), and what remains

**`r3`** is stated over `ℂ`: every QM surface `A/ℂ` is a Jacobian on the Lin–Yang family or a product. It
combines:
* Lin–Yang's Example 29: on the finite nonzero `j`-locus of `X₆`, the Jacobian with the printed Igusa vector;
* the moduli coordinate `j = π₆(x)` of the point of `(A, ι)`;
* Lemma QP (the polarization);
* Weil's dichotomy at `j ∈ {0, ∞}`.

The moduli witnesses here give `Ψ` only on `ℚ̄`-points, so `j(A)` for `A/ℂ` needs `X₆(ℂ)` with a moduli bijection
over `ℂ`. Lin–Yang's statement is about principally polarized surfaces, and `PkgI3.AbVar` has no polarization. A
published-form split needs:
* a polarization object;
* `X6Props` over `ℂ` (or the Lefschetz principle, the chain's glue target `R03_LinYang_algClosed`).

Not attempted.

**Remaining glue and leaf-forms.**
* `PairsLift` is a consequence. A Lean proof would need, beyond T5 (`|A[3]| = 81`), either `deg ι(b) = nrd(b)²`
  or "a homomorphism killing `A[n]` factors through `[n]`" (Mumford §7, UNVERIFIED); neither is in the project.
  Given that, the rest is a finite computation in `𝒪/3𝒪`, a local ring with residue field `𝔽₉`: `A[3]` is free
  of rank one iff the uniformizer does not kill it.
* `DeligneComponentsX6` and `DeligneComponentsY3` both specialize one theorem (Deligne's `π₀`). The specialization
  computations (`nrd(K)`) need local quaternion algebras.
* `ElkiesConicX6`, `BabaGranathX6'` and `ReciprocityCanonical` are consequences, as described above.
