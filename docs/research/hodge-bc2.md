## Proofs

### HodgeBCInjective (`hbc_proof.lean.part`)

1. **`L`-structure on `ω_{A_L} = 𝔪_ξ/𝔪_ξ²`.** The map `φL : L → 𝒪_{𝒢,ξ}` is `L ≅ κ(η)`
   (`IsLocalization.algEquiv` with Mathlib's `functionField_isFractionRing_of_affine`) followed by
   the stalk map of `𝒢 → Spec 𝓞_L` at the generic point. `cotL` is `Module.compHom` along `φL`,
   and `cotL_isScalarTower` holds because `φL ∘ algebraMap = structMap`.
2. **Semilinearity of `π^*` over `L → L'`** (`bcCot_smulL`). This is ring-hom extensionality on the
   localization `L` of `𝓞_L`, together with `bcMap_structMap`.
3. **Injectivity of cotangent base change** (`bcCot_core`). Infra-stalks'
   `cotangent_baseChange_injective` is applied to the fibre square `𝒢 ×_{𝓞_L} 𝓞_{L'}` at
   `z = e(ξ')`, with `s, y` the generic points.
   - The result is transported to `𝒢'` along the stalk isomorphism of `e`
     (`mapCotangent_injective_of_isIso`).
   - `Module.Flat.linearIndependent_one_tmul` is used over the field `κ(η)`.
   - Conclusion: `𝓞_{L'}`-combinations of `π^*b_i` vanish only trivially when the `b_i` are
     `L`-independent.
4. **`π^* ∘ b` is an `L'`-basis** (`bcCotBasis`). Independence follows from step 3 after clearing
   denominators, and spanning from `cotangentSpanBC`.
5. **Exterior powers** (`ExtFrac`, Mathlib-only). For a domain `R` with fraction field `K` and an
   `R`-module `M` that is a compatible `K`-space, `extFracEquiv : ⋀[K]^n M ≃ₗ[K] K ⊗[R] ⋀[R]^n M`.
   Hence `extFracBasis`: `1 ⊗ ιMulti_family R n b` is a `K`-basis of the Hodge space
   (`Module.Basis.exteriorPower` is in the pinned Mathlib).
6. **The final step.** `ψ = bcHodge` maps the basis `1 ⊗ ιMulti_family b` onto the basis
   `1 ⊗ ιMulti_family (π^*∘b)` and is `L`-semilinear, so it is injective. Both finranks equal
   `Nat.card (powersetCard I g)`, for every `g`.

### OmegaBaseChange (`omega_proof.lean.part`)

- **Step A** (`mem_of_forall_locSub`): `v ∈ N` as soon as, for every maximal `P`, some `d ∉ P` has
  `d • v ∈ N`.
- **Step B** (`omegaAt_bc_local`, conormal base change at a point `s'` of `Spec 𝓞_{L'}`). Every
  `v ∈ (ω_{𝒢'})_{s'}` has a multiple `d • v` with `d ∉ s'` lying in `𝓞_{L'}·π^*((ω_𝒢)_{s})`,
  where `s` is the image of `s'`.
  1. Apply `pullback_stalkGen` (infra-stalks) at `e'(s')`: `f'·w = c`, where `c` lies in the subring
     generated by `π^♯` and `G'^♯`.
  2. Such `c` are `G'^♯(γ) + u`, with `u` in the ideal generated by `π^♯(I_{e(s)})`.
  3. Applying `e'^♯` gives `γ = 0`.
  4. Coefficients from `𝒪_{𝒢',e'(s')}` act on `ω` through `(𝓞_{L'})_{s'}` (`exists_act`).
- **`OmegaLocalization`** (new named Prop, one group model, now proved as `omegaLocalization`):
  `∀ s, ∀ v ∈ omegaAt s, ∃ d ∉ s, d • v ∈ omega`. Take a generator `df` with `f ∈ I_{e(s)}`.
  - `f` extends over an open `W ∋ e(s)`, and `e^♯F = 0` on `e⁻¹W` by germ injectivity
    (`omegaAt_open`).
  - The points `s₁` with `e(s₁) ∉ W` form a finite set of closed points
    (`finite_of_isClosed_not_genericPt`, via the minimal primes of a nonzero ideal).
  - At each such `s₁`, the ideal `{d | d • df ∈ omegaAt s₁}` is nonzero (`omegaAt_full`: an affine
    chart, `𝒪_ξ = Γ(U)_𝔭`) and saturated (`omegaAt_sat`).
  - So it contains `𝔭_{s₁}^n` (DVR `R_{𝔭}`), hence an element `∉ s` (`exists_mem_not_mem_of_sat`).
    The product of these elements works.
- `omegaBaseChange_of_localization : OmegaLocalization → OmegaBaseChange`, and
  `omegaBaseChange := omegaBaseChange_of_localization omegaLocalization`.

## Remarks

- **`finrank` holds for every `g`.** The `finrank` clause of `HodgeBCInjective` is proved for all
  `g`, not only for `g = dim A`. In fact `finrank L (hodgeSpace g) = finrank L' (hodgeSpace' g)`.
  (VERIFIED: the proof shows both equal the same `Nat.card`.)

  the smoothness or separatedness instances of `GroupModel`. They use the unit section, the stalks
  of the fibre product (infra-stalks), and the fact that `𝓞_L` is Dedekind. This agrees with the
  model review recorded in height-bc's log.

  `H5Final.lean` elaborate unchanged under `import Mathlib` (VERIFIED).
