**The four leaves:**

- `ClassNumberOneB` is Eichler's class number one in adelic form: `(B⊗𝔸_f)^× = B^×·𝒪̂^×`.
- `ShimuraMainThmIDeligneY3AbU` is shimura-cft's leaf plus `ModuliAtUnits`, the moduli clause at every
  `u ∈ 𝒪̂^×`, describing the QM pair only. `mainThmI_of_U` recovers shimura-cft's leaf from it.

**The Deligne form alone is not enough, and the missing piece is stated.** As stated, the Deligne leaf describes
the moduli object only at `Ψ(τ, 1)`. Those points fill one of the two geometric components of `Y₃`
(`π₀ = (ℤ/3)^×`). So for CM points on the other component, the leaf cannot provide the CM dictionary that
reciprocity needs. The two printed facts above fill the gap: every point becomes `Ψ(τ, u)` with `u ∈ 𝒪̂^×`, where
the torus is `ℂ²/ι(𝒪)(τ,1)ᵀ`.
