# infra-smooth-dim (fleet note)

**Verdict.** The dimension theory of smooth schemes over a field is now in Lean, proved from Mathlib
v4.35 alone. Let `X → Spec K` be smooth of relative dimension `n` and `x` a closed point (for
example a `K`-point). Then `𝒪_{X,x}` is a regular local ring of Krull dimension `n`, and
`dim_{κ(x)} 𝔪_x/𝔪_x² = n`. If `X` is irreducible, `dim X = n`. Also proved: integral morphisms do
not raise dimension, and finite surjective morphisms (isogenies) preserve it.

axioms propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound (VERIFIED: `#print axioms`).
