# glue-ssbc (fleet summary)

**Result:** glue-I3-linalg's glue Prop `SemisimpleBaseChange` is **proved in Lean** (no sorry; axioms
propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound). In characteristic 0, a semisimple finite-dimensional
representation of any monoid stays semisimple after **any** field extension `E/k` (algebraic or not).
The statement is glue-I3-linalg's, verbatim.

**Proof:** the trace-form criterion. Let `A` be the span of `ρ(G)` in `End V`. Because `V` is semisimple
and faithful over `A`, `A` is a semisimple ring. Its trace form is nondegenerate: the radical of the form
is a left ideal, so it is generated by an idempotent, and that idempotent has trace equal to its rank,
which is nonzero in char 0 unless the idempotent is 0. Nondegeneracy passes to `A_E` by applying the
`k`-linear functionals `E → k` to the coefficients. Then the Jacobson radical of `A_E` is 0, because it
is nilpotent and so has trace 0 against `A_E`. So `A_E` is a semisimple ring and `E ⊗ V` is semisimple.
Mathlib v4.35 has no base-change theorem for semisimplicity (checked by grep).
