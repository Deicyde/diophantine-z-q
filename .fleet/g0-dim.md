# g0-dim (fleet note)

**Result: both pieces are proved.** `DimFinite` and `DimBaseChange` (pieces 1 and 2 of g0-split) are
theorems, proved from Mathlib v4.35.0 alone: no leaf, no new hypothesis, no `sorry`, axioms
`[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`. The statements are copied verbatim from
`scratch/g0-split/part_split.lean` (checked with `diff`). g0-split's reduction now needs 8 glue pieces
instead of 10: `HSyn.glueOutputs_of_leaves_dim` (in `scratch/g0-dim/part_dim.lean`).

**Proof.** The proofs are general, for any scheme locally of finite type over a field `K`. They use
neither the group structure nor smoothness.
- Commutative algebra: an integral injective extension has the same Krull dimension (lying over,
  going up, incomparability; this lemma is not in Mathlib). Then `dim (R ⊗_K L) = dim R` for `R` of
  finite type (Stacks 00P4), via Noether normalization, flatness of `L/K`, and the fact that finite
  maps stay finite under base change.
- Topology: `dim X = ⨆ dim Uᵢ` for a cover by open embeddings.
- Schemes: `dim (X ×_K L) = dim X`, using the affine cover and its pullback along `X_L → X`.
  `dim X ∈ ℕ` when `X` is also quasi-compact and nonempty (finite subcover; `A` is nonempty
  because of its unit section).
