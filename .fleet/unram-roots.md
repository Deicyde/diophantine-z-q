**How the proof goes.**
1. `UnramRoots` follows from `UnramProcyclic` (new Prop, now proved): `G_F/I_F` is procyclic and
   generated by a Frobenius lift.
2. Metric part (Mathlib only): metric inertia fixes the roots of unity of order prime to `p`. An
   element fixing every root of every `X^{p^m} − X` is in metric inertia (a Teichmüller argument
   through the residue field of `ℚ̄_p`).
3. Frobenius data from Tau Ceti:
   - the residue cardinality `q = p^f`;
   - a Frobenius lift, by lifting `maximalUnramifiedFrobenius`;
   - cyclicity at each level, from `zpowers_frobeniusAlgEquiv` on `unramifiedExtension F ℚ̄_p f`.
4. `UnramProcyclic` follows from the Frobenius data, using Mathlib's finite and infinite Galois
   correspondence.
