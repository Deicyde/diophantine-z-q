# hdec-adopt (fleet report)

**Result.** All seven clusters of the H-decomposition glue are now in the built project:
26 modules under `ZInQKernel/HDecomp/` (about 9,200 lines). `ZInQKernel.lean` imports them
through `ZInQKernel/HDecomp.lean`, so CI's `lake build ZInQKernel` checks them. The clusters:
- the shared abelian-variety layer (`Objects`);
- the Arakelov end (`Assembly`);
- descent (`Descent.*`, 8 modules);
- the conductor bound (`Conductor.*`: glue-conductor, glue-I6-rest, glue-d1-local);
- A6 (`A6.*`);
- C10 (`C10.*`);
- the repository glue (`Repo.*`).

**What compiles (VERIFIED).**
- `lake build ZInQKernel` exits 0 (00:48 PST).
- `lake env lean scripts/print_axioms.lean` prints 179 axiom lists, every one
  `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`. 88 of them are the new HDecomp entries.
- No `sorry`, no `axiom`, no line over 100 characters.
- Every module docstring says that it is glue of `docs/research/h-decomposition.md`, with the
  literature leaves as hypotheses (`def … : Prop`). The theorem statements are those of the
  drafts.

**Deduplication.** Six verbatim copies were replaced by imports:
- C10/Trace now uses pkg-I3 from `Objects`;
- D1Local now imports `Breaks`;
- ArtinUpper now gets pkg-I6 Part II from `I6Base`;
- `I6Base.exists_sq_eq_one_add` now calls Dyadic's;
- `Assembly` now imports `Hypotheses.JHeight` in place of F4Copy;
- A6's pkg-I2 copy, identical to `Objects` §B (checked by diff), is dropped and `PkgI2` opened.

The modules are generated by `scratch/hdec-adopt/adopt.py`, which reproduces them and also wraps
long lines.

**What remains.**
- Not adopted: `scratch/glue-assembly/{C8,E1Lin,E2Index,F3Glue,Leaves}.lean`. They are outside
  `GlueAssembly.lean`, which the task named.
- Not adopted: `scratch/glue-a6-alg/Quat.lean`. Its content is already inside `A6`.
- About 150 compiler warnings (deprecations, unused simp arguments) were left untouched.
