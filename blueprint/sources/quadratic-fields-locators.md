# Locators for the quadratic fields chapter

The facts about $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-n})$ in this chapter are standard and are not in the manuscript. Their
plan is step 1 of §3.2 of the project-authored research note `docs/research/redei-plan.md` of this
repository, written for this project and not a published source.

## The field

`docs/research/redei-plan.md` §3.2, step 1: $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-n})$ as a number field with two
automorphisms, the integrality of $\omega$ and of $\theta=(1+\omega)/2$ for $n\equiv3\pmod4$, the
norm $N(x)=x\sigma(x)\ge0$, $\mathcal O\cap\mathbb Q=\mathbb Z$ and the rational units $\pm1$,
$\sigma\omega=-\omega$.
