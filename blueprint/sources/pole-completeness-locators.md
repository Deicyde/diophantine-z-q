# Locators for the pole-completeness chapter

The derivation of pole completeness from the restricted converse is Section 5.1 of the manuscript
([Theorem 5.2](manuscript.md#theorem-52)). The facts about square classes and 2-descent in this chapter are
not in the manuscript. They are written in the project-authored notes `docs/research/pc-audit.md` and
`docs/research/nt-prop0.md` of this repository, notes written for this project and not published sources.

## RK9

`docs/research/pc-audit.md` §3, record RK9 (and §2.2): the bound $\#(A/2A)\ge8$ for a finitely generated
abelian group $A$ with four 2-torsion points and an element of infinite order, applied there to the rational
points of an elliptic curve with full rational 2-torsion. The squarefree integer representative of a rational
square class is `docs/research/nt-prop0.md` §1.3, item 2.

## Square classes

`docs/research/nt-prop0.md` §0: $a\equiv a'$ in $K^\times$ means $a/a'\in K^{\times2}$.

## Squares in the p-adic numbers

`docs/research/nt-prop0.md` §2.1, Lemma SQ: square classes of $\mathbb Q_p$ for odd $p$ and for
$p=2$, the perturbation rule (P) and the residue test (RT).
