# Locators for the decomposition of the minimal height hypothesis

The decomposition follows the project-authored note `docs/research/h-decomposition.md` and the fold
notes under `docs/research/` that changed it (section 3 of `docs/research/leaf-fold9.md` groups the
statements by the theory they come from), and the list `docs/one-proof/OPEN.md`, generated from the
build, which gives each statement's source, its kind and who is working on it. These are notes
written for this project, not published sources. The locators below are those of
`docs/one-proof/OPEN.md`. An audit of the citations against the primary sources by AI agents with
web access (`docs/research/locators.md`) read some of them, as marked; the others are unverified.

## The decomposition

The minimal height hypothesis is proved from the twenty-one statements of the chapter in the
project's assembly of the proof of the manuscript's Theorem 4.1; the trust assessment of that proof
is the project-authored note `docs/research/h-trust.md`.

## Pazuki bound

F. Pazuki, *Theta height and Faltings height*, Bull. SMF 140 (2012), Corollary 1.3(2), upper half,
read by the locator audit. The level-$2$ coordinates are a risk (project note height-split, §8).

## Deligne representations

(fourth printing, 2016: p. 404; AI-read), stated over $K_{f,\lambda}$, with their Definition 5.8.1
of normalized eigenforms; P. Deligne, Sém. Bourbaki 355 (1969); G. Shimura 1971, Theorem 7.14, for
$k=2$. The numbers of Definition 5.8.1 and of Shimura's theorem are unverified.
