# Z is Diophantine in Q

This blueprint formalizes Theorem 1.1 of the 11/5 manuscript *ℤ is Diophantine in ℚ*
(`diophantine_zq.tex` in this repository), with its deep arithmetic inputs as explicit
hypotheses and its finite computations checked by the Lean kernel.

- [Roadmap](roadmap/README.md) — the book: chapters, statements, and their
  dependencies.
- [Coverage](coverage/README.md) — what counts as done, and what is out of
  scope.
- [The manuscript, annotated](https://deicyde.github.io/diophantine-z-q/companion/pdf-overlay/viewer/index.html)
  — the 11/5 manuscript page by page, each passage linked to the Lean that
  states it. An unreviewed machine audit.
