# Ideal counts in quadratic fields

The Dedekind zeta function is the Dirichlet series of the number $a_K(n)$ of ideals of norm
$n$. In a quadratic field these counts are multiplicative, at a prime power $p^k$ they are
the partial sums of the powers of a sign $\varepsilon_p\in\{-1,0,1\}$, and for odd
$p\nmid d$ that sign is the Legendre symbol $(d/p)$. Comparing Euler products then factors
$\zeta_K$ as $\zeta$ times an $L$-function, up to finitely many local factors.
