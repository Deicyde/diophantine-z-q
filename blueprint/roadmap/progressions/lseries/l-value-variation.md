# Variation of $L(\sigma,\chi)$ near $1$

Let $q\ge1$, let $\chi$ be a nonprincipal Dirichlet character modulo $q$, and put
$M=\log q+3$. For every real $\sigma$ with $1\le\sigma\le2$,
$$|L(\sigma,\chi)-L(1,\chi)|\le(\sigma-1)M^2 .$$

**Proof sketch.** The function $t\mapsto L(t,\chi)$ on $[1,\sigma]$ is the restriction of an
entire function, so it is differentiable with derivative $L'(t,\chi)$, of absolute value at
most $M^2$ on $[1,2]$. The mean value inequality for vector-valued functions on a segment
gives the bound.
