# Roots of unity in a quadratic field

Let $d$ be a squarefree integer with $d\ne1$ and $K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt d)$. The group of roots
of unity of $K$ has order $w_K\le6$.

**Proof sketch.** The roots of unity of $K$ form a finite cyclic group; let $\zeta$ generate
it, of order $w_K$. Then $\mathbb Q(\zeta)\subseteq K$ has degree $\varphi(w_K)$ dividing
$2$, so $\varphi(w_K)\le2$, which forces $w_K\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$.
