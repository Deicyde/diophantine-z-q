# The regulator of an imaginary quadratic field

Let $d<0$ be a squarefree integer and $K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt d)$. The unit group of
$\mathcal O_K$ has rank $0$, and the regulator of $K$ is $R_K=1$.

**Proof sketch.** By the signature, $r_1+r_2-1=0$, so Dirichlet's unit theorem gives rank
$0$, and the regulator is the covolume of the zero lattice in a space of dimension $0$
(the determinant of an empty matrix), which is $1$.
