# Minimality of $C_d$ at $2$ for even squarefree $d$

Notation of [the places of $\mathbb Q$](../../l-function-cd/completion.md) and
[minimal Weierstrass equations](../../l-function-cd/minimal-models.md); $C_{d,v}$ is
[the curve $C_d$ over $\mathbb Q_v$](../../l-function-cd/cd-local.md).

1. (Minimality from $c_4$.) Let $R$ be a discrete valuation ring with maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$
   and fraction field $K$, and $W$ a Weierstrass equation with coefficients in $R$. If there are no
   $r\in\mathfrak m$ and Weierstrass equation $W'$ with coefficients in $R$ such that
   $c_4(W)=r^4c_4(W')$, then $W$, viewed over $K$, is minimal over $R$.
2. For a finite place $v$ of $\mathbb Q$ and $x\in\mathcal O_v$: $x\in\mathfrak m_v$ exactly when
   $p_v$ divides $x$ in $\mathcal O_v$. Moreover $2\ne0$ in $\mathcal O_v$.
3. If $p_v=2$, then every Weierstrass equation $W$ over $\mathcal O_v$ has $c_4(W)=16X$ for some
   $X\in\mathcal O_v$, or $c_4(W)\notin\mathfrak m_v$.
4. If $p_v=2$ and $e$ is an odd natural number, then $C_{2e,v}$ is minimal over $\mathcal O_v$.

**Proof sketch.** (1) $W$ is integral. Let $C$ be a change of variables, with scaling factor $u$,
such that $C\cdot W$ is integral. If $v(u)\le0$, then
$v(\Delta(C\cdot W))=v(\Delta(W))-12v(u)\ge v(\Delta(W))$. Otherwise $v(u)>0$, so $u$ is the image
of some $r\in\mathfrak m$; writing $C\cdot W=W'$ with $W'$ integral, $c_4(W')=u^{-4}c_4(W)$ gives
$c_4(W)=r^4c_4(W')$, which is excluded.

(2) $\mathfrak m_v$ is generated by $p_v$; $\mathcal O_v$ has characteristic $0$.

(3) If $a_1\in\mathfrak m_v$, write $a_1=2t$; then
$c_4=b_2^2-24b_4=16\bigl((t^2+a_2)^2-3(a_4+ta_3)\bigr)$. Otherwise, if $c_4\in\mathfrak m_v$, the
identity $a_1^4=c_4-2(4a_1^2a_2+8a_2^2-24a_4-12a_1a_3)$ puts $a_1^4$, hence $a_1$, in
$\mathfrak m_v$.

(4) $C_{2e,v}$ comes from the model over $\mathcal O_v$, with $c_4=48(2e)^2=16\cdot12e^2$. Suppose
$c_4=r^4c_4(W')$ with $r=2s\in\mathfrak m_v$ and $W'$ integral, so $12e^2=s^4c_4(W')$. By (3),
either $c_4(W')=16X$, and then $3e^2=2\cdot2s^4X$ is divisible by $2$; or $c_4(W')\notin\mathfrak m_v$,
and then $s\in\mathfrak m_v$ since $2\mid12e^2$, so $s=2s'$ and $3e^2=2\cdot2s'^4c_4(W')$. Both
contradict $3e^2$ odd. Apply (1).
