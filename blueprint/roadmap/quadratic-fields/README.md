# The quadratic fields $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-n})$

The manuscript's Section 4 meets imaginary quadratic fields in its treatment of CM fibers (L581-631). The
development models such a field as $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-n})=\mathbb Q[\omega]/(\omega^2+n)$ for a natural number
$n\ge1$, and its Lean on ring class fields and Shimura's reciprocity law at CM points is written over this model.
The pages below define the field and prove facts about its automorphisms, its ring of integers $\mathcal O$ and
its norm. These facts are standard and are not in the manuscript.
