# Proposition F.5: the arithmetic realization of the coefficient rules

Fix type data for primes (types read from residue classes, with sign coordinate $(p-1)/2\bmod2$), a
reading $R$ of finite sets of good primes, integers $a_p$, and a character of $G_{\mathbb Q}$ that reads
the type of each good prime at its Frobenius elements. Assume a trace model of the source within the
vertex budget $W+1$ (`GaloisTraceModel`, named inputs, not proved): a vector space $M$ over
$\mathbb F_2$, the reduced integral trace module, with operators $t(g)$ and $d(g)$ for
$g\in G_{\mathbb Q}$, forms $F_U$ (the reductions of $\theta H_U$, $\theta=1+2\sum q^{m^2}$) and
coefficient-one functionals $\mathrm{read}_U$ for the sets $U$ of good primes in the budget, and genuine
Frobenius elements at the good primes, such that:

- $t(g)t(h)=t(gh)+d(h)t(gh^{-1})$, $t(1)=2$ and $t$ is a class function (GLUE: the trace identities of
  the two-dimensional constituents);
- $d$ is trivial on the trace orbit of $F_U$, and any two traces kill $F_U$ (ELLIOT);
- an element trivial on the type character and on the characters of $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{s^*})$,
  $s\in U$, lies in the subgroup generated by the squares and by $N_U$, the elements $n$ with trivial
  type and characters on $U$ such that $t(gn)F_{U'}=t(g)F_{U'}$ for all $g$ and $U'\subseteq U$; and
  $N_U$ has finite index (GLUE: Kummer theory and continuity);
- every class of $G_{\mathbb Q}/N_U$ is the Frobenius class of good primes outside any finite set
  (PRINT, Chebotarev);
- the reading of a fresh prime $q$ is $\mathrm{read}_U(t(\mathrm{Fr}_q)F_U)$, and
  [(O.12)](equation-o-12-inertia-identity.md) holds at coefficient one (ELLIOT);

and assume $a_p$ even at every good prime. Then the symbol (the reading of any prime realization of a
typed graph) satisfies the coefficient rules of [Lemma 7.6](../section-7/lemma-7-6-coefficient-rules.md)
on typed graphs with at most $W+1$ vertices; with a model at every budget it satisfies them on all typed
graphs. In particular it depends only on the typed graph, a pair difference contracts the endpoints, and
an isolated primary vertex gives zero.

The Lean statement does not contain the integer divisions (O.9)–(O.11) or the fresh coefficient-one
reading as conclusions: the forms $F_U$ of the model stand for the halved forms after reduction, and the
reading is a field. The divisions are proved separately for an abstract coefficient source:
[(O.9)](equation-o-9-divided-operators.md), [(O.10)](equation-o-10-shell-parity.md),
[(O.11)](equation-o-11-halved-operator.md). As in the manuscript, nothing is asserted beyond the budget
or at an empty contraction output.

The ELLIOT fields `det_orbit` and `two_fresh` are the manuscript's trace-orbit argument (tex
9719–9745). Its passage at tex 9725–9729 from the coefficientwise identity to the abstract residual
orbit is recorded as an error (`docs/research/rc-trace-limits.md`). The leaf does not rely on that
passage: the model's module may be $\Lambda$ modulo its largest operator-stable submodule inside the
kernel of coefficientwise reduction, where both identities follow from the coefficientwise ones
(`GaloisConstituentModel.toGaloisDensityModel`, with `det_orbit'` and `two_fresh'`). No saturation is
needed there; saturation is the separate hypothesis `Saturated`, used only by the bridge
`ConstituentModelRel`. The coefficientwise `det_orbit` is proved (`detOrbit_coefficient`) only for
`TraceLimits.buildConstituentModel`, which by its docstring is not a discharge for the genuine
added-prime packets.

In the reduction of Theorem 5.3 the theorems of this page are unused:
`GaloisTraceModel.rulesUpTo_of_model` and `coefficientRules_of_galois` are not on its path. The leaf path is `PostLeaves.address`, then
`AddressDensityLeaf`, then `OddDensityModels`, whose models are `GaloisDensityModel`s, fields of
[the remaining hypotheses](../remaining-hypotheses.md), in which Chebotarev's theorem and the finite
index are proved from the openness of $N_U$ and its unramifiedness at the good primes outside $U$ and a
finite set (GLUE) by the density theorem. For the odd source `oddReadings_of_galois` turns the models
into the readings (in `addressGenuineLeaf_of_densityLeaf` and `addressPackageStagedLeaf_of_galois`),
and `rulesUpTo_stageSymbol`, the form of Lemma 7.6 that takes readings, gives the rules for the odd
stage symbols within each budget (in `addressPackageStaged_of_genuine`). For the transported even
source `evenReadings_of_galois` gives the even readings (in `addressPackageStagedLeaf_of_galois`), and
`CoefficientDictionary.coefficientRules_symb` gives the rules at every budget (in
`addressPackageStaged_of_genuine`). The evenness of $a_p$ comes from $a_p\equiv p+1\pmod4$, proved
for the actual curves.

**Proof sketch.** Two traces kill $F_U$ and $d$ is trivial on its trace orbit, so the trace identity
gives $t(gh^2)F_U=t(g)F_U$: the map $g\mapsto t(g)F_U$ is invariant under squares and, by Kummer theory,
factors through the elementary quadratic quotient cut out by the type and the characters of $U$. With
Chebotarev, the reading of a fresh prime depends only on its type and its bits with $U$; a fresh vertex
with trivial label reads like $t(1)F_U=2F_U=0$; and (O.12) at coefficient one, realized by fresh primes,
gives the product-label rule. These are the readings of Lemma 7.6, which gives the rules.
