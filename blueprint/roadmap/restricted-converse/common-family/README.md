# Coefficient symbols, the genus bound and the common family

Sections 7.3, 7.4 and 7.6 of the manuscript, with the coefficient rules of Appendix F and the rest of
Appendix G.

Coefficients of a fixed source are read through $\mathbb F_2$-valued symbols on graphs that record quadratic
residue data of the primes of the index. The rules these symbols obey are identities of the actual sources
(Appendix F). Appendix G's main result is the address construction: grounded networks over $\mathbb F_2$ and a
degree count that make one or two symbols equal to one at every nonzero address of a binary cube at once.

The manuscript fixes the order in which these tools are used (Appendix G). The even-source case comes first. With
a Boolean-degree count from Section 9, the address construction bounds $v_2L_*$ above at partitionable
parameters, which gives a companion of bounded size for every simple-zero parameter, and through the explicit
Gross–Zagier formula and the uniform bound of Section 9 the lower bound for the genus index.

Only after the genus bound is the odd minimum fixed; then the odd case of the rules and the two-symbol address
construction are applied. If the odd symbol is a unit at the empty configuration, Theorem 5.3 follows at once.
Otherwise a companion field is fixed and both coefficient tests are met on one binary family.
