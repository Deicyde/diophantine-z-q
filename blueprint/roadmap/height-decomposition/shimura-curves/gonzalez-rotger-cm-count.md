# González–Rotger: the number of CM points on X₆

The *González–Rotger count* is the following assertion. Let $(Y,X)$ be a moduli pair, $k$ an
imaginary quadratic field and $R$ an order of $k$ whose conductor is prime to $6$, and suppose that
some QM abelian surface over $\overline{\mathbb Q}$ has CM by $R$. Then the number of points
$x\in X(\overline{\mathbb Q})$ whose QM abelian surface has CM by $R$ is $2^{b}h(R)$, where $b$ is
the number of primes dividing $6$ that are inert in $k$ and $h(R)$ is the order of the Picard group
of $R$.

It is a published statement: the printed theorem, or a direct instance of it that needs no argument.
This is Proposition 5.6 of González and Rotger at $(D,N)=(6,1)$, stated for every moduli pair; it
asserts nothing about the existence of one. The hypothesis that $R$ occurs is the moduli form of the
printed hypothesis that $R$ embeds optimally in $\mathcal O$. The proposition was read by the
locator audit, but its wording has not been checked against the paper by the statement review: a
reader with the paper should confirm that their $D(R)$ is the product of the primes $p\mid6$ with
$(R/p)=-1$. It is a hypothesis, not proved here.
