# González–Rotger: the number of CM points on X₆

The *González–Rotger count* is the following assertion. Let $(Y,X)$ be a moduli pair, $k$ an
imaginary quadratic field and $R$ an order of $k$ whose conductor is prime to $6$, and suppose that
some QM abelian surface over $\overline{\mathbb Q}$ has CM by $R$. Then the number of points
$x\in X(\overline{\mathbb Q})$ whose QM abelian surface has CM by $R$ is $2^{b}h(R)$, where $b$ is
the number of primes dividing $6$ that are inert in $k$ and $h(R)$ is the order of the Picard group
of $R$.

It is not printed in this form but follows from published theorems; reaching it from the print takes
further theorems, which are not proved here. It is Proposition 5.6 of González and Rotger at
$(D,N)=(6,1)$, read on every moduli pair; it asserts nothing about the existence of one. Their count
is of optimal embeddings of $R$ in $\mathcal O$ modulo its norm-one units; reading it as a count of
QM abelian surfaces over $\overline{\mathbb Q}$ with CM by $R$, without polarization, takes
Shimura's theorem of 1967 and the descent of CM pairs. The proposition was read by the locator
audit, but its wording has not been checked against the paper by the statement review: a reader with
the paper should confirm that their $D(R)$ is the product of the primes $p\mid6$ with $(R/p)=-1$. It
is a hypothesis, not proved here.
