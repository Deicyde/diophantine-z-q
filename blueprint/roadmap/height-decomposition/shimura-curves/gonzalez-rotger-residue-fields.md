# González–Rotger: residue fields of CM points on X₆

The *González–Rotger statement on residue fields* is the following assertion. Let $(Y,X)$ be any
moduli pair, $k$ an imaginary quadratic field, $R$ an order of $k$ whose conductor is prime to $6$,
and $x\in X(\overline{\mathbb Q})$ a CM point by $R$ in the sense of González and Rotger: the
complex torus of its QM abelian surface is $\mathbb C^2/\iota(\mathcal O)(z,1)^{T}$ for a point $z$
of the upper half-plane fixed by an optimal embedding of $R$ into $\mathcal O$. If $2$ or $3$ is
inert in $k$, the residue field $\mathbb Q(x)$ has degree $2h(R)$ over $\mathbb Q$ and contains a
copy of $k$; otherwise it has degree $h(R)$. Here $h(R)$ is the order of the Picard group of $R$.

The project classes it as a published statement; its reading against the print is still pending.
Theorem 5.12 of González and Rotger (2006) gives the residue field of a CM point of $X_0(6,1)$ as
the ring class field of $R$ or a subfield of index two; the statement keeps the degree and the
embedding of $k$, and carries the points over to any moduli pair. It asserts nothing about the
existence of a moduli pair, which comes from the statement on
[coarse moduli](x6-coarse-moduli.md) and [Shimura's Main Theorem I](shimura-main-theorem-y3.md).
It is a hypothesis, not proved here.
