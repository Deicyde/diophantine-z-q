# The integral model of Y₃ over ℤ[1/30]

The *integral model statement* is the following assertion. Over a scheme $S$, consider abelian
schemes $\mathcal A$ of relative dimension $2$ with a ring homomorphism
$\mathcal O\to\operatorname{End}_S(\mathcal A)$ and a full $\mathcal O$-linear level-$3$ structure:
an $\mathcal O$-linear homomorphism $\mathcal O/3\mathcal O\to\mathcal A(S)$ that is a bijection
onto $\mathcal A_s[3]$ at every geometric point $s$ of $S$. There are a proper smooth curve
$\mathcal Y$ over $\mathbb Z[1/30]$ and such an abelian scheme $\mathcal A$ over $\mathcal Y$ that
is universal on locally noetherian schemes over $\mathbb Z[1/30]$: for every locally noetherian
$\mathbb Z[1/30]$-scheme $S$ and every such $\mathcal A'$ over $S$ there is exactly one morphism
$g:S\to\mathcal Y$ over $\mathbb Z[1/30]$ for which $\mathcal A'$ is isomorphic to $g^*\mathcal A$
compatibly with the actions of $\mathcal O$ and the level structures.

It is not printed in this form but follows from published theorems; reaching it from the print takes
further theorems, which are not proved here. This is the manuscript's own claim, which it derives
from Buzzard's Theorem 2.1 and Corollary 2.3 at the level $V_1(5)$ over $\mathbb Z[1/30]$, the
finite étale sheaf of $\mathcal O$-linear isomorphisms $\mathcal O/3\mathcal O\cong A[3]$, the free
action of $(\mathcal O/5\mathcal O)^\times$ and descent. No print states it at level $3$: Buzzard
excludes level structure at the primes dividing the discriminant. The statement asks for a proper
curve where the manuscript has a projective one, tests the universal property only on locally
noetherian bases, and omits the polarization; the two forms of the moduli problem agree through the
canonical principal polarization, an argument made on the spot and not proved here. That the generic
fibre of $\mathcal Y$ represents the same problem over $\mathbb Q$, and so is the curve $Y_3$ of
this chapter, is proved here. It is a hypothesis, not proved here.
