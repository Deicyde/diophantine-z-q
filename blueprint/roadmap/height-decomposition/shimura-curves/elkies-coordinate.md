# Elkies' coordinate on X₆/W

The *Elkies statement* is the following assertion. For every moduli pair $(Y,X)$ there is a rational
function $t$ on $X$ such that: if $X$ is integral and $t$ is defined at its generic point, the field
of rational functions on $X$ fixed by $W$, acting through its moduli action, is $\mathbb Q(t)$; $t$
takes the value $0$ at the points whose QM abelian surface has CM by the order of discriminant
$-24$, the value $1$ at those with CM of discriminant $-4$, and the value $\infty$ at those with CM
of discriminant $-3$; and $X$ is covered, over the $t$-line, by Elkies' charts $s^2=-t$ and
$r^2=3(t-1)$.

The project classes it as a published statement; its reading against the print is still pending.
Elkies normalizes $t$ on $X_6/W$ by its values at the three elliptic points and gives the two
equations; he works on the canonical model, and the statement carries his description over to any
moduli pair. An AI reading of his paper found the three CM discriminants at $t=0,1,\infty$ as stated
here; no person has read it. The statement asserts nothing about the existence of a moduli pair,
which comes from the statement on [coarse moduli](x6-coarse-moduli.md) and
[Shimura's Main Theorem I](shimura-main-theorem-y3.md). It is a hypothesis, not proved here.
