# Shimura's Main Theorem I on Y₃ in Deligne's form

The *Shimura–Deligne statement on $Y_3$* is the following assertion. Let $K_3$ be the level-$3$
subgroup of $(B\otimes\mathbb A_f)^\times$ and $\widehat{\mathcal O}^\times$ the units of the
completion of $\mathcal O$. There is a fine moduli scheme $Y_3$ over $\mathbb Q$ for QM abelian
surfaces with level-$3$ structure, with its moduli properties stated for abelian schemes, with a
bijection
$$B^\times_+\backslash\bigl(\mathfrak H\times(B\otimes\mathbb A_f)^\times\bigr)/K_3\;\cong\;Y_3(\mathbb C),
\qquad(\tau,u)\mapsto\Psi(\tau,u),$$
holomorphic in $\tau$, such that for every $u\in\widehat{\mathcal O}^\times$ the QM abelian surface
at $\Psi(\tau,u)$ is $\mathbb C^2/\iota(\mathcal O)(\tau,1)^{T}$, and Shimura's reciprocity law
holds at the CM points.

No single source prints it. It combines Shimura's Main Theorem I (1967), his reciprocity law at CM
points, with Deligne's adelic form of the canonical model and the fine moduli of QM abelian surfaces
with level-$3$ structure. It is a hypothesis, not proved here.
