# Complex QM abelian surfaces carry a compatible principal polarization

Every abelian surface over $\mathbb C$ with an action of $\mathcal O$ and a full level-$3$ structure
has a principal polarization whose Rosati involution induces $x\mapsto\mu^{-1}\bar x\mu$ on
$\mathcal O$, with $\mu=2j+ij$.

It is a published statement: the printed theorem, or a direct instance of it that needs no argument.
It is Boutot and Carayol's Proposition (1.5) over $\mathbb C$, at the level $U(3)$ and for their
choice $t=\mu$ (III.0.5). A polarization is read as a principal Riemann form on the period lattice
(Appell–Humbert). The article cites the existence of a polarization up to a rational multiple and
sketches the proof that it can be taken principal; uniqueness, which it also states, is not used. It
is a hypothesis, not proved here.
