# X₀(6,1) as a coarse moduli scheme

The *coarse moduli statement* is the following assertion. There is a proper smooth curve $X$ over
$\mathbb Q$, with a $\overline{\mathbb Q}$-point, such that: $X(\overline{\mathbb Q})$ is in
bijection with the isomorphism classes of QM abelian surfaces over $\overline{\mathbb Q}$,
compatibly with the action of $\operatorname{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb Q}/\mathbb Q)$; every
automorphism of the ring $\mathcal O$ acts on these classes through an automorphism of $X$ over
$\mathbb Q$; and every QM abelian scheme with a level-$3$ structure over a proper smooth curve $S$
over $\mathbb Q$ has a classifying morphism $S\to X$, which sends each $\overline{\mathbb Q}$-point
of $S$ to the class of its fibre without the level structure.

No single source prints it. It combines Shimura's canonical model of $X_0(6,1)$, Deligne's moduli
interpretation, and the description of the curve as a coarse moduli space of QM abelian surfaces
(Buzzard); the statement takes the surfaces without polarization and the Atkin–Lehner involutions as
twists by automorphisms of $\mathcal O$. With [Shimura's Main Theorem I](shimura-main-theorem-y3.md)
it gives, here, a moduli pair. It is a hypothesis, not proved here.
