# The isogeny theorem of Gaudron and Rémond

The *isogeny statement* is the following assertion. Let $A$ and $B$ be isogenous abelian varieties
over a number field $K$ of degree $d$, with $\dim A=g$. Then there are isogenies $A\to B$ and
$B\to A$ of degree at most
$$\kappa=\Bigl((14g)^{64g^2}\,d\,\max\bigl(h_F(A),\log d,1\bigr)^2\Bigr)^{1024g^3}.$$

It is not printed in this form but follows from published theorems; reaching it from the print takes
further theorems, which are not proved here. The height in the statement is the project's coordinate
form of the stable Faltings height, identified with the published one as for
[Bost's lower bound](bost-lower-bound.md). It is a hypothesis, not proved here.
