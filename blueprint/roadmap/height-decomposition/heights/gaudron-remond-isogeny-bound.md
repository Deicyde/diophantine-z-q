# The isogeny theorem of Gaudron and Rémond

The *isogeny statement* is the following assertion. Let $A$ and $B$ be isogenous abelian varieties
over a number field $K$ of degree $d$, with $\dim A=g$, and let $h_F(A)$ be a stable Faltings height
of $A$. Then there are isogenies $A\to B$ and $B\to A$ of degree at most
$$\kappa=\Bigl((14g)^{64g^2}\,d\,\max\bigl(h_F(A),\log d,1\bigr)^2\Bigr)^{1024g^3}.$$

It is a published statement: the printed theorem, or a direct instance of it that needs no argument.
It is Theorem 1.4 of Gaudron and Rémond (2014) for isogenies over the base field, with the stable
height in Faltings' original normalization, built as for [Bost's lower bound](bost-lower-bound.md).
The project's earlier form, over its coordinate form of the height, is proved here from this one.
It is a hypothesis, not proved here.
