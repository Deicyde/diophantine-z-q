# The isogeny theorem of Gaudron and Rémond

The *isogeny statement* is the following assertion. Let $A$ and $B$ be isogenous abelian varieties
over a number field $K$ of degree $d$, with $\dim A=g$. Then there are isogenies $A\to B$ and
$B\to A$ of degree at most
$$\kappa=\Bigl((14g)^{64g^2}\,d\,\max\bigl(h_F(A),\log d,1\bigr)^2\Bigr)^{1024g^3}.$$

It is a published statement: the printed theorem, or a direct instance of it that needs no argument.
It is a hypothesis, not proved here.
