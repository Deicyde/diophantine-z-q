# Javanpeykar's bound for curves with a Belyi function

The *Javanpeykar statement* is the following assertion. Let $X$ be a smooth proper geometrically
connected curve over a number field $K$ with a $K$-rational point, $J$ its Jacobian, of dimension
$g\ge1$, and $t$ a Belyi function on $X$ over $K$ (a rational function unramified outside
$0,1,\infty$) of degree $d$. Then
$$h_F(J)\le13\cdot10^6\,g\,d^5.$$

It is not printed in this form but follows from published theorems; reaching it from the print takes
further theorems, which are not proved here. Reaching it from the print takes the identification of
the Jacobian with its Albanese model (Milne, *Jacobian varieties*, 6.1, with base change), the
equality of its dimension with the genus (Milne, 2.1), and the passage from a Belyi function on $X$
to a Belyi map of $X_{\overline{\mathbb Q}}$. It is a hypothesis, not proved here.
