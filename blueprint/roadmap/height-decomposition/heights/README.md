# Faltings 1983 and Faltings heights

Faltings' theorems of 1983 on Tate modules, four bounds for the stable Faltings height of an
abelian variety over a number field, and Chevalley's structure theorem, which the comparison of two
forms of that height uses. Heights are in Faltings' normalization $h_F$; Deligne's
normalization is $h_D=h_F+\frac g2\log\pi$ for an abelian variety of dimension $g$. Three of the
bounds are stated over the stable Faltings height as the literature builds it, from a Néron model
with semistable reduction and the metric on its invariant differentials; that this construction
agrees with the published definitions is a judgement of AI reviews.

Of these seven statements, the project classes five as published statements and two as
consequences of published theorems; none is proved here.
