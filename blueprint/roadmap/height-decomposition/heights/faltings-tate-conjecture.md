# Faltings' theorem on Tate's conjecture

The *Tate statement* is the following assertion. For abelian varieties $A$ and $B$ over a number
field $K$ and a prime $\ell$, the natural map
$\operatorname{Hom}_K(A,B)\otimes\mathbb Z_\ell\to\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb Z_\ell}(T_\ell A,T_\ell B)$
is injective, with image the $G_K$-equivariant maps.

It is a published statement: the printed theorem, or a direct instance of it that needs no argument.
It is a hypothesis, not proved here.
