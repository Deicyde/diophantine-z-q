# The minimal height hypothesis from the literature

This chapter proves the [minimal height hypothesis](../main/six-contact-min.md) from twenty-nine
statements: theorems from the literature, some of them specialized, weakened or combined. The
manuscript's route to the hypothesis uses twenty-three research-level theorems, thirty-five textbook
theorems and twenty glue steps; the project has split these into smaller statements, proved many of
them, and replaced others by published statements from which it derives them. What remains is listed
here, grouped by the theory each statement comes from; the groups follow the project's own
classification, and their boundaries are a matter of convenience.

Of the twenty-nine statements from the literature, thirteen are published statements: the printed
theorem, or a direct instance of it that needs no argument. Eleven are consequences of published
theorems, which take further theorems to reach from the print, and five combine several published
theorems. The project's strict convention, which counts a published statement only once its reading
against the print is checked, counts eleven of the twenty-nine as published. None of the twenty-nine
is proved here, and every step from them to the minimal height hypothesis is.
