# The minimal height hypothesis from the literature

This chapter proves the [minimal height hypothesis](../main/six-contact-min.md) from thirty-one
statements: theorems from the literature, some of them specialized, weakened or combined. The
manuscript's route to the hypothesis uses twenty-three research-level theorems, thirty-five textbook
theorems and twenty glue steps; the project has split these into smaller statements, proved many of
them, and replaced others by published statements from which it derives them. What remains is listed
here, grouped by the theory each statement comes from; the groups follow the project's own
classification, and their boundaries are a matter of convenience.

Of the thirty-one statements, the project classes twenty-three as published statements, some of
them specialized or weakened; five are consequences of published theorems, which take further
theorems to reach from the print; and three combine several published theorems. The project's
strict convention, which counts a published statement only once its reading against the print is
checked, counts fifteen of the thirty-one as published. AI raters assigned these classes, and no
person has yet confirmed a statement against its source. None of the thirty-one is proved here. The
list changes as statements are proved or restated; `docs/one-proof/OPEN.md` is the current one.
Every step from the thirty-one statements to the minimal height hypothesis is proved here.
