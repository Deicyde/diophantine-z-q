# The minimal height hypothesis from the literature

This chapter proves the [minimal height hypothesis](../main/six-contact-min.md) from forty-five
statements: forty-four theorems from the literature, some of them specialized, weakened or combined,
and one step of the project's own argument. The manuscript's route to the hypothesis uses
twenty-three research-level theorems, thirty-five textbook theorems and twenty glue steps; the
project has split these into smaller statements, proved many of them, and replaced others by
published statements from which it derives them. What remains is listed here, grouped by the theory
each statement comes from; the groups follow the project's own classification, and their boundaries
are a matter of convenience.

Of the forty-four statements from the literature, thirty-four are published statements: the printed
theorem, or a direct instance of it that needs no argument. Six are consequences of published
theorems, which take further theorems to reach from the print, and four combine several published
theorems. The remaining statement,
[local inertia of tame covers of the line](local-inertia-of-tame-covers.md), has no published form.
None of the forty-five is proved here, and every step from them to the minimal height hypothesis is.
