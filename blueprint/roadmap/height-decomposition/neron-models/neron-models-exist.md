# Néron models over Dedekind domains

The *Néron existence statement* is the following assertion. Let $D$ be a Dedekind domain that is not
a field, with fraction field $L$, and $A$ an abelian variety over $L$. Then there is a smooth,
separated, quasi-compact scheme $N$ over $D$ with generic fibre $A$ such that, for every smooth
$D$-scheme $Y$, every morphism $Y_L\to A$ extends to a morphism $Y\to N$.

It is a published statement: the printed theorem, or a direct instance of it that needs no argument.
Its reading against the print or its review is not complete, and until both are the project's strict
count does not count it as published. It asks only for the existence of the extension, which is
unique since $Y$ is flat over $D$ and $N$ is separated, and it is stated over an affine base; the
group structure of the Néron model is not asked for. It is a hypothesis, not proved here.
