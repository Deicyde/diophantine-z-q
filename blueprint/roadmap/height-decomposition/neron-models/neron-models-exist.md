# Néron models over Dedekind domains

The *Néron existence statement* is the following assertion. Let $D$ be a Dedekind domain with
fraction field $L$ and $A$ an abelian variety over $L$. Then there is a smooth, separated,
quasi-compact scheme $N$ over $D$ with generic fibre $A$ such that, for every smooth $D$-scheme $Y$,
every morphism $Y_L\to A$ extends to a morphism $Y\to N$.

It is a published statement: the printed theorem, or a direct instance of it that needs no argument.
It asks only for the existence of the extension, which is unique since $Y$ is flat over $D$ and $N$
is separated, and it is stated over an affine base; the group structure of the Néron model is not
asked for. It is a hypothesis, not proved here.
