# Néron models over rings of integers

The *Néron existence statement* is the following assertion. Let $A$ be an abelian variety over a
field $K$ and $L$ a number field containing $K$, with ring of integers $\mathcal O_L$. Then there is
a smooth, separated, quasi-compact scheme $N$ over $\mathcal O_L$ with generic fibre $A_L$ such
that, for every smooth $\mathcal O_L$-scheme $Y$, every morphism $Y_L\to A_L$ extends to a morphism
$Y\to N$.

It is a published statement: the printed theorem, or a direct instance of it that needs no argument.
It asks only for the existence of the extension, which is unique since $Y$ is flat over
$\mathcal O_L$ and $N$ is separated, so it is weaker than the existence of Néron models. It is a
hypothesis, not proved here.
