# Semistable reduction persists under base change

The *base-change statement for semistable reduction* is the following assertion. Let
$\mathcal O\to\mathcal O'$ be a local extension of strictly henselian discrete valuation rings whose
fraction fields $K\subset K'$ form a finite extension, and $A$ an abelian variety over $K$ with
semistable reduction, with Néron models $N$ over $\mathcal O$ and $N'$ of $A_{K'}$ over
$\mathcal O'$. Let $c:N\times_{\mathcal O}\mathcal O'\to N'$ be a homomorphism of group schemes that
is an isomorphism on generic fibres. Then $c$ restricted to $N^0\times_{\mathcal O}\mathcal O'$, the
preimage of the identity component $N^0$, is an open immersion with image the identity component
$N'^0$.

It is not printed in this form but follows from published theorems; reaching it from the print takes
further theorems, which are not proved here. The print is Corollaire 3.3 of SGA 7 I, Exposé IX; the statement specializes its base, passes
between the two descriptions of the Néron model, and allows any comparison morphism in place of the
canonical one.
Its reading against the print is pending, and until it is checked the project's strict count does
not count it as published. SGA 7 IX 3.3 may assume an algebraically closed residue field; then the
print is the restriction of this statement to such rings. It is a hypothesis, not proved here.
