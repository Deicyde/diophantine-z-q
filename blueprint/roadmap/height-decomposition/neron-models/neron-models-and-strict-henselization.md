# Néron models commute with strict henselization

The *strict henselization statement* is the following assertion. Let $\mathcal O\to\mathcal O'$ be
an unramified extension of discrete valuation rings, with fraction fields $K\subset K'$, such that
$\mathcal O'$ is strictly henselian and its residue field is separable over that of $\mathcal O$,
and let $A$ be an abelian variety over $K$ with a Néron model $N$ over $\mathcal O$. Then
$N\times_{\mathcal O}\mathcal O'$ is a Néron model of $A_{K'}$.

It is a published statement: the printed theorem, or a direct instance of it that needs no argument.
The hypotheses make $\mathcal O'$ a strict henselization of $\mathcal O$; the commutative algebra
behind this is proved here. It is a hypothesis, not proved here.
