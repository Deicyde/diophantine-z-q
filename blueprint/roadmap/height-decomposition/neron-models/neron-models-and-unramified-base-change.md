# Néron models commute with unramified base change

The *base-change statement for Néron models* is the following assertion. Let $\mathcal O\subset K$
and $\mathcal O'\subset K'$ be discrete valuation rings with $\mathcal O'\cap K=\mathcal O$, such
that $K'/K$ is separable algebraic, a uniformizer of $\mathcal O$ is a uniformizer of $\mathcal O'$,
and the residue field of $\mathcal O'$ is separable over that of $\mathcal O$. Let $A$ be an abelian
variety over $K$ with a Néron model $N$ over $\mathcal O$. Then $N\times_{\mathcal O}\mathcal O'$ is
a Néron model of $A_{K'}$.

It is a published statement: the printed theorem, or a direct instance of it that needs no argument.
Its reading against the print is pending, and until it is checked the project's strict count does
not count it as published. The print is stated for every smooth group scheme of finite type and, as
recalled, for any extension $K'/K$; this is a specialization. If the print covers only étale
extensions, strict henselization and completion, this form exceeds it; the restricted form would
then come from strict henselization and EGA IV₄ 18.8.8. The reading is pending. It is a hypothesis,
not proved here.
