# The criterion of Néron–Ogg–Shafarevich

The *Néron–Ogg–Shafarevich statement* is the following assertion. Let $K$ be a field, $\mathcal O$ a
discrete valuation ring with fraction field $K$, $K_s$ a separable closure of $K$, $w$ a valuation
ring of $K_s$ over $\mathcal O$, and $A$ an abelian variety over $K$. Then $A$ has good reduction at
$\mathcal O$ (it extends to an abelian scheme over $\mathcal O$) if and only if, for every integer
$m\ge1$ invertible in $\mathcal O$, the inertia group at $w$ acts trivially on $A(K_s)[m]$; and if
and only if this holds for infinitely many such $m$.

It is not printed in this form but follows from published theorems; reaching it from the print takes
further theorems, which are not proved here. Serre and Tate assume that the residue field is perfect, and the statement is for every discrete
valuation ring; over an imperfect residue field it is more than their Theorem 1 prints. The proof uses
it over number fields only.
Serre and Tate's fourth equivalent condition, on the $\ell$-adic Tate module, is not stated. The
form over an algebraic closure of $K$, which the proof uses, is proved here from this one. The
statement has not been read against the paper. It is a hypothesis, not proved here.
