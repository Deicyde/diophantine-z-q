# The criterion of Néron–Ogg–Shafarevich

The *Néron–Ogg–Shafarevich statement* is the following assertion. Let $K$ be a field, $\mathcal O$ a
discrete valuation ring with fraction field $K$, $w$ a valuation ring of $\overline K$ over
$\mathcal O$, and $A$ an abelian variety over $K$. Then the following are equivalent: $A$ has good
reduction at $\mathcal O$; for every integer $m\ge1$ invertible in $\mathcal O$, the inertia group
at $w$ acts trivially on $A(\overline K)[m]$; the same holds for infinitely many such $m$.

It is a published statement: the printed theorem, or a direct instance of it that needs no argument.
It is a hypothesis, not proved here.
