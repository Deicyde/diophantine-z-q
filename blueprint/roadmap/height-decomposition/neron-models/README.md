# Néron models, semistable reduction and monodromy

Let $\mathcal O$ be a discrete valuation ring with fraction field $K$ and $A$ an abelian variety
over $K$. A *Néron model* of $A$ is a smooth separated group scheme of finite type over $\mathcal O$
with generic fibre $A$ to which every morphism from the generic fibre of a smooth
$\mathcal O$-scheme to $A$ extends uniquely; $A$ has *semistable reduction* when the identity
component of the special fibre of its Néron model is an extension of an abelian variety by a torus.
These statements give the existence of Néron models and their behaviour under base change, the
criterion of Néron–Ogg–Shafarevich, Raynaud's criterion for semistable reduction, and two results of
SGA 7, Exposé IX, on semistable reduction over a strictly henselian base.

All five are published statements; none is proved here.
