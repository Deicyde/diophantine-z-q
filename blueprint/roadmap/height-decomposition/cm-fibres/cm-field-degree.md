# The degree of a field of definition of a CM elliptic curve

The *degree statement* is the following assertion. Let $L$ be a number field and $E$ an elliptic
curve over $L$ whose ring of endomorphisms over an algebraic closure of $L$ is isomorphic to an
order $R$ of an imaginary quadratic field. Then $\#\operatorname{Pic}(R)\le[L:\mathbb Q]$.

It is not printed in this form but follows from published theorems; reaching it from the print takes
further theorems, which are not proved here. The manuscript uses the classical fact without a
citation: "The degree of $j(E)$ is its order class number, so $h(\Delta)\le d\le D$." The classical
theorem is that the $j$-invariant of an elliptic curve with complex multiplication by an order $R$
generates a field of degree $h(R)$ over $\mathbb Q$ (for orders, Cox's Theorem 11.1, through the
ring class field of $R$); since $j(E)$ lies in $L$, the statement follows. Reaching it from the
print also takes the identification of the class number of $R$ with $\#\operatorname{Pic}(R)$, which
is standard and not proved here. The upstream audit of the manuscript's citations attributes the
degree to §2.1 of Fowler's paper, which has not been read for this statement. Cox's theorem number
is unverified, and a review of the statement is pending. It is a hypothesis, not proved here.
