# The identity component of a smooth group scheme is open

The *identity-component statement* is the following assertion. For a smooth group scheme $G$ over a
scheme $S$, the union $G^0$ of the identity components of the fibres of $G$ is open in $G$.

It is a published statement: the printed theorem, or a direct instance of it that needs no argument.
It is a hypothesis, not proved here.
