# The identity component of a smooth group scheme contains a neighbourhood of the identity

The *identity-component statement* is the following assertion. Let $G$ be a smooth group scheme over
a scheme $S$, with identity section $e$, and $s\in S$. Then the union $G^0$ of the identity
components of the fibres of $G$ contains an open neighbourhood of $e(s)$ in $G$.

It is a published statement: the printed theorem, or a direct instance of it that needs no argument.
It is weaker than the printed theorem, which says that $G^0$ is open. It has not been read against
the source. It is a hypothesis, not proved here.
