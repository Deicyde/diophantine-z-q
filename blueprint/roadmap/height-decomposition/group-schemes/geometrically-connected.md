# A connected scheme with a rational point is geometrically connected

The *connectedness statement* is the following assertion. A connected scheme of finite type over a
field $k$ with a $k$-rational point is geometrically connected.

It is a published statement: the printed theorem, or a direct instance of it that needs no argument.
The Stacks Project states it without the finiteness hypothesis and for a point whose residue field
contains $k$ algebraically closed in it; a rational point is such a point. It is a hypothesis, not
proved here.
