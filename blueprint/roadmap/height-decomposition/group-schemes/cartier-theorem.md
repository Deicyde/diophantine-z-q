# Cartier's theorem

The *Cartier statement* is the following assertion. A group scheme locally of finite type over a
field of characteristic $0$ is smooth.

It is a published statement: the printed theorem, or a direct instance of it that needs no argument.
It is a hypothesis, not proved here.
