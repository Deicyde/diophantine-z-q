# Properness spreads out from a limit

The *spreading-out statement* is the following assertion. Let $(D_i)$ be a filtered inverse system
of quasi-compact quasi-separated schemes with affine transition morphisms and limit $D$, and $X_i$ a
scheme of finite presentation over $D_i$. If $X_i\times_{D_i}D$ is proper over $D$, then
$X_i\times_{D_i}D_j$ is proper over $D_j$ for some $j\ge i$.

It is a published statement: the printed theorem, or a direct instance of it that needs no argument.
It is a hypothesis, not proved here.
