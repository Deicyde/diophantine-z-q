# Group schemes over a field are equidimensional

The *equidimensionality statement* is the following assertion. For a group scheme $G$ of finite type
over a field, every irreducible component of $G$ has dimension $\dim G$.

It is a published statement: the printed theorem, or a direct instance of it that needs no argument.
It is a hypothesis, not proved here.
