# Shimura's abelian varieties attached to newforms

The *Shimura statement* is the following assertion. Let $f\in S_2(\Gamma_1(N))$ be a newform with
character $\chi$, $K_f$ the field generated by its coefficients and $J=J_1(N)$. Then $K_f$ is a
number field, and there are an abelian variety $A$ over $\mathbb Q$ with a surjection $J\to A$ over
$\mathbb Q$ and an embedding $\theta:K_f\to\operatorname{End}^0(A)$, with $\dim A=[K_f:\mathbb Q]$,
such that for every prime $\ell$ and every prime $p\nmid N\ell$ an arithmetic Frobenius at $p$ acts
on each component of $V_\ell(A)$ for an embedding $\iota$ of $K_f$ with trace $\iota(a_p)$ and
determinant $\iota(\chi(p))\,p$.

No single source prints it. It combines two printed theorems about Shimura's abelian variety $A_f$:
its construction, with the action of $K_f$ and its dimension, and the characteristic polynomial of
Frobenius on its Tate modules, from the Eichler–Shimura congruence relation. The rank-two clause of
Shimura's theorem is proved here from the torsion and characteristic-polynomial statements. It is a
hypothesis, not proved here.
