# The Deligne–Serre theorem

The *Deligne–Serre statement* is the following assertion. For a normalized eigenform
$f\in S_1(N,\chi)$ there is a continuous representation
$\rho_f:G_{\mathbb Q}\to\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb C)$ with finite image, unramified at every prime
$p\nmid N$, such that the trace and the determinant of an arithmetic Frobenius at $p$ are $a_p(f)$
and $\chi(p)$.

It is a published statement: the printed theorem, or a direct instance of it that needs no argument.
It is a hypothesis, not proved here.
