# The coefficients of an eigenform generate a number field

The *coefficient-field statement* is the following assertion. For a normalized eigenform
$f\in S_k(N,\chi)$ of weight $k\ge2$, the field $K_f=\mathbb Q(a_n(f):n\ge1)$ is a finite extension
of $\mathbb Q$.

No single source prints it. It combines Diamond and Shurman's Theorem 6.5.1, printed for $k=2$, with
Shimura's theorem that $S_k(\Gamma_1(N))$ has a basis with rational Fourier coefficients and the
stability of that rational structure under the Hecke operators. It is a hypothesis, not proved here.
