# Cusp forms with integral Fourier coefficients

The *integral basis statement* is the following assertion. For $N\ge1$ and $k\ge2$, the space
$S_k(\Gamma_1(N))$ of cusp forms is spanned over $\mathbb C$ by the forms all of whose Fourier
coefficients at infinity are integers.

It is a published statement: the printed theorem, or a direct instance of it that needs no argument.
It is Shimura's theorem that $S_k(\Gamma_1(N))$ has a basis with integral Fourier coefficients, in
the form "such forms span". The statement has not been read against the source. With the statement
on [Hecke operators](hecke-operators-rational-coefficients.md), it gives here that the coefficients
of an eigenform of weight $k\ge2$ generate a number field. It is a hypothesis, not proved here.
