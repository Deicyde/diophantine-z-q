# Hecke operators and rational Fourier coefficients

The *Hecke statement* is the following assertion. Let $N\ge1$, $k\ge2$, $p$ a prime not dividing
$N$, and $g$ a cusp form on $\Gamma_1(N)$ of weight $k$ all of whose Fourier coefficients at
infinity are integers. Then the Fourier coefficients of $T_pg$ are rational.

It is a published statement: the printed theorem, or a direct instance of it that needs no argument.
It is a weak form of the theorem that the Hecke operators preserve the cusp forms with integral
Fourier coefficients. Through the formula for the coefficients of $T_pg$ it amounts to saying that
the diamond operators preserve rational Fourier coefficients; where the literature prints that for
$\Gamma_1(N)$ has not been checked. It is a hypothesis, not proved here.
