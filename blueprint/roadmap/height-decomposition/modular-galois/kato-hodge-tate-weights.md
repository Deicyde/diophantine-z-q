# Hodge–Tate weights of the representation of a newform

The *Hodge–Tate weight statement* is the following assertion. Let $\ell$ be a prime,
$f\in S_k(N,\chi)$ a newform of weight $k\ge2$, and
$\rho:G_{\mathbb Q}\to\mathrm{GL}_2(\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell)$ a semisimple representation attached
to $f$. Then $\rho$ restricted to a decomposition group at $\ell$ has Hodge–Tate weights $0$ and
$1-k$.

It is not printed in this form but follows from published theorems; reaching it from the print takes
further theorems, which are not proved here. Kato prints that the representation $V_{F_\lambda}(f)$
he constructs is de Rham at $\ell$ with Hodge–Tate weights $0$ and $k-1$, in the cohomological
normalization. The statement needs, besides, that every semisimple $\rho$ attached to $f$ is the
dual of $V_{F_\lambda}(f)$ (Chebotarev and Brauer–Nesbitt), and that duals negate Hodge–Tate
weights. It is a hypothesis, not proved here.
