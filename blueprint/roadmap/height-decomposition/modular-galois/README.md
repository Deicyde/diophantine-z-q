# Galois representations of modular forms

Cusp forms $f=\sum_{n\ge1}a_n(f)q^n$ of weight $k$ on $\Gamma_1(N)$ carry the Hecke operators $T_n$
and the diamond operators $\langle d\rangle$, $d\in(\mathbb Z/N)^\times$; $S_k(N,\chi)$ is the space
of those on which every $\langle d\rangle$ acts by $\chi(d)$, for a Dirichlet character $\chi$
modulo $N$. A *normalized eigenform* is an element of some $S_k(N,\chi)$ that is an eigenvector of
every $T_n$ and has $a_1(f)=1$. A *newform* of level $N$ is a cusp form $f$ with $a_1(f)=1$ on which
the diamond operators act through a character and which is a Hecke eigenform new at level $N$ in the
sense of Atkin, Lehner and Li. The statements read this in one of two ways, which agree by the
theory of newforms: $f$ is an eigenvector of every $T_n$ and lies outside the span of the forms
coming from lower levels, or $f$ is an eigenvector of the $T_n$ with $n$ prime to $N$ and lies in
the new subspace. The *coefficient field* $K_f\subset\mathbb C$ is generated over $\mathbb Q$ by the
$a_n(f)$, and $K_{f,\lambda}$ is its completion at a finite place $\lambda$. An *arithmetic
Frobenius* at a prime $p$ is an element of
$G_{\mathbb Q}=\operatorname{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb Q}/\mathbb Q)$ that maps a place of
$\overline{\mathbb Q}$ above $p$ to itself and acts on its residue field as $x\mapsto x^p$; a
*geometric Frobenius* is the inverse of one. In weight $2$ the forms are studied on a Jacobian $J$
of a model of $X_1(N)$ over $\mathbb Q$. For $N\ge2$, a *model of $X_1(N)$ over $\mathbb Q$* is a
proper smooth geometrically connected integral curve $X$ over $\mathbb Q$ whose function field is
generated by two functions $j_X,t_X$ with exactly the polynomial relations over $\mathbb Z$ of
$j(\tau)$ and the Weber function $f_1(\tau)=\frac{g_2(\tau)g_3(\tau)}{\Delta(\tau)}\wp_\tau(1/N)$ on
the upper half-plane, $\wp_\tau$ being the Weierstrass function of $\mathbb Z+\mathbb Z\tau$. Write
$x_\tau$ for a point of $X(\mathbb C)$ at $\tau$: a point at which every rational function of
$j_X,t_X$ whose counterpart in $j,f_1$ has a limit at $\tau$ is regular with that value. Let $P_0$
be a rational point of $X$ and $J$ a Jacobian of $X$ with its Abel–Jacobi map based at $P_0$. *Hecke
operators on $J$* are endomorphisms $T_p$ ($p$ prime) and $\langle d\rangle$
($d\in(\mathbb Z/N)^\times$) of $J$ over $\mathbb Q$ that act on the classes of degree-zero divisors
supported on such points by
$$T_p[x_\tau]=\sum_{i<p}[x_{(\tau+i)/p}]+[x_{g(p\tau)}],\qquad\langle d\rangle[x_\tau]=[x_{g\tau}],$$
for every choice of these points, where the last term of $T_p$ occurs only for $p\nmid N$, and $g$
is any element of $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb Z)$ with lower row $(c,d')$, $N\mid c$ and $d'\equiv p$,
respectively $d'\equiv d$, modulo $N$. That such operators exist on every model, for Shimura's Hecke
correspondences, is proved here and is no longer an open statement.

Of these six statements, two are published statements and four are consequences of published
theorems; none is proved here.
