# The Galois invariants of the ring of de Rham periods

The *invariants statement* is the following assertion. For a finite extension $F$ of $\mathbb Q_p$,
the elements of $B_{\rm dR}$ fixed by $G_F$ form a $\mathbb Q_p$-vector space of dimension
$[F:\mathbb Q_p]$.

The project classes it as a published statement; its reading against the print is still pending.
The print says $B_{\rm dR}^{G_F}=F$ (Fontaine); the statement keeps its dimension. The ring
$B_{\rm dR}$ is the one in Mathlib, with its Galois action. It is a hypothesis, not proved here.
