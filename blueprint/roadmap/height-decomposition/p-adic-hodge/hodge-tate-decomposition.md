# The Hodge–Tate decomposition of an abelian variety

The *Hodge–Tate statement* is the following assertion. Let $A$ be an abelian variety of dimension
$g$ over a finite extension $F$ of $\mathbb Q_p$, and $V_p(A)$ its rational Tate module. Then there
is a $G_F$-equivariant $\mathbb C_p$-linear isomorphism
$$\mathbb C_p\otimes_{\mathbb Q_p}V_p(A)\;\cong\;\mathbb C_p(1)^g\oplus\mathbb C_p^g,$$
where $G_F$ acts on the left by $\sigma\otimes\rho(\sigma)$ and on $\mathbb C_p(1)$ through the
cyclotomic character.

It is not printed in this form but follows from published theorems; reaching it from the print takes
further theorems, which are not proved here. The printed decomposition is
$(\mathbb C_p(1)\otimes_F t_A)\oplus(\mathbb C_p\otimes_F t_{A'}^{*})$, with $t_A$ the tangent space
at the origin and $A'$ the dual; the statement chooses bases of the two spaces, which needs
$\dim t_{A'}^{*}=g$, the dimension of the dual abelian variety (Mumford, §§8 and 13), unless
Fontaine prints the coordinate form. It is a hypothesis, not proved here.
