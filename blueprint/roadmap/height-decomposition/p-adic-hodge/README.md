# p-adic Hodge theory

Let $p$ be a prime, $\overline{\mathbb Q}_p$ an algebraic closure of $\mathbb Q_p$ and $\mathbb C_p$
its completion. For a finite extension $K$ of $\mathbb Q_p$, $G_K$ is its absolute Galois group and
$\mathbb C_p(1)$ the twist of $\mathbb C_p$ by the cyclotomic character. These statements give the
Hodge–Tate decomposition of an abelian variety over a $p$-adic field, the fact that its Tate module
is de Rham, the Galois invariants of the ring $B_{\rm dR}$, Berger's theorem that de Rham
representations are potentially semistable, and Pan's Fontaine–Mazur theorem for two-dimensional
representations of $G_{\mathbb Q}$.

The project classes all five as published statements; for four of them the reading against the
print is still pending. None is proved here.
