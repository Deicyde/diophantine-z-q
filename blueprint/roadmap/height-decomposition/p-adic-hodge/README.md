# p-adic Hodge theory

Let $p$ be a prime, $\overline{\mathbb Q}_p$ an algebraic closure of $\mathbb Q_p$ and $\mathbb C_p$
its completion. For a finite extension $K$ of $\mathbb Q_p$, $G_K$ is its absolute Galois group and
$\mathbb C_p(1)$ the twist of $\mathbb C_p$ by the cyclotomic character. These statements give the
Hodge–Tate decomposition and the de Rham comparison for abelian varieties over $p$-adic fields, and
Pan's Fontaine–Mazur theorem for two-dimensional representations of $G_{\mathbb Q}$.

Of these three statements, two are consequences of published theorems and one combines several
published theorems; none is proved here.
