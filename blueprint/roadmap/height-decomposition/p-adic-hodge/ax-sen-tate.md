# The Ax–Sen–Tate theorem

The *Ax–Sen–Tate statement* is the following assertion. For a finite extension $K$ of $\mathbb Q_p$
in $\overline{\mathbb Q}_p$, $\mathbb C_p^{G_K}=K$.

It is a published statement: the printed theorem, or a direct instance of it that needs no argument.
The inclusion $K\subseteq\mathbb C_p^{G_K}$ is trivial; the content is the other inclusion. It is a
hypothesis, not proved here.
