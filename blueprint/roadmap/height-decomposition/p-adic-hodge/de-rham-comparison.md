# The de Rham comparison for an abelian variety

The *de Rham statement* is the following assertion. Let $A$ be an abelian variety over a finite
extension $F$ of $\mathbb Q_p$ and $W=\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb Q_p}(V_p(A),\mathbb Q_p)$, with
the contragredient action of $G_F$. Then $W$ is de Rham: $D_{\rm dR}(W)=(B_{\rm dR}\otimes W)^{G_F}$
has dimension $\dim_{\mathbb Q_p}W$ over $F$.

It is a published statement: the printed theorem, or a direct instance of it that needs no argument.
The literature states it for $H^1_{\rm et}(A_{\overline F},\mathbb Q_p)$, which is identified with
$W$ in the standard way (Milne, *Abelian varieties*, Theorem 15.1(a)). It is a hypothesis, not
proved here.
