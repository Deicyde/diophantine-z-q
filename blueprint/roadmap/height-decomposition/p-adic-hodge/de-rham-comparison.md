# The de Rham comparison for an abelian variety

The *de Rham statement* is the following assertion. Let $A$ be an abelian variety over a finite
extension $F$ of $\mathbb Q_p$ and $W=\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb Q_p}(V_p(A),\mathbb Q_p)$, with
the contragredient action of $G_F$. Then $W$ is de Rham: $D_{\rm dR}(W)=(B_{\rm dR}\otimes W)^{G_F}$
has dimension $\dim_{\mathbb Q_p}W$ over $F$.

It is not printed in this form but follows from published theorems; reaching it from the print takes
further theorems, which are not proved here. The literature states it for
$H^1_{\rm et}(A_{\overline F},\mathbb Q_p)$, which is identified with $W$ by Milne's Theorem 15.1(a)
(Serre–Lang); Scholze's Corollary 1.8 also needs the comparison for rigid-analytic varieties. It is
a hypothesis, not proved here.
