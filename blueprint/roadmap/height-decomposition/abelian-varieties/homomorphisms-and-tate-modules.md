# Homomorphisms of abelian varieties and Tate modules

The *homomorphism statement* is the following assertion. Let $X$ and $Y$ be abelian varieties over
an algebraically closed field $k$. Then $\operatorname{Hom}(X,Y)$ is a free $\mathbb Z$-module of
finite rank at most $4\dim X\dim Y$, and for every prime $\ell\ne\operatorname{char}k$ the natural
map
$\mathbb Z_\ell\otimes\operatorname{Hom}(X,Y)\to\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb Z_\ell}(T_\ell X,T_\ell Y)$
is injective.

It is a published statement: the printed theorem, or a direct instance of it that needs no argument.
It is a hypothesis, not proved here.
