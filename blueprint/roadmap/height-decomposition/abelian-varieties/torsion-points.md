# Torsion points over an algebraically closed field

The *torsion statement* is the following. Let $X$ be an abelian variety of dimension $g$
over an algebraically closed field $k$ and $n\ge1$ an integer with $n\ne0$ in $k$. Then the group of
points $x\in X(k)$ with $nx=0$ is isomorphic to $(\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z)^{2g}$.

It was a hypothesis of this chapter and is now proved in Lean.
