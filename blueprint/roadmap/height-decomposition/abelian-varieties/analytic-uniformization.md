# The analytic uniformization of a complex abelian variety

The *uniformization statement* is the following assertion. For every abelian variety $A$ over
$\mathbb C$ there are an integer $g$, a lattice $\Lambda\subset\mathbb C^g$ (a discrete subgroup of
rank $2g$) and a group isomorphism $\varphi:\mathbb C^g/\Lambda\to A(\mathbb C)$ that is an
isomorphism of complex Lie groups in the following sense: every regular function on a Zariski open
subset of $A$ pulls back along $\varphi$ to a holomorphic function on an open subset of
$\mathbb C^g$; at every point, some $g$ regular functions pull back to local holomorphic
coordinates; and $\varphi^{-1}$ is continuous for the topology on $A(\mathbb C)$ that the regular
functions define.

It is not printed in this form but follows from published theorems; reaching it from the print takes
further theorems, which are not proved here. The printed theorem says that a compact connected
complex Lie group is a torus; applying it to the analytic space of $A$, in these coordinates, takes
the analytification and GAGA. It is a hypothesis, not proved here.
