# The kernel of an ample line bundle is finite

The *finiteness statement* is the following assertion. Let $X$ be an abelian variety over an
algebraically closed field $k$ and $L$ an ample line bundle on $X$ with a nonzero global section.
Then the group $K(L)=\{x\in X(k):t_x^*L\cong L\}$ is finite.

It is a published statement: the printed theorem, or a direct instance of it that needs no argument.
Mumford states it for $L=\mathcal O_X(D)$ with $D$ effective; the zero locus of a nonzero section is
such a $D$. The statement has not been read against the book. It is a hypothesis, not proved here.
