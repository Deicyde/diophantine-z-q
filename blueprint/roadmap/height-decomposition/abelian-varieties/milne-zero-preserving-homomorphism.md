# Morphisms of abelian schemes fixing zero are homomorphisms

For every locally noetherian scheme $S$, abelian schemes $\mathcal A$ and $\mathcal A'$ of relative
dimension $2$ over $S$ with ring homomorphisms from $\mathcal O$ to their endomorphism rings, and
every morphism $f:\mathcal A\to\mathcal A'$ over $S$ carrying the zero section of $\mathcal A$ to
that of $\mathcal A'$, $f$ is a homomorphism.

It is a published statement: the printed theorem, or a direct instance of it that needs no argument.
It is Milne's Corollary 20.2(a), which is stated for abelian schemes over any base, restricted to
locally noetherian bases and relative dimension $2$; the actions of $\mathcal O$ play no part.
Milne's proof applies his rigidity lemma, Proposition 20.1, which is stated for a connected base.
Lan states the corollary over any base, citing Mumford's *Geometric invariant theory*, Corollary
6.4. It is a hypothesis, not proved here.
