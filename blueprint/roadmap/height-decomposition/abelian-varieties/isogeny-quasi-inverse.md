# A quasi-inverse of an isogeny

The *quasi-inverse statement* is the following assertion. Let $f:A\to B$ be a homomorphism of
abelian varieties over a field that is finite and surjective. Then there are an integer $d\ge1$ and
a homomorphism $g:B\to A$ with $g\circ f=[d]_A$ and $f\circ g=[d]_B$.

It is a published statement: the printed theorem, or a direct instance of it that needs no argument.
It is read off Milne's display, which takes $d=\deg f$ and also gives that $g$ is an isogeny. It is
a hypothesis, not proved here.
