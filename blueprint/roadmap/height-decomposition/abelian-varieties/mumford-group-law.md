# Mumford's theorem: families of abelian varieties are abelian schemes

The *group-law statement* is the following assertion. Let $S$ be a connected locally noetherian
scheme and $F\to S$ a proper smooth morphism with geometrically connected fibres and a section $e$,
such that for every field $K$ and every $K$-point $s$ of $S$ the fibre of $F$ over $s$ is an abelian
variety over $K$ with identity $e(s)$. Then $F$ is a group scheme over $S$ with identity $e$.

It is a published statement: the printed theorem, or a direct instance of it that needs no argument.
Mumford asks only one fibre to be an abelian variety and concludes that $F$ is an abelian scheme;
the statement assumes more and concludes less. It is a hypothesis, not proved here.
