# Poincaré's complete reducibility

The *Poincaré statement* is the following assertion. Let $J$ be an abelian variety over a field of
characteristic $0$ and $i:B\to J$ a homomorphism of abelian varieties that is a closed immersion.
Then there are an abelian variety $Z$ and a homomorphism $j:Z\to J$ such that $B\times Z\to J$,
$(b,z)\mapsto i(b)+j(z)$, is an isogeny.

It is a published statement: the printed theorem, or a direct instance of it that needs no argument.
It is weaker than the published statement: the characteristic is $0$, and it does not assert that
$j$ is a closed immersion. It is a hypothesis, not proved here.
