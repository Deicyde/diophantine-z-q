# The Picard scheme of an abelian variety

The *Picard statement* is the following assertion. For an abelian variety $A$ over a field $K$, the
relative Picard functor of $A$ over $K$, as an fppf sheaf, is representable by a scheme
$\operatorname{Pic}_{A/K}$.

It is a published statement: the printed theorem, or a direct instance of it that needs no argument.
A statement review of this form is pending; one model review found no errors. It is a hypothesis,
not proved here.
