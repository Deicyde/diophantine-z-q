# GAGA for homomorphisms of complex abelian varieties

The *GAGA statement* is the following assertion. Let $A$ and $B$ be abelian varieties over
$\mathbb C$, $\varphi:\mathbb C^g/\Lambda\to A(\mathbb C)$ a uniformization of $A$ as in
[the uniformization statement](analytic-uniformization.md), and $\psi:\mathbb C^g\to B(\mathbb C)$ a
holomorphic homomorphism that vanishes on $\Lambda$. Then the map $A(\mathbb C)\to B(\mathbb C)$
that $\psi$ induces comes from a morphism of varieties $A\to B$.

It is a published statement: the printed theorem, or a direct instance of it that needs no argument.
It is a hypothesis, not proved here.
