# GAGA for homomorphisms of complex abelian varieties

The *GAGA statement* is the following assertion. Let $A$ and $B$ be abelian varieties over
$\mathbb C$, $\varphi:\mathbb C^g/\Lambda\to A(\mathbb C)$ a uniformization of $A$ (a group
isomorphism that is an isomorphism of complex Lie groups), and $\psi:\mathbb C^g\to B(\mathbb C)$ a
holomorphic homomorphism that vanishes on $\Lambda$. Then the map $A(\mathbb C)\to B(\mathbb C)$
that $\psi$ induces comes from a morphism of varieties $A\to B$.

It is not printed in this form but follows from published theorems; reaching it from the print takes
further theorems, which are not proved here. GAGA is stated for a holomorphic map between the
analytic spaces of $A$ and $B$; reaching that hypothesis from $\psi$ takes the holomorphic inverse
function theorem, which makes the inverse of the uniformization holomorphic. The printed statement
for proper varieties is SGA 1 XII 4.4; Serre's GAGA is stated for projective varieties. It is a
hypothesis, not proved here.
