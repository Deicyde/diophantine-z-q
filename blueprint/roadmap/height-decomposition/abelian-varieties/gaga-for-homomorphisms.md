# GAGA for homomorphisms of complex abelian varieties

The *GAGA statement* is the following. Let $A$ and $B$ be abelian varieties over
$\mathbb C$, $\varphi:\mathbb C^g/\Lambda\to A(\mathbb C)$ a uniformization of $A$ (a group
isomorphism that is an isomorphism of complex Lie groups), and $\psi:\mathbb C^g\to B(\mathbb C)$ a
holomorphic homomorphism that vanishes on $\Lambda$. Then the map $A(\mathbb C)\to B(\mathbb C)$
that $\psi$ induces comes from a morphism of varieties $A\to B$.

It was a hypothesis of this chapter and is now proved in Lean, from the GAGA theorem for projective
varieties in a Lean library the project vendors.
