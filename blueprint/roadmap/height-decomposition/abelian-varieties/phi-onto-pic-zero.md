# Translation-invariant line bundles come from an ample one

The *surjectivity statement* is the following assertion. Let $X$ be an abelian variety over an
algebraically closed field $k$ and $L$ an ample line bundle on $X$. Then every class of line bundles
on $X$ that is invariant under all translations $t_x$, $x\in X(k)$, is the class of
$t_x^*L\otimes L^{-1}$ for some $x\in X(k)$.

It is a published statement: the printed theorem, or a direct instance of it that needs no argument.
That is, $\varphi_L:X(k)\to\operatorname{Pic}^0(X)$ is surjective, where $\operatorname{Pic}^0(X)$
is the group of translation-invariant classes, as in Mumford. The statement has not been read
against the book. It is a hypothesis, not proved here.
