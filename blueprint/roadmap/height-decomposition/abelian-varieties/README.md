# Abelian varieties

Let $K$ be a field, $\overline K$ an algebraic closure of $K$ and
$G_K=\operatorname{Aut}_K(\overline K)$; let $A$ be an abelian variety over $K$,
$A_{\overline K}=A\times_K\overline K$, and $\ell$ a prime different from the characteristic of $K$.
The Tate module $T_\ell A=\varprojlim_nA(\overline K)[\ell^n]$ is a $\mathbb Z_\ell$-module on which
$G_K$ acts through its action on the points of $A$. The $\ell$-adic cohomology of $A_{\overline K}$
is taken on the pro-étale site, with coefficients in the sheaf of continuous $\mathbb Z_\ell$-valued
functions, and $G_K$ acts on it through its action on $\overline K$. The duality between the first
cohomology group and the Tate module carries the comparison theorems of $p$-adic Hodge theory, which
are stated for cohomology, over to Tate modules.

All five are published statements; none is proved here.
