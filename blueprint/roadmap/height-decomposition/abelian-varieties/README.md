# Abelian varieties

Statements about abelian varieties: over an algebraically closed field, from Mumford's book; over a
general field, the Picard scheme and the Weil pairing, from which the proof builds a symmetric
isogeny to the dual; over $\mathbb C$; and in families.

Of these eleven statements, eight are published statements and three are consequences of published
theorems; none is proved here.
