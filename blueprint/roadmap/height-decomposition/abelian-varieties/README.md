# Abelian varieties

Statements about abelian varieties over a field: the torsion, the homomorphisms and the
characteristic polynomials of endomorphisms over an algebraically closed field (Mumford), Poincaré's
complete reducibility, quasi-inverses of isogenies, Mumford's criterion for a family to be an
abelian scheme, the analytic uniformization over $\mathbb C$ and GAGA for its homomorphisms, and
four statements on the Picard scheme of an abelian variety and the Weil pairing, from which the
proof builds a symmetric isogeny to the dual.

Of these twelve statements, eight are published statements and four are consequences of published
theorems; none is proved here.
