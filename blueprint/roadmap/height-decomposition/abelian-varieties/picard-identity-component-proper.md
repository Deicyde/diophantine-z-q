# The identity component of the Picard scheme is proper

The *properness statement* is the following assertion. Let $X$ be a proper scheme over a field $K$
that is geometrically normal. Then for every scheme representing the relative Picard functor of $X$
over $K$, as an fppf sheaf, its identity component $\operatorname{Pic}^0_{X/K}$ is proper over $K$.

It is not printed in this form but follows from published theorems; reaching it from the print takes
further theorems, which are not proved here. Kleiman's Theorem 9.5.4 is stated for $X$ projective and geometrically integral, and his Remark
9.5.6 asserts the proper case; the statement assumes neither projectivity nor geometric integrality.
The proof applies it to abelian varieties only.
The statement has not been read against the source. It is a hypothesis, not proved here.
