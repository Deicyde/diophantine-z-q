# The canonical height is quadratic (canonical-height-quadratic)

- Lean names: `ZInQ.canonHeight_parallelogram`, `ZInQ.canonHeight_zsmul (m : ℤ) (P) :
  canonHeight (m • P) = m ^ 2 * canonHeight P`.
- Mathlib v4.35.0: `WeierstrassCurve.Affine.approx_parallelogram_law` (Height/EllipticCurve.lean)
  for `naiveHeight` (= 2 * hx) over any field with `AdmissibleAbsValues`, needs `[DecidableEq K0]`
  and `[curveC.IsElliptic]`. Divide by 4^n along 2^n multiples and pass to the limit.
- Gap: canonical (Néron–Tate) height and its quadraticity are not in Mathlib; this article
  provides them for `curveC` only.
- FINITE COMPUTATION: no. Difficulty: medium.
