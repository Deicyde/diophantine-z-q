# Implementation note: Heights of the codes in the guard box

- Lean name: `ZInQ.Size.iota_coord_height`.
- Draft:
  ```lean
  theorem iota_coord_height (T : ℕ) (n : ℤ) (hn : n.natAbs ≤ T) (i : Fin 5) :
      ((iota n) i).num.natAbs ≤ 2 ^ (2 ^ 30 * (T + 1) ^ 3) ∧
      ((iota n) i).den ≤ 2 ^ (2 ^ 30 * (T + 1) ^ 3)
  ```
- Proof: `n = 0` by `simp [iota, pointCode]`. Otherwise the model chapter's multiplication
  formula theorem (`ZInQ.mulByN_formulas`, note of division-polynomial-multiples) applied to
  `genP0`, with `genP0` non-torsion from `ZInQ.torsion_E` and `genP0.y ≠ 0`. Evaluate
  `psiComponent m c` at the four rational coordinates: build `QRep`s for `ψ_m(P0)` via
  `MvPolynomial.eval₂` monomial-by-monomial bound (homogenize with `32^δ`); the key estimate
  `|32^δ * eval (N/32) Ψ| ≤ l1 Ψ * 2^(17 δ)` follows from `MvPolynomial.eval₂_monomial`
  (Mathlib/Algebra/MvPolynomial/Eval.lean:92) and `Finset.abs_sum_le_sum_abs`. Then chain the
  `QRep` operation lemmas and the closed comparison `65536 * 1281 ≤ 2^27`.
- Depends on the model chapter's multiplication-by-n theorem; until that background theorem is
  formalized, this article inherits its status (see the syntax chapter's proposition
  `ZInQ.DivisionPolynomialMultiplication`).
- Alternative (if the formula theorem is late): any bound of the form `2^(c (T+1)^3)` with
  `c ≤ 2^30` suffices; the canonical-height route (model chapter, heights) gives
  `h([n]P0) ≤ n^2 ĥ(P0) + C` but needs translation from logarithmic heights to numerators and
  denominators of the rational coordinates; not recommended.
- Finite computation: no (closed literal comparisons only). Never evaluates `iota n` for any
  concrete `n`.
- Upstream reuse: none.
- Difficulty: medium-hard (~250 lines).

Shared design (norm notes): polynomials are `MvPolynomial σ ℤ` (σ = ℕ for templates, `Fin 4`
for components, `Fin 15` for the guard). `l1 p := ∑ m ∈ p.support, (p.coeff m).natAbs : ℕ`;
pairs are `QuadraticAlgebra (MvPolynomial σ ℤ) 2 0` with `pairNorm u := l1 u.re + 2 * l1 u.im`
and `pairDeg u := max u.re.totalDegree u.im.totalDegree`. All bounds are natural-number
inequalities; logarithms appear only as exponents of 2.
