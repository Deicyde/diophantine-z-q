# Reduction and prime-to-p torsion (reduction-torsion)

- Lean names: `ZInQ.reductionHom (p r A ...) : (shortCurve A).toAffine.Point →+ (shortCurve (ρ A)).toAffine.Point`
  over `ZMod p`, `ZInQ.reduction_injective_on_torsion`.
- Mathlib v4.35.0: `Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Reduction.lean` defines integral and
  minimal models, `reduction` of a minimal Weierstrass equation over a DVR fraction field and
  `HasGoodReduction`, but no reduction map on points, no formal group and no torsion injectivity.
  This is a genuine gap (background).
- Cheaper alternative for this project (same statement, specialized): prove injectivity on
  prime-to-p torsion directly for y² = x³ + Ax with the elementary "kernel of reduction is
  torsion-free away from p" argument via the z = -x/y parameter and valuations
  (Silverman VII.2–3 / Silverman–Tate IV), working in the localization `Localization.AtPrime 𝔭`
  of `Zs`, or in `ℚ_p` after embedding K0 by s ↦ a p-adic square root of 2 (it exists by Hensel's
  lemma since r² ≡ 2 mod p and p is odd).
- Alternative avoiding reduction entirely (not the manuscript's route, record only): torsion of
  E(K0) can be bounded by the explicit duplication formula (no point of order 4: x(2T) ∈ {0, ±d}
  forces non-squares), plus Mathlib's `finite_torsion`; still needs odd torsion exclusion, so the
  reduction route is preferable.
