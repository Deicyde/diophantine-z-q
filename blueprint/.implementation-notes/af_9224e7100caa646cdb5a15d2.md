# Implementation note: The fixed Jacobian triple of the generator

- Lean name: `ZInQ.Machine.jacP0_spec` (with `ZInQ.Machine.jacP0 : K0 × K0 × K0`).
- FINITE COMPUTATION (small, closed). Two forms:
  - Field form, the one the IR proof uses:
    ```lean
    def jacP0 : K0 × K0 × K0 := (⟨3282, -2320⟩, ⟨-247440, 174966⟩, ⟨4, 0⟩)
    theorem jacP0_spec :
        jacP0.2.1 ^ 2 = jacP0.1 ^ 3 + (-genD ^ 2) * jacP0.1 * jacP0.2.2 ^ 4 ∧
        jacP0.1 / jacP0.2.2 ^ 2 = ⟨1641 / 8, -145⟩ ∧
        jacP0.2.1 / jacP0.2.2 ^ 3 = ⟨-15465 / 4, 87483 / 32⟩
    ```
    Check: `ext <;> norm_num [QuadraticAlgebra.ext_iff]` (or `decide` on the integer-pair form
    below followed by a cast lemma). `QuadraticAlgebra` derives `DecidableEq`
    (Mathlib/Algebra/QuadraticAlgebra/Defs.lean, line 49), so after `simp` reduces the
    operations to rational components the goal is `decide`-able; the numbers are below `10^12`.
  - Integer-pair form, the one the register program computes with:
    `zsMul (zsMul Y Y) = ...` over `ℤ × ℤ` with `(a,b)(a',b') = (aa'+2bb', ab'+a'b)`; both sides
    are `(122452755912, -86587174080)`; kernel `decide` (Int arithmetic, GMP-accelerated `Nat`).
- The affine point must be the model chapter's `P_0` (`genP0` or whatever name the model chapter
  gives it); the last two conjuncts identify the coordinates.
- Mathlib: `QuadraticAlgebra.ext_iff`, `norm_num`.
- Upstream reuse: none.
- Difficulty: trivial (~15 lines).

Shared design (machine notes): namespace `ZInQ.Machine`, files under
`ZInQKernel/Compiler/Machine/`. Large programs are Lean terms built by functions; their codes,
in particular `c_3`, are never evaluated by any proof.
