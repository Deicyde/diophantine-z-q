# Implementation note: Closed numerical comparisons

- Lean name: `ZInQ.Size.closed_comparisons` (or one lemma per item, `closed_cmp_1` ... `closed_cmp_8`,
  if the consumers prefer to import them separately).
- FINITE COMPUTATION. Exact decidable form:
  ```lean
  set_option exponentiation.threshold 9100 in
  set_option maxRecDepth 20000 in
  theorem closed_comparisons :
      433 + 22 * 2 ^ 2048 < 2 ^ 2053 ∧ 4 + 1262628 + 16104 * 2 ^ 2048 < 2 ^ 2063 ∧
      1262628 = 433 * 2916 ∧ 16104 = 22 * 732 ∧
      2 + 10 * 2 ^ 2054 < 2 ^ 2059 ∧
      10 ^ 12 < 2 ^ 40 ∧ 2250 + 2 ^ 9009 < 2 ^ 9010 ∧
      4096 * 85033 < 2 ^ 29 ∧
      (96 * 10 ^ 13) ^ 13 < 2 ^ 650 ∧ 2210 * 116064000000 ^ 2 < 2 ^ 86 ∧
      11243 + 2 * 7950 = 27143 ∧
      3 * 3 ^ 4 + 6 * 27143 * 3 ^ 2 + 27143 ^ 2 < 2 ^ 30 ∧
      2 * (3 ^ 6 + 5 * 27143 * 3 ^ 4 + 5 * 27143 ^ 2 * 3 ^ 2 + 27143 ^ 3) < 2 ^ 46 ∧
      16 * (3 ^ 3 + 27143 * 3) ^ 2 < 2 ^ 38 ∧
      123720 < 2 ^ 17 ∧ 65536 * 1281 < 2 ^ 27 ∧
      4 * 3181317 < 2 ^ 24 := by
    decide
  ```
  (all in `ℕ`). Upstream proves `(96 * 10 ^ 13 : Nat) ^ 13 < 2 ^ 650` the same way
  (`ZInQ.ExplicitCutoffBounds.prefactor_bound`, private, with `exponentiation.threshold 650`), and
  `2210 * g0 ^ 2 < 2 ^ 100` (`exponent_coefficient_bound`, private); either constant works for the
  bound on `T`.
- Size: at most 9011-bit numbers, about twenty multiplications. Method: kernel `decide`
  (`Nat.decLt` reduces to GMP-backed `Nat.blt`); `exponentiation.threshold` must exceed the
  largest exponent so that `2 ^ 9010` is not rejected by the elaborator's safeguard. If `decide`
  is slow on the conjunction, split into separate lemmas.
- Mathlib: none.
- Upstream reuse: the proof pattern of `ZInQ.ExplicitCutoffBounds.prefactor_bound`.
- Checked: this exact statement was elaborated with `lake env lean` (Lean 4.35.0, Mathlib v4.35.0) by the reviewer; `decide` succeeds and the proof uses only `propext`.
- Difficulty: trivial.

Shared design (size notes): namespace `ZInQ.Size`, files under `ZInQKernel/Size/`. Constants are
the syntax chapter's abbreviations `ZInQ.Syntax.Ps`, `W`, `Hcat`, `Hmax`, `Ns`, `M0`, `cstar`,
`ell`, `F`, `D`, `G0`, `E0`, `R`, `Q`, `Bcm`, `Ccm`, `Aht`, `T` (natural numbers or `toNat` of
`FixedExpr.val`; prove unfolding lemmas `T_def : T = (48 * Aht)^13` etc. by `rfl`/`simp
[FixedExpr.val]`). None of them is ever evaluated: never `decide`, `norm_num` or `rfl`-reduce a
goal containing `D`, `T`, `Hcat`, `M0`, `cstar`, `ell` or `c_3`; mark them `@[irreducible]`
after the unfolding lemmas, and work with `Nat.pow_lt_pow_left/right`, `Nat.pow_le_pow_left/right`,
`Nat.lt_two_pow_self`, `Nat.mul_lt_mul_of_lt_of_lt` as in upstream
`ZInQ.ExplicitCutoffBounds` (lean/ZInQ/ExplicitCutoffBounds.lean).
