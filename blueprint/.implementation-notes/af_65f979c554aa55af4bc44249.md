# The formal group law of y^2=x^3+Ax (formal-group-law)

- Lean names: `ZInQ.Mult.omegaSeries : PowerSeries 𝒪`, `ZInQ.Mult.formalGroupLaw`,
  `ZInQ.Mult.z_add_eq_eval`.
- Draft:
  ```lean
  variable {K : Type*} [Field K] [CharZero K] [Valued K ℤᵐ⁰] [CompleteSpace K]  -- or 𝔭.adicCompletion K0
  noncomputable def omegaSeries (A : K) : PowerSeries K   -- ω = T³ + A T ω², coefficients in 𝒪
  noncomputable def formalGroupLaw (A : K) (hA : Valued.v A ≤ 1) : FormalGroup (Valued.integer K)
  theorem z_add_eq_eval (P Q) (hP : P ∈ formalNbhd A hA) (hQ : Q ∈ formalNbhd A hA) :
      zCoord (P + Q) = evalSeries₂ (formalGroupLaw A hA).F (zCoord P) (zCoord Q)
  ```
- Mathlib: `FormalGroup` (Mathlib/RingTheory/FormalGroup/Basic.lean: the structure with
  associativity and commutativity fields, `𝔾ₐ`, `𝔾ₘ`), `MvPowerSeries.subst`, `PowerSeries.subst`,
  `PowerSeries.HasSubst` (Mathlib/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Substitution.lean), `adicCompletion`,
  `adicCompletionIntegers`. Evaluation of a two-variable power series at elements of the maximal
  ideal of a complete DVR: not packaged in Mathlib; use `tsum` with
  `NonarchimedeanAddGroup.summable_of_tendsto_cofinite_zero` (verified with `#check`).
  GAP: the formal group of an elliptic curve (Silverman IV.1-IV.2, VII.2.2) is not in Mathlib.
- Proof route: define `ω` coefficientwise by the recursion; the associativity field is the hard
  part. Since the chapter only uses (a) `z(P+Q) = F(z(P), z(Q))` and (b) the logarithm identity
  `log ∘ F = log + log`, a project-specific route can skip the `FormalGroup` structure: prove (b)
  directly for the explicit series `F` of the article and the explicit binomial logarithm of the
  logarithm-series article (both sides have the same partial derivative in `T₁`, using the
  invariant differential `dz/√(1 - 4Az⁴)`).
- FINITE COMPUTATION: no. Difficulty: HARD (with formal-logarithm, the largest gap of the
  chapter; estimate 1000-2000 lines together).

Shared design (multiplication chapter): namespace `ZInQ.Mult`, files under
`ZInQKernel/Multiplication/`. Points are `genCurve.toAffine.Point` over `K0 = QuadraticAlgebra ℚ 2 0`;
codes are `ZInQ.iota`; template relations are `ZInQ.Syntax.Rel`. Valuations on `K0` at a prime
`𝔭 : IsDedekindDomain.HeightOneSpectrum Zs` (with `Zs` the model chapter's `ℤ[√2]`) are taken as
`ℤ∪{∞}`-valued via `IsDedekindDomain.HeightOneSpectrum.valuation K0 𝔭` (multiplicative, `ℤᵐ⁰`);
a thin wrapper `ZInQ.Mult.vP 𝔭 : K0 → WithTop ℤ` is recommended. The completion is
`𝔭.adicCompletion K0`. The constants `c_*`, `ℓ_0`, `A_ht`, `T` are the syntax chapter's symbolic
`FixedExpr` values and are never evaluated (no `decide`/`norm_num` on them).
