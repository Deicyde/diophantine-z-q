# The formal logarithm and its isometry range (formal-logarithm)

- Lean names: `ZInQ.Mult.formalLog : PowerSeries K`, `ZInQ.Mult.formalLog_add`,
  `ZInQ.Mult.ellLog : formalNbhd A hA →+ K`, `ZInQ.Mult.ellLog_val_eq`, `ZInQ.Mult.ellLog_injective`.
- Draft:
  ```lean
  noncomputable def ellLog (A) (hA) : formalNbhd A hA →+ K
  theorem ellLog_val (P) (hP : P ∈ formalNbhd A hA) (h : (e : ℚ) / (p - 1) < vz P) :
      vK (ellLog A hA ⟨P, hP⟩) = vz P
  theorem ellLog_injective_of_unramified (hp2 : p ≠ 2) (he : vK (p : K) = 1) :
      Function.Injective (ellLog A hA)
  ```
- Mathlib: `FormalGroup` only; `PowerSeries.derivative`, `PowerSeries` integration is not in Mathlib
  under that name (define `∫` coefficientwise: `coeff (n+1) = coeff n / (n+1)`), `tsum` over a
  nonarchimedean complete field (`NonarchimedeanAddGroup.summable_of_tendsto_cofinite_zero`),
  `padicValNat_le_nat_log` / `Nat.log` for `v_p(r) ≤ log_p r`.
  GAP: invariant differential and formal logarithm of a formal group (Silverman IV.4-IV.6) absent.
- Alternative route specific to this project (recommended): define `ellLog` directly as the binomial
  series of the logarithm-series article and prove the homomorphism property from the explicit
  addition law of `y² = x³ + Ax` by the classical argument "both sides have the same derivative in
  `z₁`" in `K[[z₁, z₂]]`, which needs the formal group law only as a two-variable power series
  identity. Either route is a substantial project.

Shared design (multiplication chapter): namespace `ZInQ.Mult`, files under
`ZInQKernel/Multiplication/`. Points are `genCurve.toAffine.Point` over `K0 = QuadraticAlgebra ℚ 2 0`;
codes are `ZInQ.iota`; template relations are `ZInQ.Syntax.Rel`. Valuations on `K0` at a prime
`𝔭 : IsDedekindDomain.HeightOneSpectrum Zs` (with `Zs` the model chapter's `ℤ[√2]`) are taken as
`ℤ∪{∞}`-valued via `IsDedekindDomain.HeightOneSpectrum.valuation K0 𝔭` (multiplicative, `ℤᵐ⁰`);
a thin wrapper `ZInQ.Mult.vP 𝔭 : K0 → WithTop ℤ` is recommended. The completion is
`𝔭.adicCompletion K0`. The constants `c_*`, `ℓ_0`, `A_ht`, `T` are the syntax chapter's symbolic
`FixedExpr` values and are never evaluated (no `decide`/`norm_num` on them).
