# Implementation note: The closed form of T

- Lean name: `ZInQ.Size.Q_eq`, `ZInQ.Size.Ccm_lt_Q17`, `ZInQ.Size.T_eq`.
- Draft:
  ```lean
  theorem Q_eq (hCAT : CatalogueBound) : Syntax.Q = Syntax.D ^ (10 * Syntax.G0 ^ 2 * Syntax.D ^ 3)
  theorem Ccm_lt_Q17 (hCAT : CatalogueBound) :
      Syntax.Ccm < 2 ^ (50000 * Syntax.D ^ 2) ∧ 2 ^ (50000 * Syntax.D ^ 2) < Syntax.Q ^ 17
  theorem T_eq (hCAT : CatalogueBound) :
      Syntax.T = (96 * 10 ^ 13) ^ 13 * Syntax.D ^ (2210 * Syntax.G0 ^ 2 * Syntax.D ^ 3)
  ```
  The right side of `T_eq` is literally upstream's `ZInQ.ExplicitCutoffBounds.t` after
  `Syntax.D = ExplicitCutoffBounds.d` and `Syntax.G0 = ExplicitCutoffBounds.g0`; state a corollary
  `T_eq_upstream : Syntax.T = ExplicitCutoffBounds.t`.
- Proof: `R_eq_D`, `E0 = 10 * (D * G0)^2`, `ring_nf` on exponents (`Nat.pow_mul`, `mul_pow`);
  for `Ccm`: `D^2 < 2^(D^2)` (`Nat.lt_two_pow_self`), `3^k < 4^k = 2^(2k)`; the maximum
  `max 1 (max Ccm (Q^17)) = Q^17` by `max_eq_right` twice. `Aht = 2 * 10^13 * Q^17`,
  `(48 * Aht)^13 = (96 * 10^13)^13 * Q^221` by `mul_pow` and `ring`.
- Mathlib: `max_eq_right`, `mul_pow`, `pow_mul`, `Nat.lt_two_pow_self`, `Nat.pow_lt_pow_left`.
- Finite computation: no (closed comparisons such as `4096 * 84006 < 2^29` by `decide`).
- Upstream reuse: `ZInQ.ExplicitCutoffBounds.t`, `d`, `g0` (definitions only).
- Difficulty: low-medium (~80 lines; exponent algebra on symbolic `D`).

Shared design (size notes): namespace `ZInQ.Size`, files under `ZInQKernel/Size/`. Constants are
the syntax chapter's abbreviations `ZInQ.Syntax.Ps`, `W`, `Hcat`, `Hmax`, `Ns`, `M0`, `cstar`,
`ell`, `F`, `D`, `G0`, `E0`, `R`, `Q`, `Bcm`, `Ccm`, `Aht`, `T` (natural numbers or `toNat` of
`FixedExpr.val`; prove unfolding lemmas `T_def : T = (48 * Aht)^13` etc. by `rfl`/`simp
[FixedExpr.val]`). None of them is ever evaluated: never `decide`, `norm_num` or `rfl`-reduce a
goal containing `D`, `T`, `Hcat`, `M0`, `cstar`, `ell` or `c_3`; mark them `@[irreducible]`
after the unfolding lemmas, and work with `Nat.pow_lt_pow_left/right`, `Nat.pow_le_pow_left/right`,
`Nat.lt_two_pow_self`, `Nat.mul_lt_mul_of_lt_of_lt` as in upstream
`ZInQ.ExplicitCutoffBounds` (lean/ZInQ/ExplicitCutoffBounds.lean).
