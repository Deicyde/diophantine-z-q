# The coefficients of the truncated logarithm (logarithm-coefficients)

- Lean name: `ZInQ.Mult.log25_coefficients`.
- Decidable statement:
  ```lean
  theorem log25_coefficients :
      (List.range 7).map (fun j => (16575 : ℚ) * (-1) ^ j * (Nat.centralBinom j) / (4 * j + 1)) =
        [16575, -6630, 11050, -25500, 68250, -198900, 612612] := by norm_num [Nat.centralBinom]
  theorem sixteen575_factor : 16575 = 3 * 5 ^ 2 * 13 * 17 := by norm_num
  ```
  plus the corollary that the syntax chapter's `L` (pair polynomial of `log25`) equals
  `16575 • (truncation of formalLog at degree 25)` in `K0[z]` — by `ext n; interval_cases` on the
  26 coefficients after unfolding.
- FINITE COMPUTATION: yes. Size: seven rational identities with numbers < 10^6. Method: `norm_num`
  or `decide` on `ℚ` (kernel `Rat` arithmetic is fine at this size). No `native_decide`.
- Difficulty: easy.

Shared design (multiplication chapter): namespace `ZInQ.Mult`, files under
`ZInQKernel/Multiplication/`. Points are `genCurve.toAffine.Point` over `K0 = QuadraticAlgebra ℚ 2 0`;
codes are `ZInQ.iota`; template relations are `ZInQ.Syntax.Rel`. Valuations on `K0` at a prime
`𝔭 : IsDedekindDomain.HeightOneSpectrum Zs` (with `Zs` the model chapter's `ℤ[√2]`) are taken as
`ℤ∪{∞}`-valued via `IsDedekindDomain.HeightOneSpectrum.valuation K0 𝔭` (multiplicative, `ℤᵐ⁰`);
a thin wrapper `ZInQ.Mult.vP 𝔭 : K0 → WithTop ℤ` is recommended. The completion is
`𝔭.adicCompletion K0`. The constants `c_*`, `ℓ_0`, `A_ht`, `T` are the syntax chapter's symbolic
`FixedExpr` values and are never evaluated (no `decide`/`norm_num` on them).
