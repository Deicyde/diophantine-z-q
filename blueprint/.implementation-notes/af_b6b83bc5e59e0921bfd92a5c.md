# Implementation note: Small explicit bounds

- Lean name: `ZInQ.Size.small_constant_bounds`.
- FINITE COMPUTATION. Decidable form:
  ```lean
  theorem small_constant_bounds :
      primorial 324 < 2 ^ 433 ∧ 10 ^ 5 < 4 * primorial 324 ∧ 4 * primorial 324 < 2 ^ 435 ∧
      2 ^ 2000 < Nat.factorial 324 ∧ Nat.factorial 324 < 2 ^ 2250 ∧
      (116064000000 : ℕ) < 2 ^ 37
  ```
  Proof: rewrite `primorial 324` with the syntax chapter's `primorial_324_eq` (decimal literal),
  then `decide` on literals (GMP-backed `Nat.blt`/`Nat.ble` in the kernel). For the factorial,
  upstream proves exactly `2 ^ 2000 < factorialBase ∧ factorialBase < 2 ^ 2250` by `decide` with
  `set_option maxRecDepth 20000` and `set_option exponentiation.threshold 2250`
  (`ZInQ.ExplicitCutoffBounds.factorial_bounds`, lean/ZInQ/ExplicitCutoffBounds.lean), but it is
  `private`: copy its proof verbatim (it is a kernel `decide`, no `native_decide`).
- Size: one 433-bit product comparison, `324!` (2241 bits, 324 multiplications), three powers of
  two. Kernel cost: negligible.
- Mathlib: `primorial` (Mathlib/NumberTheory/Primorial.lean), `Nat.factorial`.
- Upstream reuse: proof pattern of `ZInQ.ExplicitCutoffBounds.factorial_bounds` (private).
- Difficulty: low.

Shared design (size notes): namespace `ZInQ.Size`, files under `ZInQKernel/Size/`. Constants are
the syntax chapter's abbreviations `ZInQ.Syntax.Ps`, `W`, `Hcat`, `Hmax`, `Ns`, `M0`, `cstar`,
`ell`, `F`, `D`, `G0`, `E0`, `R`, `Q`, `Bcm`, `Ccm`, `Aht`, `T` (natural numbers or `toNat` of
`FixedExpr.val`; prove unfolding lemmas `T_def : T = (48 * Aht)^13` etc. by `rfl`/`simp
[FixedExpr.val]`). None of them is ever evaluated: never `decide`, `norm_num` or `rfl`-reduce a
goal containing `D`, `T`, `Hcat`, `M0`, `cstar`, `ell` or `c_3`; mark them `@[irreducible]`
after the unfolding lemmas, and work with `Nat.pow_lt_pow_left/right`, `Nat.pow_le_pow_left/right`,
`Nat.lt_two_pow_self`, `Nat.mul_lt_mul_of_lt_of_lt` as in upstream
`ZInQ.ExplicitCutoffBounds` (lean/ZInQ/ExplicitCutoffBounds.lean).
