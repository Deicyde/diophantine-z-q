  (`IsSquare B.den` is exactly the form used by `ZInQ.PoleCompleteness`.)
- Mathlib: `padicValRat`, `Rat.den` and its factorization (`Nat.factorization`,
  `Rat.padicValRat_eq_neg… `: relate `padicValNat p B.den` to `-padicValRat p B` when negative);
  a square criterion via even `Nat.factorization` (no `Nat.isSquare_iff` in Mathlib, verified;
  search for the factorization criterion or prove it, ~30 lines).
  Residues: `Ideal.Quotient.mk 𝔭` for the trace-residue argument, or avoid residues by working with
  `vP 𝔭 (W g⁻¹ - 1) ≥ 1` vs `vP 𝔭 (W g⁻¹) ≥ 1` and the sum `Σ = m + (element of valuation ≥ 1)`
  with `m ∈ {1..12}` a natural number prime to `p`.
- FINITE COMPUTATION: no. Difficulty: hard-ish (~400 lines; the largest completeness leaf).

Shared design (multiplication chapter): namespace `ZInQ.Mult`, files under
`ZInQKernel/Multiplication/`. Points are `genCurve.toAffine.Point` over `K0 = QuadraticAlgebra ℚ 2 0`;
codes are `ZInQ.iota`; template relations are `ZInQ.Syntax.Rel`. Valuations on `K0` at a prime
`𝔭 : IsDedekindDomain.HeightOneSpectrum Zs` (with `Zs` the model chapter's `ℤ[√2]`) are taken as
`ℤ∪{∞}`-valued via `IsDedekindDomain.HeightOneSpectrum.valuation K0 𝔭` (multiplicative, `ℤᵐ⁰`);
a thin wrapper `ZInQ.Mult.vP 𝔭 : K0 → WithTop ℤ` is recommended. The completion is
`𝔭.adicCompletion K0`. The constants `c_*`, `ℓ_0`, `A_ht`, `T` are the syntax chapter's symbolic
`FixedExpr` values and are never evaluated (no `decide`/`norm_num` on them).
