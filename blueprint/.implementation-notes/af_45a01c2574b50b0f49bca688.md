# Implementation note: Bounds for D

- Lean name: `ZInQ.Size.D_bounds`.
- Draft:
  ```lean
  theorem D_bounds : 2 ^ 2 ^ 8008 < Syntax.D ∧ Syntax.D < 2 ^ 2 ^ 9010
  ```
- Upstream reuse: `ZInQ.ExplicitCutoffBounds.d` has the same formula (`factorialBase * 3 ^ (256 *
  factorialBase ^ 4)`); prove `Syntax.D = ExplicitCutoffBounds.d` by `rfl`/`simp [FixedExpr.val]`.
  The upper bound `d_upper : D < 2 ^ (2 ^ (a * 4 + 10))` with `a = 2250` appears inside the
  private theorem `arithmetic_bound` of lean/ZInQ/ExplicitCutoffBounds.lean; it is not exported,
  so copy that block (about 40 lines) into a public lemma. The lower bound is new:
  `256 * F^4 > 2^8 * (2^2000)^4 = 2^8008` and `3^k > 2^k` (`Nat.pow_lt_pow_left (by decide : 2 < 3)`).
- Mathlib: `Nat.pow_lt_pow_left`, `Nat.pow_lt_pow_right`, `Nat.le_mul_of_pos_right`.
- Finite computation: no (uses `small_constant_bounds`); `D` itself is never evaluated.
- Difficulty: low.

Shared design (size notes): namespace `ZInQ.Size`, files under `ZInQKernel/Size/`. Constants are
the syntax chapter's abbreviations `ZInQ.Syntax.Ps`, `W`, `Hcat`, `Hmax`, `Ns`, `M0`, `cstar`,
`ell`, `F`, `D`, `G0`, `E0`, `R`, `Q`, `Bcm`, `Ccm`, `Aht`, `T` (natural numbers or `toNat` of
`FixedExpr.val`; prove unfolding lemmas `T_def : T = (48 * Aht)^13` etc. by `rfl`/`simp
[FixedExpr.val]`). None of them is ever evaluated: never `decide`, `norm_num` or `rfl`-reduce a
goal containing `D`, `T`, `Hcat`, `M0`, `cstar`, `ell` or `c_3`; mark them `@[irreducible]`
after the unfolding lemmas, and work with `Nat.pow_lt_pow_left/right`, `Nat.pow_le_pow_left/right`,
`Nat.lt_two_pow_self`, `Nat.mul_lt_mul_of_lt_of_lt` as in upstream
`ZInQ.ExplicitCutoffBounds` (lean/ZInQ/ExplicitCutoffBounds.lean).
