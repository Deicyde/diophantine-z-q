This additive edition connects previously reviewed supporting results to the original abelian points and Tate sequences. It does **not** prove the unrestricted wild-inertia lemma or the full Z-in-Q statement.

## New bridges

- **Original-point Frobenius multiplier.** Actual special-point reduction preserves multiplication and powers because it is the map/pullback adjunction transport. Torsion-reduction uniqueness reflects the derived torus multiplier to original integral ℓ-power torsion points whose reduction lies in the torus. The common period is derived; the torus-membership condition remains essential.
- **Original inertia/residue correspondence.** Integral torsion of invertible order is inertia-fixed. Conversely, Néron inertia descent supplies an integral section for every original inertia-fixed torsion point. Separatedness gives injectivity. The resulting group equivalence composes with the proved residue-torsion restriction equivalence. No lifting, injectivity or group-equivalence conclusion is supplied as a hypothesis, and no blanket geometric-torsion integrality is asserted.
- **Finite components at every prime.** A finite group's Tate module is zero, and finite-quotient exactness gives the original Tate-map bijection and coordinate-image identities. The actual open-subgroup wrapper derives finite index and commutativity. Compatible roots in the open subgroup are still required to identify its coordinates with all finite-level torsion. The finite-index equivalence overlaps an earlier independent source result and is not counted as a new global obligation discharged.

A uniform finite container for finite-level right kernels implies that both Tate-pairing kernels vanish. On original abelian points, torsion finiteness, roots of unity and lifts are discharged. The actual level pairing, finite container and right-kernel containment remain inputs; no finite-level perfectness or coprimality with the container size is assumed.
