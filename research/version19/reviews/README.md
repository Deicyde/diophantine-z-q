These original reports are retained unchanged; `ORIGINALS.json` records their
byte counts and hashes. They report local component checks, not protected
acceptance of Version 19. The component reports' source hashes and line references
refer to the drafts they name, before the final exposition and abstract edits.

- `fable-construction-audit.md` records Fable's checks of the upstream
  constructions, including Sections 9–14 used for the explicit 250 result.
- `fable-sections34.md` checks the effective-sum and reflected-height arguments
  as typeset and gives the short explanations requested for their exposition.
- `fable-tables.md` checks the printed 195 and 250 tables and the 250 maps.
- `astra-195-component.md` checks the printed 195 transformation, matrix,
  weights, exceptional stratum and coefficient height for every integer
  `N >= 1`. It does not re-audit the inherited arithmetic theorem.
- `astra-195-weight-supplement.md` recounts the printed nonzero weights on the
  final source: 186 Laurent weights and 64 C-balancing weights. It preserves the
  original component report's scope.
- `v19-builder-review.json` is Fable's original review of the final source/PDF
  correspondence, build selection, preservation of Version 16–18, and the seven
  exposition edits. Its exact bindings and limitations are in the record.

The seven final exposition edits define the formal subgroup and five-contact
radical, spell out monotonicity and both reflected-domain/radical arguments,
and distinguish coordinate constants from exponent weights. The abstract's
JSON-size sentence has been replaced by the owner's exact plain-expression

The local reports' stated limitations remain limitations of those reports.
The component reports do not assert that the final PDF was read; the separate
builder review records its own PDF checks. No local reviewer record is acceptance
of the assembled release. The actual gate decisions on the
final revision, including any remaining objections or scope limitations,
belong to the release's protected review and publication record.
