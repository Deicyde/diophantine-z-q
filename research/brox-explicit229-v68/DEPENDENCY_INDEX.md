## Table-level source-to-consumer checks

`check_graft.py` authenticates both source tables, all unchanged rows, copied coordinates and detector rows, all twelve contact equations, both sum components, and all three literal unit guards. `check_source_bridge.py` separately authenticates the source 229 table digest `f0e6fc3f62525d7b251a10fc5f4cf0e6663c3f6f08beeef3cb28092b2e1555a8`; checks that contact helper coordinates 74–76, 87–89 and 201–203 have no consumer outside their four contact rows; reconstructs six secant field-component rows (111,112,127,128,143,144), with exactly the C-scalings on the third and fourth pairs; checks the two inverse-Vieta scalar product rows (155,156) with the signs and factor 2 for rho²=2; and checks the two subsequent r rows (157,158). It binds `x192` and `x193` to the real and imaginary parts of the same physical product. These are **finite algebra** checks, not an independent proof of the analytic or pole-realization theorems.

```sh
cd research/brox-explicit229-v68
```
