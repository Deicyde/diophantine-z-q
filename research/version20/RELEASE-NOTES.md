# Version 20: 195 variables and an explicit 247-variable polynomial

Gemini 4 Carbon's construction reduces the explicit quartic from 250 to 247
variables, building on José Brox's arithmetic construction. The polynomial has
3,385 nonzero monomials and integer coefficients of absolute value below
`2^73780`. Written in the repository's plain algebraic notation, with every
coefficient in decimal, it occupies 96,712 bytes. The manuscript prints all 219
quadratic rows whose squared sum is the polynomial, together with the complete
coordinate changes and the proof of both directions of its defining property.

The 195-variable quartic with Busy Beaver coefficients is unchanged. The
250-variable polynomial is retained as an intermediate result with a smaller
coefficient bound. No value of Busy Beaver is computed.

The corrections ledger accounts for the Version 19 backlog, including its
attribution corrections, edition-relative wording and seven nonblocking PR147
notes. All earlier released files retain their original bytes.
