# Compatible arithmetic labels

## Frobenius match

`frobeniusMatchesLabel_of_inert` proves the existing `FrobeniusMatchesLabel` proposition for a quadratic number field from the rational arithmetic Frobenius elements, the incidence of the stage primes, and their residue cardinalities. No conjugacy witness or Frobenius comparison is assumed.

The residue-cardinality hypothesis implies that the stage prime is generated by the rational prime. Hence the prime witnessing the rational Frobenius contracts to that stage prime. Iterating the rational residue action gives the relative square Frobenius. The decomposition embedding gives an arithmetic Frobenius at its own prime of the algebraic integers. Profinite prime transitivity supplies a conjugator fixing the number field. Unramified uniqueness on the finite subextensions of the maximal extension unramified outside the support identifies the restrictions to the global Galois group.

## Character condition

`exists_characterField` produces a finite normal rational extension containing the number field and detecting the character modulo the coefficient-stage ideal. Its proof uses continuity of the character and a finite fixing subgroup in the Krull topology, followed by a rational normal closure.

`selectedDerivativePrimes` applies the existing finite Chebotarev producer to the compositum of the original stage field and this character field. If the kernel representative fixes the character field, the selected Frobenius restricts to the actual conjugation there. Its square therefore fixes the character field and the number field. `selectedDerivativePrimes_character` proves the character condition for the resulting square Frobenius.

`exists_derivativePrimes_with_character` is the character-aware extension of the derivative-prime producer. `exists_derivativePrimes_with_character_one` has no representative-compatibility hypothesis: identity representatives work for every continuous character.

## Construction

`derivativeKernel` is the genuine subgroup of the rational absolute Galois group fixing both the required Tate torsion field and the finite character field. Identity elements belong to this subgroup; other elements may act nontrivially on the original stage fields.

`compatibleArithmeticLabelFromKernel` takes actual elements of these finite-stage kernels and actual stage primes outside the support, above the selected rational primes and of the required residue cardinality. It constructs the existing `CompatibleArithmeticLabel`, deriving the fixing of the number field, the Frobenius match, and the character condition. Its inputs contain neither of the last two label fields.

`compatibleArithmeticLabelFromSelection` is the corresponding constructor with explicit field-fixing proofs. `compatibleArithmeticLabel_one` specializes it to identity representatives and requires no character-compatibility proof. The existing consequences of a compatible label, including its twisted derivative congruence and realized derivative matrices, apply to these constructions.

## Scope and remaining arithmetic data

The character must be continuous. Arbitrary preselected derivative primes and representatives are not asserted to satisfy the character condition. Prescribed representatives must lie in the enlarged finite-stage kernel, or the selection must be made with compatible representatives. Identity representatives prove existence of the character-aware selection but are not claimed to detect any prescribed cohomology classes.

The quadratic degree, the stage primes outside the support, their incidence above the selected rational primes, and their inert residue cardinalities remain genuine arithmetic inputs. The constructors do not prove existence of that stage-prime family or choose representatives satisfying additional detection conditions.

No assumption equivalent to the Frobenius match or the character condition occurs in the kernel-element or identity-representative constructors. The finite character fields are produced from continuity, rather than assumed as unproved factorization data.
