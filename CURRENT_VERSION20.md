# Version 20

The current manuscript is [Version 20](manuscripts/quartic_v20.pdf), with its
[TeX source](manuscripts/quartic_v20.tex). It contains the unchanged 195-variable
quartic with Busy Beaver coefficients and Gemini 4 Carbon's explicit
247-variable quartic, built on José Brox's construction.

The explicit polynomial has 3,385 monomials, coefficients below `2^73780`, and
a 96,712-byte plain algebraic expression. See the
[release guide](research/version20/README.md),
[corrections ledger](research/version20/CORRECTIONS.md), and
[source and consumer map](research/version20/DEPENDENCIES.md).
