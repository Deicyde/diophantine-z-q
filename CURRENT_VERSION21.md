# Version 21

The manuscript is [Version 21](manuscripts/quartic_v21.pdf), with its
[TeX source](manuscripts/quartic_v21.tex). It adopts José Brox's explicit
229-variable quartic while preserving the 195-variable polynomial with
Busy Beaver coefficients. It retains the 247 and 250 as intermediate results.

The explicit polynomial has 3,312 monomials, largest absolute coefficient
149^30 < 2^217, and a 74,007-byte plain decimal expression. See the
[preparation guide](research/version21/README.md),
[corrections ledger](research/version21/CORRECTIONS.md) and
[source and consumer map](research/version21/DEPENDENCIES.md).

Version20 remains the published release until the exact Version21 head has
passed the required review and received release approval.
